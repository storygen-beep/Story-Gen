## 1. Who she is

**June, 24.** Two years ago she left for a city job and a man, and everyone at home heard
about it. Both are gone now — the job in a restructure, the man about a month after. She came
back on a coach with two bags because there was nowhere else to be.

Her room is half a storage room now. Her father's weight bench is where the desk was. There is
one bathroom in the house and her bedroom door has no lock — the catch broke years ago and
nobody fixed it because nobody needed to.

Her mother left two years ago, around the same time June did. Nobody talks about either.

**What she has to lose:** being the one who got out. It is the only status she has in this
house, it is already fiction, and every favour she asks for spends some of it.

## 2. The appetite that never fills

**To be wanted — not looked after, not tolerated, not managed — by the people who have only
ever seen her as the kid.**

It cannot complete. There is no state where she has been wanted *enough*, no villain to
defeat, no door at the end. Each person can go further; each room can turn.

**What does release 41 add?** Another rung on someone who is already in the house, another
routine turned, or a new body in the box room. The house is a machine for generating them.

## 3. What she is becoming — as ACCESS

**Bottom.** She knocks. She waits for the bathroom. She wears the robe over the towel. She asks
before she takes anything, and she is careful in the rooms that were never hers.

**Top.** She does not knock, and neither do they. The bathroom door stays open because there
is no longer a reason to close it. The garage, the box room, her father's room — places she had
no business in — are simply places she goes. **The house rearranges itself around what she
wants, and nobody in it says the word for what has happened.**

### The ascent tiers

| tier | what going further means | 15 | 35 | 55 | 75 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **`nerve`** | how far she'll go *with family* — the taboo itself | stops covering up around them | lets contact happen and doesn't move away | initiates, undeniably | it's routine, and the house runs on it |
| **`exposure`** | how much she lets herself be seen | robe loose, door ajar | stops bothering with the towel | naked in shared rooms | does it *where* she can be walked in on, on purpose |
| **`need`** | what she'll trade, and how openly | asks for money | takes work she'd have refused | trades directly | the trade is simply the arrangement |

**Counterweight: `pride`** — starts at 100. Every ask, every accepted favour, every time she
lets something pass spends it. It buys nothing; it is what she is paying with.

## 4. The charge

**Reversal**, primarily. She was the one who made it out — the one held up at every family
thing. Now she is broke in a box room in her father's house, and the men who were beneath her
in that story are the ones with something she needs. The taboo is the second layer; **the
first is that they get to watch her come down.**

| **Ray** *(stepfather, 52)* | He was the proud one. His approval is the thing she came back without, and he is careful — every inch with him is expensive and deniable, which makes an inch feel like a mile. He has been alone in this house for two years. |

## 6. Register

- **`narration_person` = `second`.** Declared once, immutable after v0.1 ships.
- **Where the crude register lives: the standing surfaces.** The bathroom, the kitchen at
  night, the front room after eleven, her own room with the door not shut. The one-time scenes
  can be careful; **the content she returns to fifty times is the crude content.** This is the
  single correction the whole system exists for.
