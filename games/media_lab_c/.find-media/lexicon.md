# Lexicon — media_lab_c (arm-A replication, 2026-07-28)

Terms confirmed against actual footage this run. SEARCH did not run in this arm (pre-stocked
shelf), so nothing here comes from a live word hunt — these are **slug-vs-strip verdicts**,
which is the other half of vocabulary work and the half that stops a term being trusted twice.

## Slugs that told the truth

| term | slot | what the strip actually showed |
|---|---|---|
| `back alley` / `behind the club` | alley | night, brick wall, outdoors — the beat, exactly |
| `downblouse` | tease | genuine lean-forward cleavage; but the genre is usually shot **headless**, and a beat needing her *aware of the viewer* cannot be proven without a face |
| `titty drop` | flash | reveal **and** re-cover inside one loop — this is the term for a flash, where `reveal` alone gets you a strip |
| `bent over a table` | behind | face-down, upper body down, position holds |
| `blowbang` | group | 3+ men simultaneously; better yield than `gangbang`, which returns two-man clips |

## Slugs that lied — verified by frame strip

These are the ones worth carrying forward. Every one is a term that reads correct and ships wrong.

| slug | promised | actually was |
|---|---|---|
| `dogging.gif` | outdoor public sex (that is what the word means) | a bed with blankets, indoors |
| `voyeur.gif` | outdoor voyeur scene | interior, seen through a doorway — the **camera** is outside, the scene is not |
| `eye-contact.gif` (asianporngif) | eye contact | pure side profile, eyes down 4/4 |
| `bent-over-the-table.gif` | bent over a table | on all fours, arms straight, head **up** |
| `creampie-dripping-as-she-jerks-cock` | creampie + aftermath | external cumshot landing on the outside |
| `hot-blonde-fucked-gently-on-the-bed` | tender finish | penetrative sex, no facial at all |
| `sharing-a-woman-among-three-men` | a group scene | true, but it is a **3-panel vertical collage**, not a scene |

**Rule this produces:** a slug naming a *setting* or a *count* is roughly trustworthy; a slug
naming an **act, a gaze, or a body position** is not. Those three are precisely what the
strip exists to check.

## Shelf pollution to expect (all four media_lab arms share this pool)

Not vocabulary, but it costs judging time and it is in the shared shelf:

- `shared.fastly.steamstatic.com/store_item_assets/...` — **Steam store assets**. A derelict
  street render with no people, harvested onto the alley slot.
- `gifdb.com` dry-hump comedy gifs and `#AYTO` MTV reality clips on the passive slot — clothed,
  mainstream, not porn.
- `media.tenor.com` returns **celebrity** cleavage gifs for tease queries (Lindsay Lohan,
  Shraddha Kapoor, Carmen Electra). Usable as placeholders; be aware of what they are.
- Stock-photo hosts on SFW slots (`dreamstime`, `gettyimages`, `shutterstock`, `alamy`,
  `istock`) serve previews with **watermarks across the subject** — a Gate-1 fail, not a craft
  deduction. 3 of 8 room candidates died on this alone. `freerangestock` and `stockcake` are
  the clean ones in this pool.
