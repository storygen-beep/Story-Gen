## Beat 4, written in full

**An explicit beat is the one thing on a sheet that cannot be reviewed from a label.** The pivot
rule is a reading test — no summary shows whether a beat stays on the body — so every explicit beat
in this release is written out where it sits, whether or not the scene is the voice sample.

> Up on your toes the shirt goes with you and your ass is at the height of the desk he sits at. Your
> tits pull up under the cotton, nipples through it. He is hard in his lap and writing nothing.

`41 words · ass · tits · nipples — 3 counted words · Del tier 1`

⚠️ **This beat was rewritten before it shipped, and the reason is worth keeping.** The first version
ended *"...Your tits pull up under the cotton. He is hard in his lap..."* and I recorded it as
carrying three frozen-list words: tits, ass, hard. **It carried two.** `hard` is not on the gate's
list (`gates.py:286`) and neither is `wet` — that list is anatomy and acts, not states. Run against
the real regex the beat scored **2** and would have failed the floor while the sheet said it passed.
`nipples` replaced the word that was doing nothing.

⚠️ **And that exposes a problem with [decision 15](../../DECISIONS.md).** Del's tier-1 ceiling is
written as *tits, ass, hard* — and **only two of those three count**, so a Del tier-1 beat cannot
reach the 3-word floor without repeating a word or reaching outside its stated ceiling. `nipples` is
the same register as `tits` and no escalation toward tier 2, so it is the honest reading; but the
ceiling row needs fixing rather than re-interpreting once per beat.

⚠️ **This is now the first explicit beat in the game**, at corruption 12, on about night two.
It used to be Marek's, at 20, on about night three. **That is a design consequence of where the
beat went, and it is reversible in one move** — put it on
[`del_30`](del_30_behind_you_at_the_monitor.md) instead and the first heat goes back to the
bathroom. `[chg] 2026-08-31, LO's call to add three explicit beats`

⚠️ **She is still fully dressed and nothing is touched.** The beat is her shape under her own
clothes and his reaction to it, which is what tier 1 is for. Nothing here reaches Del's tier 2.

⚠️ **First rung that raises `exhibitionism`, and it raises it by one.** The climb starts before
anything is off, which is the whole reason the meter is called what it is.
