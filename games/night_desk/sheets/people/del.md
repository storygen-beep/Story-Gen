## His mood — full week, or empty week

Set by occupancy over the last seven days. **Not a third meter** — it is a read of the economy that
already exists.

| | |
|---|---|
| **full** | expansive. Buys the coffee himself. Talks. Doesn't watch the monitor much |
| **empty** | at the monitor at 4am, short with her, counting. Watches everything |

**The empty week is when he is reachable and unpleasant at once**, and that is the point of having
the axis at all. Rungs 12, 20 and 30 all read better on an empty week — he is already watching.

⚠️ **Every scene that can fire in both moods is written twice.** Rungs 2 and 6 can fire in both.
Rung 30 effectively only fires on an empty week, so it needs one version, not two.

## What he asks for before she will do it

**This is where his corruption outruns hers**, and it is content the yes-path never reaches.

| his corruption | what he asks | at her corruption below |
|---|---|---|
| 25 | to leave the office door open while she changes | 30 → **she says no**, and he does not ask twice that week |
| 50 | to work a shift without the cardigan | 45 → **no**, and it costs 5 relation |
| 70 | to let him watch the bathroom camera | 78 → **no**, and this one she thinks about |

**The refusals are written at full length.** The field puts a real one on about one click in fifty
and **79% of them go somewhere the yes does not.** We have never written one.

## What I want your read on

**Del's relation gate rises faster than his corruption ladder does**, which means the bottleneck on
him is always *whether he trusts her*, never whether she is willing. He is the slow one by design —
he has the building, the money and forty years on her, and all three make him careful.

The risk is that he reads as **passive** rather than careful. A man who never initiates is not
restrained, he is furniture.

The fix, if you want it: **his corruption drives the asks in the table above, and those arrive
whether or not his relation has moved.** So he *is* pushing — just at things she will not do yet.
That is already how I have written it, and it is the thing I would most like you to check, because
if it does not land he is the dull half of a two-man cast.
