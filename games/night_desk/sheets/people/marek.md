# [REVIEW] Marek

## In short

- **Thirties. Room 6, eleven weeks, cash on Fridays.** He has unpacked, and that is the thing about
  him.
- **Same nine corruption rungs as Del, but his relation gates are far lower.** He has no power over
  her, nothing to protect, and no reason to be careful — so in practice **he is reachable much
  earlier**, on the same ladder.
- **He shares the bathroom.** Room 6 is the cheap half. So the two of them wash in the same room
  from day one, before anything else has happened.
- **Four rungs authored** this release (2 → 20).
- **His mood: going, or staying.** Decided every Friday.

**Compare the relation column with Del's.** Del needs 85 at the top; Marek needs 50. Same corruption
ladder, half the trust. **That is the two-meter system doing its job** — the men are different
people without needing different mechanics.

## His mood — going, or staying

Decided every Friday, when he pays.

| | |
|---|---|
| **going** | honest and reckless. Says things out loud. Nothing to lose because he is gone Monday |
| **staying** | careful, evasive, slightly ashamed of having stayed again |

**He has said he is leaving eleven times.** The mood is not a coin flip — it drifts toward *staying*
as her relation climbs, which is the quiet version of the arc: **she is the reason he keeps not
going.**

⚠️ That drift is the one place his mood is not free. Everything else about him is.

## The bathroom, and why he is the fast one

The Wayside is old. Rooms 1–6 share the bathroom in the back hall behind the desk, and staff use
it.
**Marek is in room 6.**

⚠️ **The bathroom is behind the desk, not down the corridor** — corrected 2026-08-31, because the map
puts it off the desk and the prose had it off the corridor. Behind the desk is the better version
anyway: the shared bathroom is a few steps from where she stands all night, so she and everyone in
the cheap half keep crossing at it.

So before a single rung is taken, they are already washing in the same room, at hours that overlap
because he keeps her hours. That is why his ladder starts at relation 0 and Del's does not — **the
proximity is free and it is structural, not something she has to earn.**

**Rung 20 is the first one that is really about it**, and it is the game's first genuine exposure
beat: she does not cover up. Nothing is done to her, nothing is asked. She just does not turn away.

## What he asks for before she will do it

He asks earlier and more plainly than Del, because he has nothing to protect.

| his corruption | what he asks | at her corruption below |
|---|---|---|
| 15 | whether she is going to keep pretending the bathroom is a coincidence | 20 → **she says no**, and he laughs and drops it |
| 35 | to leave the door unlocked | 45 → **no**, and it costs nothing. He expected it |
| 55 | to come to room 6 after her shift | 60 → **no**, and she thinks about it for two nights |

**He does not sulk when refused.** That is the difference between the two men and it should be legible
in one line of every refusal.

## What this sheet turned up

**Marek reaches a real exposure beat at corruption 20 while Del is still at "let him watch you reach
for a shelf."** That is a big gap this early, and it is not an accident of my numbers — it is what
the design says: proximity is free, trust is not.

**But it means the first time she is naked in front of anybody is Marek's, in the bathroom, at
corruption 20.** Worth you knowing that, because it sets what the game is on night three or four —
long before Del is anywhere.

⚠️ **Updated 2026-08-31.** The game's first *explicit beat* is now Del's, at corruption 12
([`del_12`](../scenes/del_12_reach_for_the_top_shelf.md)) — she is fully dressed in it and it is
tier 1. Marek still owns the first scene where she is not dressed. The two men now open at 12 and
20 rather than 20 and 30, which narrows the early gap between them without touching either gate.

If that is too fast, the lever is **rung 20's corruption cost, not his relation gate.** Moving the
relation gate would break the thing that makes him different.
