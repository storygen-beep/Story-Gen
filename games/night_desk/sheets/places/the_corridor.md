# [REVIEW] The Corridor

## In short

- Twelve doors, six a side. **Room 6 is Marek's.** The ice machine hums and makes nothing.
- **The route past everything**, and the reason the property walk is a job duty and not a hobby.
- **Four things to do**, one of which is the laundry.
- The corridor is where corruption is cheapest early: she is *supposed* to be walking down it.

## The one thing worth saying

**`Listen at a door` is the corruption on-ramp that is not the monitor**, and it needed to exist —
one source per tier is how the early game becomes a grind. The monitor is safe and passive; this one
requires her to stop walking, which is a slightly bigger step and reads as one.
