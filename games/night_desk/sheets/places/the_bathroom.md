# [REVIEW] The Bathroom

## In short

- **Shared, and not split by sex.** Back hall behind the desk. Rooms 1–6 use it and so does staff.
- **The exposure surface of the whole game.** Not because a rung says so — because `hygiene` forces
  her in and somebody else is often already there.
- **Three things to do**, which is the field median. It does not need more; what makes it work is
  *who is in it*, not how many buttons it has.
- Occupancy is the mechanic. It is on the sheet as a table because it matters more than the choices.

## Occupancy — the thing that makes this room work

| hour | who | how often |
|---|---|---|
| 00:00-01:00 | Marek | most nights |
| 02:00-05:00 | nobody | almost always |
| 05:30-06:30 | Marek | most mornings |
| 06:00-07:00 | Del | most mornings |
| any | a guest from 1-6 | 1 in 5 |

**She controls when she washes. That is the whole decision.** Wash at 3am and it is private and
free. Wash at 06:00 and she is choosing to be in there with somebody.

⚠️ **`hygiene` is what stops that being optional.** She cannot bank it forever — under the band
every `exhibitionism` rung in the game is shut, so she has to wash, and the only question is when.
**A need that forces a choice about risk is worth more than three that empty bars.**

## The walk-in

Our rule: a room where she is alone with someone scheduled carries a walk-in. This is the strongest
case in the game -- **Del holds keys to every door in the building**, and the bathroom does not lock
from the inside because the bolt has been broken since before she arrived.

Not authored this release. Named here so it is not forgotten.
