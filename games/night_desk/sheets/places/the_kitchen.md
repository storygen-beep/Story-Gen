# [REVIEW] The Kitchen

## In short

- Staff kitchen behind the desk. A table with something on it, a fridge, a kettle.
- **Where hunger is fixed, and the fix is two speeds** -- fast and bad, or slow and real.
- **Del uses it too**, which makes it a place they collide at 3am rather than a vending machine.
- **Three things to do.**

## The two speeds are the mechanic

**Eating fast costs ten minutes and half fixes it. Eating properly costs thirty-five and fixes it.**

Thirty-five minutes is a car she did not check in -- six dollars, sometimes twelve. So hunger is
priced in money, exactly as the design says, and the player makes that trade three or four times a
night without the game ever refusing her anything.

## Del at 3am

**Only on an empty week.** He comes in with the ledger when the numbers are bad and sits with a mug
he does not drink.

That is the mood axis doing real work: the kitchen has a person in it *because* the motel is losing
money, and the motel losing money is also why her pay is short. **One condition, two consequences,
and they point the same way.**
