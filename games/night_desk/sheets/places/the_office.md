## The recorder

**The desk monitor is live; the office recorder is the archive.** The monitor shows what is
happening now, which is a matter of luck. The recorder shows what already happened, which is a
matter of choosing to look.

That is a real escalation and it costs nothing to build -- same mechanism, higher gate, and the
difference is entirely that one is passive and the other is deliberate.

⚠️ **It sits behind a door that is shut two nights in three**, which is fine here and was not fine
for the monitor. The recorder is a corruption-30 rung, not an on-ramp; waiting for the door is part
of it.
