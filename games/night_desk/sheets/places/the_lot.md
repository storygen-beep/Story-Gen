## Why the lot earns its place

**It is where the exhibitionism ladder works without a man in the room.** Everything else she can be
seen doing involves Del, Marek or a guest at the counter. Out here the audience is the highway, and
that makes it the cheapest place to take a rung she is not ready to take in front of somebody who
knows her name.

⚠️ **`Fix the sign` opens nothing this release and it is on the sheet saying so.** It is a one-time
job duty with no consequence yet -- the kind of thing that quietly becomes filler if nobody writes
down that it is owed something.
