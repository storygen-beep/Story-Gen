## Why it is in 0.0.1 at all

**Because the locked door is content.** Our own rule is that the release ends on a visible locked
door, and this is the clearest one in the game: a room she has a key to and will not go into.

The line on the door does the work -- *"a key to every door on this hall and no reason to use that
one"* -- and it says exactly what is missing without naming a number.

⚠️ **The photograph is a promise.** It is face-down, it is on the sheet, and it opens nothing. That
is fine for one release and a defect by the third. Logged in `iterations/001` as owed.
