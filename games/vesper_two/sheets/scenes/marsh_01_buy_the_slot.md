## ⚠️ A4b's third key, and the game's only instance

The field's arc keys come in three kinds: a meter the scene feeds · a skill practised elsewhere ·
**a preparation bought and endured.** This is the third.

> **Money buys the key to a rung, not a stat.** `the-economy.md` R1b arriving from the arc side —
> a shop that sells an arc's prerequisite does more work than a shop that sells a meter point.

Rue was cut from the cast, so **the coin is the obstacle now**: the slot belongs to somebody and it
has to be bought off her. `the-surfaces.md` R4 — a sink belongs where the thing being bought lives.
