## The DOOR

`the-map.md` R6: a door belongs to a **person**, not a room, and it is rare — DoL carries **six** in
a 15,626-passage game. Presence is not the test; 63% of our own rooms hold a scheduled person and a
door on all of them would be a knocking simulator.

**This one is the room.** He strips her at the door every visit, because he took her drain in the
cell and knows where she carries it. The threshold *is* the content, and it is the only place in the
game where `seated` is read against her before she has said anything.

⚠️ **The door always renders; what is conditional is whether the door EXISTS.** Do not build a rule
that skips the threshold when it has nothing to say — `become-someone` ships 54 door screens, 50
gating on occupancy, and skips none.
