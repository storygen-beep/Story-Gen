This release pays the first two and turns the third into the new pressure.

## Part one — THE FITTING

Kess takes three parts out of Vega's wreck and fits them into Wren, on his table at the Berth, over **three
paid sessions**, one part a session:

1. **Strength.** She can lift what two men could not.
2. **Armour** under the skin. Cain tries his knife on her, the way he tried it on Vega's shell on the first study
   night. It barely marks her.
3. **The arm blade.** It comes out of her forearm **whenever she wants**. No charge, no condition. It is hers.

So she now carries two weapons that do two different jobs: **the drain** controls a man and still runs on sex, the
way it always has; **the blade** kills.

## Part two — SHE FEELS IT

Vega felt no pain, and that was the reason her body kept going until it broke. **Wren keeps her pain.** When
Cain's knife meets the armour it barely marks her, and she still flinches. Kess sees it and says it plainly: the
company can build the strength and the armour and the blade a line at a time, and it cannot build the part of her
that hurts. That is the soul the Chairman wants, and the thing nobody but her has.

This is what keeps her herself, and it is the cost she carries into every fight.

## Part three — CAIN'S NIGHTS

The repeatable loop the cradles lines promised. At the Site, at night, with the man she chose knowing everything.

The ladder:
1. his mouth on her
2. her on top
3. a second round
4. **the night she asks him for it out loud**, for herself — the first time in the game she wants sex as something
   of her own, not something used or spent

Cain's one trait here: he waited years and asks her for nothing. So every rung is her reaching, never him taking.

**Held out: anal.** An anal finish fires her weapon and she would drain him (Cain's signed row, 2026-10-12, in
`design_the_transfer.md`). That row covers only the two 0.2.4 scenes; **this loop needs its own ceiling row**,
drafted at the blueprint for LO to sign.

## Part four — THE HUNT

**Lyra and Nova come for her, always together.** Never one alone.

It works the way Vega's hunt worked in 0.2.3 (`rand_vega_street`, `5_scenes.toml:22594`): they can step out of
the crowd when she walks onto the Underground strip, at most once a day, and when they do there is nothing to
choose. **She runs.** It costs her damage and the rest of the day, and she wakes at her cot.

Why they do not finish her: Wren is the one the units were told to spare (`design_the_transfer.md` part seven).
That order was never given about Vega, which is why they tore Vega apart; it still holds for Wren.

What is different from Vega's hunt:
- **The new body shows.** With the armour and the strength she lasts longer before she has to run, and the prose
  says so. It is still a loss.
- **She feels every hit.** They feel nothing.
- **No habits.** Losing teaches her nothing she can count this release.
- **No last fight, and no "Draw them out" card.** Wren cannot beat the two of them together, and this release does
  not pretend she can or show her a locked door to it.

## Part five — THE ENDING

The **first loss that leaves her Badly hurt** (the Body line at 60 or more) ends the release.

She comes round at the Site and Cain is there. He tells her what Lyra and Nova are, and that more are coming off
the line at the Rise — the empty bodies she saw as Vega, many at a time. She is stronger than she has ever been,
and two of them were enough.

**The end card:** she has the blade, the strength and the armour, and she still hurts. Two units hunt her, and the
company is building more.

| **Fit** | She pays, Kess puts the part in. It is in, and it does not work yet. | 40 / 60 / 80 coin | `parts_out` +1 |
| **Settle** | The next day her body pushes back. It hurts and will not answer her; he adjusts it. Her pain lives here. | free | `parts_settled` +1 |
| **Proof** | The day after, he tests it and it works. The part counts as done. | free | `parts_fitted` +1 |

- Strength: settle, her back locks and he re-seats a cable; proof, she lifts the pump housing.
- Armour: settle, the sheets burn under her skin and he shaves an edge; proof, he hits her full force with a bar
  and it rings off her. Kess's pain line ("She never did that.") stays on the armour fit.
- Blade: settle, it jams half out and he re-wires it; proof, out and back at will, a clean cut.
- **`parts_fitted` now counts parts PROVEN**, so card T, the hunt, Cain's "Show me." and the knife test (gte 2)
  all wait for the proof. The knife test now comes after the armour has been proven.
- **New keys:** two more odometers, `parts_out` (+1 on each fit; the wreck reads it) and `parts_settled` (+1 on
  each settle), and the flag `fitting_day`, set on every fitting visit. The three counters together fix the exact
  visit with AND-only gates (fit n: out n + settled n; settle n: out n+1 + settled n; proof n: settled n+1 +
  fitted n). Every visit after the very first gates on `days_since_flag fitting_day gte 1`. On the same day Kess
  sends her away and only "Not now." shows.
- The three images move with their payoff beats onto the proof nodes. The settle nodes carry no media.
- **Dev-only gap:** a portal save already part-way through the old fitting (`parts_fitted` 1 or 2, `fitting_day`
  never set) finds the day-gated visits closed, because `days_since_flag` fails closed on an unset flag. 0.2.5 is
  unreleased; the "0.2.5: start" dev jump resets it.
- Kess's card choice into the fitting is renamed **"Get on his bench."** (was "Have him fit the next part."), so it
  reads true on the settle and proof visits too.
- Quest card S is now nine exclusive cards, one per visit, on the same three counters, so its tip always names the
  next visit (the part, fit / settle / test, and the price on a fit).
- Kess's after-fitting quest card (parts_fitted gte 3) now reads in plainer words: "Three of Vega's parts are in you
  now. What is left of her is a shell on his table. When Lyra and Nova hurt you, he is the one who puts you back
  together." LO asked what the old card meant; the new line says it. True on render: Lyra and Nova are named to the
  player in 0.2.4, and Kess's repair (`kess_patch_up`, vega_damage -25) stays open after the fitting.
