## Why

0.2.5 ended with Wren stronger than she has ever been, and still losing. Lyra and Nova hunt her together, she
always runs, and Cain told her more units are coming off the line at the Rise (`cap_more_coming`,
`design_the_fitting.md` part five). She has a body that fights and a man she chose, and no way to win.

This release takes the man away, and she gives herself up to get him back. It spends three things that were
held for later:

- **The Chairman on screen.** Aldous Vance has never spoken to her. Here he does, once, at the Spire.
- **The Chairman's wife.** The opening tells Wren that Cain murdered her (`design_book.md:478`). The book has
  always said that story is a lie (`design_book.md:545`). Here she learns the truth.
- **Mercer's end.** He is still alive in the underworld under a new name (`design_book.md:2866`). He dies in
  this release.

## Part one — CAIN IS TAKEN

Cain is caught. It is the company's doing. He is not killed; he is held, because he is the one thing Wren would
trade herself for.

**Agreed with LO 2026-11-10 ("Yes"): the hunt does not come.**

1. **The wrong night.** Since 0.2.5, Lyra and Nova step out of the crowd on the Underground strip to hunt her
   (`rand_units_street`, `5_scenes.toml:25566`). One night they do not. She walks the strip ready to run, and
   nobody comes.
2. **She works it out.** If they are not hunting her, they are hunting someone else, and there is only one person
   they would want. She runs to the Site.
3. **Too late.** The Site is wrecked: the lamp at the far end knocked over, blood on the floor, Cain gone. He is
   there every night from 21:00 to 03:00 (`1_metadata_and_locations.toml:848-865`), so they knew exactly where to
   find him.
4. **The sign.** On his bench, where she will see it: her own designation, written the company's way. It says
   *we know where you go.*
5. **The next morning Mercer comes** with the offer (part two).

Why it holds: it uses the hunt the player already knows, and the threat is the hunt *not* happening. She was not
there and could not have stopped it; it is not her fault and it feels like it. The order to spare Wren
(`design_the_transfer.md` part seven) now reads as patience: they were waiting for him.

**When:** at the start of the release, the first time a player walks the strip at night. The release opens here.

## Part two — MERCER BRINGS THE OFFER

The company does not send soldiers to Wren. It sends Mercer.

He is the man who used to own her, living in a rented room behind the market stalls (`mercer_room`, "The
Lockup"). The company finds him and gives him a job: carry one message, and get his old life back.

The message: **"You, for him."** She gives herself up, and Cain walks free.

Mercer wants this deal more than she does. It is the penthouse, the name, the man he used to be.

## Part three — SHE SAYS YES, AND MEANS IT

There is no plan and no trick. She gives herself up so that Cain goes free.

The company keeps its word: Cain is let go. They never needed him. They need her.

## Part four — MERCER'S END

Mercer gets what he was promised. His old life comes back.

Then the Chairman has him killed. A man who sold once will sell again. It is the first thing the player sees the
Chairman do, before he ever speaks, and it says who he is.

**Agreed with LO 2026-11-10 ("Yess"): reported, not shown.**

1. **He walks her in himself.** His last job. He takes her into the Spire in his old suit, proud of it.
2. **He is at the meeting.** He stands at the Chairman's shoulder the way he used to. The Chairman thanks him,
   politely.
3. **His life comes back.** The penthouse, the name, for a few days.
4. **One last visit.** He comes to her cell once, because he wants her "like before": him on top, the way the game
   began. The drain works on him now (`mercer_drains_done`), so the player chooses: let him have her, or drain him.
5. **The guard tells her.** The next night, in passing: *"Your old owner. Went off the roof of his own
   building."* She knows who did it.

Why it holds: one cold line lands harder than a death scene and says how the Chairman deals with people. Mercer
gets a real end, on top for one night and then gone. And it gives the guard a reason to talk to her early, which
is the way into his ladder (part seven).

**The player loses:** the last visit is Mercer's final scene. After it he is gone for good.

## Part five — THE CHAIRMAN

Back in the Spire, the Chairman meets her. It is the first time they have ever spoken.

He is calm and polite, and that is what makes him frightening. He tells her **what** he wants: her body, and the
part of it that feels. He does not tell her **why**. He keeps his wife out of it. She finds that out herself, at
the end.

**Agreed with LO 2026-11-10 ("Yes").**

- **Where:** his floor, behind the one door with the two men in front of it (`chairmans_floor`'s own
  description). Mercer stands at his shoulder.
- **The register:** he is the first man in the game who does not want to fuck her. He wants to live in her body.
  Calm, polite, never raises his voice.
- **The beats, with the lines as drafted** (the prose pass may tighten them, not change what they say):
  1. Kind: *"Sit. You've been running a long time."*
  2. What she is to him: *"I've built a thousand of them. Not one has ever been afraid. You're afraid right now.
     That is the most valuable thing in this building."*
  3. What he wants: *"I want to know how it was put in you. All of it."*
  4. She asks why: *"That's not a question you get to ask yet."* He never says why; that is the ending.
  5. Cain: *"He's free. I keep my word. Ask Mr. Mercer."* Mercer smiles. The player will know, days later, what
     that line was worth.
  6. Sent down: *"They'll take you down now. Try to be useful."*
- **Her part:** almost nothing. She has just given herself up. Small choices only (look at him, look away); the
  power in the room is all his.
- **What it opens:** the cell, the tests and the guard. The routine of the release starts here.

## Part six — THE TESTS

She is the company's test subject. The empty bodies on the line do not respond; she does. That is why they want
her and not a robot.

The tests are sex as data. They measure what she feels and how much. This is the "used" half of the release, the
daytime routine, repeatable, and at the start she simply takes it.

The tests cost her energy, and that is the one meter that can slow the night side down.

**Agreed with LO 2026-11-10 ("Yes").**

- **Where and when:** the lab, in the day, one test a day.
- **Who:** the technician (man 2) runs them. This is how she meets him; his ladder grows out of the tests.
- **Cost:** energy. A hard test day leaves her too tired for the guard that night.
- **Her part:** she does not choose them. It is done to her. This is the "used" half of the release.

| Day | Test | What they measure |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Wired up.** Stripped, sensors all over her, his hands checking her body like a machine's | whether she reacts at all |
| 2 | **The machine.** A device on her, run until she comes, while they watch the readings | how she climaxes |
| 3 | **Pain.** They push until it hurts and check what the robots cannot do | the part of her that feels pain, which she kept in 0.2.5 |
| 4 | **The empty body.** They put her with one of the line's male bodies. It fucks her and feels nothing | whether her feeling passes into it |
| 5+ | **The daily test.** Repeatable, a mix of the four | more data, every day |

Step 4 shows what they are after, putting feeling into an empty body, before she knows why. The player starts to
suspect; the file at the end confirms it.

The readings from these tests are what the technician's drain gives her later (part seven, man 2): she gets to see
her own data. And they are the release's contrast: by day she is used, by night, with the guard, she uses him.

## Part seven — THE CLIMB: THE DRAIN WORKS ON EVERYONE

Inside, she finds out the drain works on every man in the building. That is the turn of the release: a prisoner
who is more powerful than she has ever been.

She climbs through four men. Each one is a ladder with the drain at the top, and each drain gives her one thing
and opens the next man.

| # | Man | Where | His drain gives her | Opens |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **The guard** | her cell, nights | an hour out of the cell each night | her floor, and the lab at night |
| 2 | **The technician** | the lab, during the tests | the test records: they are building a body for someone | the head scientist's office |
| 3 | **Sabin** | his lab (`sabin_lab`), late evening | the project's name, and that there was a first attempt years ago | the records room |
| 4 | **The keeper of records** | the records room | the wife's file | the ending |

The guard, the technician and the keeper are new people. **Man 3 is Sabin** (LO, 2026-11-10: "A"). He built the
line (`design_the_transfer.md`, "the eleven thousand is in this room"), so a separate head scientist would have
duplicated him. This is not the Sabin pitch LO turned down: there is no deal and no "study" sex. He is one man on
her climb.

### The worked example — the guard (agreed shape)

- **Who he is:** the night guard on her cell. Big, bored, ordinary. He has been told she is company property, a
  machine, not a person. He has been watching her through the glass for weeks.
- **Where and when:** her cell, at night, while he is on shift (about 23:00 to 05:00).
- **The rule:** one step per night. The next step opens the next night. That is the rule Cain's loop and Kess's
  fittings already use.
- **The ladder:**

  | Night | Step |
  |---|---|
  | 1 | He watches through the glass. She knows, and undresses slowly for it. |
  | 2 | He comes in on a made-up reason. His hands are on her and she lets him. |
  | 3 | She is on her knees in the cell, the door open behind him. |
  | 4 | He fucks her against the cell wall, the first time. |
  | 5–6 | He keeps coming back, a repeatable scene, while she builds him up. |
  | 7 | The drain. He is hers. |

- **What she gets:** he leaves the door open an hour each night. A new choice appears on the cell, "Walk the
  floor", and his quest card names the next step and his shift.
- **The day around it:** the tests in the day, rest in the cell, the guard at night. No money and no travel.
- **Length:** about seven nights, twenty to thirty minutes of play.

The other three men use the same rule, one step a visit, five to seven visits each. The whole release is about
25 to 30 in-game days.

### The other three (agreed shape, LO 2026-11-10)

Same rule as the guard, one step a visit, five to seven visits each. Each is a different kind of man so the sex
does not repeat.

**Man 2 — the technician.**
- **Who:** young, careful, runs her tests every day. Trained to see her as a machine; talks to her like an
  appliance.
- **What he wants:** to prove she is only a machine, because then nothing he does to her counts.
- **Where:** the lab. By day he runs the tests; at night he works late alone, and the guard's open door lets her
  reach him.
- **The ladder:** by day his hands go further than the test needs. At night she comes to him on purpose, and he
  takes her on the test table. The turn: he starts calling her by her name.
- **His drain gives:** the test records (they are building a body for someone) and Sabin's hours.

**Man 3 — Sabin.**
- **Who:** the roboticist who built the line. He already knows her: in Whose Hand she worked for him under a cover
  and slept with him in his lab (`hub_sabin`, `loop_sabin_lab`, both still open in 0.2.5.1).
- **What he wants:** admiration. Someone who understands how good his work is.
- **The twist:** his best reader was the test subject all along. Same woman; now he knows what she is.
- **Where:** his lab, late evening.
- **The ladder:** she asks about his work, he shows her the plans, she praises it, and nobody has ever done that.
  Then she is on his bench on top of the plans.
- **His drain gives:** the project's name, that there was a first attempt years ago, and that its file is in the
  records room.
- *For the blueprint:* his existing hub and loop gate on `sabin_consulted` and `in_vega`
  (`5_scenes.toml:17021`). Inside the company the new ladder replaces them; how the old canvases are closed is a
  blueprint question, extend-only.

**Man 4 — the keeper of records.**
- **Who:** the Chairman's most loyal man, at his side for thirty years. He guards the sealed files.
- **What he wants:** nothing from her, which makes him the hardest. Only the Chairman's approval. His loyalty is
  the lock.
- **Where:** the records room, at night.
- **The ladder:** at first he will not look at her. She keeps coming back until he wants her more than he fears
  the Chairman. The slowest climb of the four.
- **His drain gives:** the wife's file. The release ends.

**The chain:** the guard opens the lab at night; the technician gives Sabin's hours; Sabin names the records room;
the keeper has the file.

## Part eight — THE WIFE'S FILE (THE ENDING)

The keeper of records' drain gives her the sealed file. What it says:

- Years ago the Chairman's wife was dying. He tried to save her by moving her into a machine body.
- The body was empty. Nothing came through. **She died on the table.**
- The company buried it by blaming Cain. "The rogue murdered the Chairman's wife" was his own failure.
- **What he wants now:** he is old, and he wants to move into a body himself. An empty body kills whoever goes
  in, the way it killed his wife. He needs one that feels, built on Wren's pattern. That is what the tests are
  for.

What she knows and he does not: **a transfer can be survived.** She did it in 0.2.4. He has never learned that
it works (`design_the_transfer.md`, Sabin "does not learn that the transfer works").

**The end card:** she knows what he wants and why. Next: the vault, the line, and the way out.

## What the player loses

Said plainly, because it has to be said before it is built:

- **Mercer is dead.** His scenes at the Lockup and his loop are gone for good after this point.
- **Cain's loop is closed** while she is inside. Cain is free, but she is not with him.
- **The city is closed** for the whole release. She is a prisoner; the release plays inside the company: the
  cell, the lab, the floors she drains her way into. The map comes back in the next release, when she gets out.

## Old saves (agreed with LO 2026-11-10, "Yes")

1. **Who starts the release:** only a save that has reached the end of 0.2.5 (the first Badly-hurt loss and Cain's
   warning, `more_coming`). Anyone earlier plays the older content first, as now.
2. **She chooses when to go in.** Mercer's offer stays open. The player can finish what they are doing in the city,
   then go to Mercer and say yes. Before the yes, a plain warning: *"Once you go in, the city is closed until the
   next release."* The sacrifice is real; the player picks the moment.
3. **Nothing is deleted.** The city, Cain's loop and every place are switched off by a flag while she is inside,
   never removed or renamed (`save-safety.md`). They come back when she gets out, next release.
4. **Mercer:** after his last visit a flag marks him dead and all his scenes switch off. His saved numbers stay in
   the save, unused.
5. **What a player loses:** anything unfinished in the city is frozen while she is inside; Mercer, for good;
   Cain's loop until the next release.

## Engine facts this rests on

- **The sealed room.** The Room held her with a location that has no `entry_from` (so it sits on no map and has
  no "Leave"), one child attached by `entry_from` and locked on a trait nothing sets, and a teleport in
  (`1_metadata_and_locations.toml:1789-1808`, `5_scenes.toml:2655`). Its three traps are written at
  `1_metadata_and_locations.toml:1770-1777`: never attach a child with `parent`; the lock is a TRAIT, not a flag;
  `version = "1.0"` or the block fails open. The city was never touched, because she could not reach it.
- **Why not `in_vega`'s way.** 0.2.4 held her by one flag gated across about 120 lines in four files. Too wide to
  repeat.
- **Why not the existing Rise rooms.** `the_rise_floor` and `sabin_lab` both have unconditional way-out cards that
  lead to `spire_plaza` and the city (`activity_leave_the_rise`, `3_activities.toml:3852`; `activity_leave_the_lab`,
  `:4061`). The release uses new rooms.
- **One step per calendar day.** A day flag re-stamped on every step, plus `days_since_flag gte 1` on every step
  after the first, and a "come back tomorrow" band first in the node's chain (`kess_fitting`,
  `3_activities.toml:4888-5039`). The step counter moves on the **located card**, never inside the triggerless loop
  (`activity_cain_trains`, `:5508-5574`).
- **The drain.** It fires on the finisher's choice, never on a node (`5_scenes.toml:17444-17445`). First drain:
  `sex_finisher_type eq 2` + `equipped_weapon eq 1` + `drain_charge gte 1` + the man's counter `lt 1`, effects
  counter `+1` and `drain_charge set 0` (`sabin`, `:17707-17726`). Repeats burn charge only (`:17728-17746`). The
  counter is a trait so the next arc reads it with `gte 1`.
- **The charge refills only in the city**: the cradle and the cot (`3_activities.toml:443`, `:529`). Inside, she
  needs her own refill (calls, 1).
- **Energy.** Shown as "Charge" (`0_systems_spec.toml:452`). A scene refuses through `costs` (greys the choice,
  `5_scenes.toml:463-475`). The Room's used scenes debit it on the exit (`:3036-3038`). There is no daily decay.
- **Night rows.** Two schedule rows across midnight, every weekday, each with a `when` carrying `version = "1.0"`
  (`1_metadata_and_locations.toml:848-866`). Rows attach to the last `[[npcs]]` above them; new people go after
  `npc_dace` (`:1026`).
- **Undo is a way out of any sealed room** (`design_captivity_the_room.md:280-303`). The fix was pulled and parked
  as beat_0045 (`authoring_state.json:840`). Known issue, not fixed in this release.
