## 1. The fantasy — what the player comes here to feel

**The premise LO picked:** **taboo at home** — the house she comes back to, and who is in the next
room; college, the café and the neighbourhood make it risky, because what happens outside comes home
with her.

**In one sentence:** the house she grew up in starts looking at her differently from her first day of
college, and every person in it wants something from her.

**The model to beat:** In Her Own Hands — **what ours does better:** the house reacts. Mark, Laura and
Ryan notice, talk, and treat her differently because of her life outside.

**Which moments does this game promise?** Being seen, taboo at home, and consequences she lives
with (LO, 2026-10-05). Her firsts mostly happen outside and come home as consequences.

## 4. The first step with one person — Ryan, "The hall, first morning"

Moment kind: **being seen**. Step 1 with `npc_ryan`.

1. **The fantasy.** The one person in the house on her side is the one she shouldn't want, and the
   first thing she learns about him on her first day of college is that he looks.
2. **The temptation, and his want before it.** His hub carries the leak first: at low want his eyes
   go to her and away too fast; higher, he holds the look and asks *"You good, @player.nickname?"*
   Why now: first morning of college, one shared bathroom, both need it before 8 — a fixed day-one
   event, not a dice roll. She steps out of the steam in a towel and Ryan is leaning on the wall,
   waiting his turn. He doesn't move. His eyes drop, come back up: *"First day of college, huh. Look
   at you."* She moves first.
3. **Her answers, and his "no".** The choices open by stage, as on SP2 §1 (fixed 10-06, lead's
   default). Good Girl: "Run to your room", face hot (want +) · "Knock next time" (parked; *"Yeah.
   Sorry."*, his next step waits three days) · stop in the doorway and talk — he's the only one who
   asks about her first day (warmth +). Curious adds "Walk past slowly" (want +). Bold adds "Let him
   look": *"Seen enough?"*, towel held at her chest (want ++). Letting him look is not on day one:
   she starts at Good Girl. Her final no on his path, each at its time: on day one *"Don't ever look
   at me like that. (ends his path)"*; later *"Your secret's safe. You're my brother, that's all.
   (ends his path)"* (SP2's Ryan block names both).
4. **Her voice at her level.** Good Girl, on day one: *"Ryan — god — I'm in a towel, turn around."*
   At Bold, on a later morning: *"You can look. You were going to anyway."* When she catches him
   looking: *"Shit. Sorry. Take your time… college suits you."*
5. **Who notices.** Laura calls up the stairs: *"@player, you'll be late on your first day!"* At
   breakfast she looks from one to the other, then rules: *"Ryan, you shower first from now on."*
   That puts him on the other side of that door every morning — her almost-noticing.
6. **What sticks.** `ryan_hall_seen`, plus `ryan_hall_tease` / `ryan_hall_talked` /
   `ryan_hall_knock` by her answer; Ryan's want and warmth move. Later he names it: *"You walked
   slower than you needed to, the other morning."* Or he raps twice on her doorframe: *"See? I knock
   now."*
7. **The moment to remember.** *"First day of college, huh. Look at you."*
8. **The door it opens, and the clip.** Ryan's ladder, the main taboo, and Laura's watching. Clip
   intent: a young adult woman (18+) stepping out of a steamy bathroom in a white towel into a narrow
   hallway, a man leaning on the wall, his point of view.

**Leads to:** Ryan A step 3, his phone on the sink (Ryan A step 2 was cut 10-06; Ryan covering a
short Sunday is now an everyday act that raises Warmth).

**How the opening hands over to it:** the opening ends on her first morning, with the bathroom door
opening onto the hall.
