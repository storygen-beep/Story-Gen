## A. The step row — seven columns, filled before a step is written

The row sits above the branch map in `sheets/scenes/<step>.md`.

| column | the question | source |
|---|---|---|
| **the moment** | One line: the thing a player would name afterwards, and what is new in it against the step before. | ARC Q1, Q2 |
| **who sees or knows** | Who is in the room, or finds out after. "Nobody" is allowed only as a declared choice, written down. | HEAT Q8; register test 5 (who notices); rule 2 |
| **what she risks** | Being caught, a secret, a person, a price. The stake is hers, not the player's loss of control. | HEAT Q8; rule 4 |
| **how she says yes** | Out loud, and both minds if she has two. The button carries the yes. | HEAT Q8; rule 3 |
| **his move** | What he wants for his own reason, shown before, and what he *does* about it here. Reacting is not a move. | ARC Q3; the-arc A13; register test 1 (want) |
| **does it finish** | The act goes to its end on the body and the finish is shown, or this is the ladder's one cut-off, named as such. | rule 5; §5.2 "no climax shown, or a scene cut off" |
| **what changes after** | Something the world keeps: a rule, a schedule, a label, a price, a person's standing line, the next step. A flag nobody reads is not a change. | rule 9; ARC Q5, Q7 |

**The fail rule.** Two empty columns: the step is not written. One empty column: the sheet's "Why" table
says why it is empty on purpose.

`the no` stays where it is today, on the ladder table of the person sheet (ARC Q4; register test 6). It is
already asked, so it is not repeated here.

## B. The ladder rules — eight, checked on the whole arc before any step is written

1. **Every step adds a new witness, a new risk or a new kind of yes** over the one before. Two flat steps in a
   row: cut one. (HEAT Q8)
2. **The top step of the release is a payoff:** a known person, a witness, the frame the arc built paid off,
   finished on the body. A talk or a locked door is not a top step. (HEAT Q8; §4, "a payoff after a long
   build")
3. **One cut-off per ladder.** Kayla's call, Claire's knock, a footstep on the stairs: once. Every other step
   finishes.
4. **He moves at least every other step.** Not all her initiative. (ARC Q3. IHOH's Bobby pulls her legs into
   his lap; CoT's best friend asks her.)
5. **His meter is the lock.** A step pays less than the next step needs; the gap is paid by his hot
   repeatables. Target: two to four hot visits between steps. (the-meters rule 4: one visit moves 1–10% of
   the next threshold, 3–10 visits between steps. Ours today: each step pays exactly what the next needs,
   so zero visits.)
6. **Each stage has one hot repeatable, designed in the same sheet as the step it follows,** written in
   full, no placeholders. (CoT: the handjob and the sex are repeatables, not steps. IHOH: TV naked, the nap,
   yoga, the sleep visit.)
7. **Her voice changes with her level:** a low line and a high line per step. (rule 7; register test 4;
   `spine/SP2_ladders.md` already has `her_line_low` and `her_line_high`.)
8. **Something still happens after the top step.** (ARC Q7)

**The fail rule.** Two of eight failing: the ladder is not built.

## C. Not columns, on purpose

- **Media.** A declared media block counts as media present. Files are checked at release, never before.
- **Word count, the explicit-beat ratio, the crude-word ceiling.** The build measures these after the fact.
  They are checks, not design.
- **Love (Warmth) on a lust path.** A side meter: rent help, covering for her. It never locks a lust step.

## D. Where the page is used

1. **Before a pitch.** The pitcher fills the seven columns for the step it proposes. (Step 7 of the redesign
   puts the columns in its brief; until then the lead fills them.)
2. **Before a sheet.** The step row goes above the branch map in `sheets/scenes/<step>.md`.
3. **Before a build.** The eight ladder rules are ticked on the person sheet, one line each.
4. **After the build.** Reader, gates, live player. Checks, not design.
