The skill has no page for this.
> It says each person's ordinary week: where they are, by day and part of the day, and what changes it.
> Steps pick their windows from here instead of inventing them. No steps, no exact hours, no money
> numbers: lecture and café hours come with their systems, and the board turns each row into hours.
> Rules followed: `the-sheets.md` S5 · `the-clock.md` C2, C5 · `the-surfaces.md:83`, `:160` ·
> `the-board.md:124-128`, `:152-170` · `the-map.md` R2, R6c · `the-first-hour.md:618-622`.
> Everything nobody had decided before this page is marked **(new)** and listed again at the end.

**How to read it.**
- The day has four parts: morning, afternoon, evening, night.
- A **row** is one person at one place in one window. Every row is a hub the game must write
  (`the-surfaces.md:83`).
- A week changes only through something the game records: a flag, a phase or a step. It never
  changes by itself.
- **release 1** on a person marks a candidate for release 1, by when Ella meets them. Release 1's
  scope: page 7 decides.
