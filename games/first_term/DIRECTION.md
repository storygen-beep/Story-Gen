# The direction — First Term

> A game-only page (layer 2), decided by LO in chat on 2026-10-05. The skill has no page for this.
> It says what is going on in the game: what keeps moving, the big phases, and the calendar.
> The goals, mystery and rival are on `IDEA.md` §2; the moments the game promises are on `IDEA.md` §1.
> No steps, hours, schedules, money numbers or system design here; those belong to later layers.

**The game in one line:** Ella, 18, starts college at the beginning of autumn while she still lives
at home. Every day she goes out, changes a little, and brings something home. The house notices.

Moving out is not in this game. The house stays her hub the whole way through.

---

## 1. What keeps moving in the house

**The debt to Vance.**
- **Source:** mostly Mark B (`proposals/ARC_IDEAS.md:474`), which LO did not pick; also Mark A
  step 2. Kept on purpose by LO, 2026-10-05.
- Rent is taken every Sunday and never stops. A short week brings a letter, or Vance at the door.
- Nobody is ever thrown out, and the game never ends over rent.
- The debt is Mark's weak spot, and the reason Vance knocks.

**Laura watching.**
- The `laura_suspicion` talk track. Laura grows more suspicious, or more curious, only when Ella
  does something: comes home late, lies, brings home a grade. It never moves on its own.

## 2. The twist after goal 1

When goal 1 ends (`pays_her_own_way`), Ella learns how far behind the house is with Vance, and she
chooses whether to help. The choice stays open: a no now is parked, and she can still help later.

**She helps** (a flag such as `helped_with_debt`):
- **Mark** owes her, not Vance. His weak spot is hers to keep, and the table stays on her terms
  for good.
- **Vance** stops knocking at the family's door and deals with Ella directly. His porch opens to
  her, which leads toward goal 3's town.
- **Laura** doesn't know where the money came from, so she watches more closely.
- **Rent** is still taken every Sunday.
- How she helps can be her body: Mark A 15.

**She doesn't help:**
- Rent keeps running, Vance keeps knocking, and the letters keep coming.
- Mark stays in debt, so his weak spot stays open.
- She can always pay with her body. Paid opens at Bold, and every demand has a written no.

## 3. Her life outside: five threads

Each thread keeps moving whatever happens at home, and each brings something home.

**College** — Hale, Nadia · campus · arc: Hale B
- What college is for: the first place she changes. Nobody there knows the good girl, so she can
  try being someone else.
- What the direction needs from it: a grades number Laura can read, and the `campus_talk` track.
  Lectures, grades and exams are designed with the college system, later.
- Phase 1: new and unknown; Hale notices her. Brings home: her first grades.
- Phase 2: she has a name on campus; Hale's office after hours opens. Brings home: grades Laura reads.
- Phase 3: she uses her name; Hale's hour is hers to set. Brings home: grades that change with her.
- Phase 4: campus knows exactly who she is. Brings home: the name itself.

**The café** — Tom · the café · arcs: Tom B, Mark A
- Phase 1: the job, the uniform, her first tips. Brings home: money for Sundays.
- Phase 2: regulars know her; Tom sells her time. Brings home: twenties; Mark asks.
- Phase 3: she names her own price. Brings home: more money than a waitress makes.
- Phase 4: after closing is hers. Brings home: perhaps the man from town who sits at the table (goal 3).

**Friends** — Zoe · Zoe's apartment · arcs: Zoe A, Laura C, Ryan A
- Phase 1: her first party, with Zoe leading. Brings home: a late night; Laura is on the stairs.
- Phase 2: dares on campus, party nights. Brings home: borrowed clothes; Ryan hears her come in.
- Phase 3: Ella sets the dares. Brings home: what she did, told or caught.
- Phase 4: Ella hosts the party everyone goes to (goal 2's end). Brings home: her name.

**Dating** — Jake · campus · arc: Jake B
- Phase 1: they meet. Brings home: a name Ryan doesn't like.
- Phase 2: dates; he wants to show her off. Brings home: Jake at the door; Laura sees.
- Phase 3: he wants more; Ryan is jealous. Brings home: a choice between them.
- Phase 4: he is the guy the house knows about, or her cover.

**The neighbourhood** — Vance · his house, the street, the corner shop, the park · arc: Mark A
(no Vance arc yet)
- Phase 1: a look from his porch as she walks past. Brings home: what he said; Mark notices.
- Phase 2: letters, a knock. Brings home: the debt, a little more each week.
- Phase 3: she learns what Mark owes. Brings home: Mark's weak spot.
- Phase 4: she deals with Vance herself, or leaves it to Mark (the twist).

## 4. The four phases

For planning only: the player never sees them, and they have no dates. Each phase ends on what the
house knows (flags and meters), never on a week, and no phase ending waits on two arcs at once.

1. **New rules.** Home knows nothing; she is still the good girl. Outside: her first lectures, the
   café job, her first party, meeting Jake.
2. **A secret in every room.** Each person at home holds one of her secrets, and she holds one of
   theirs. Outside: a name on campus, Tom selling her time, dates with Jake, grades going home.
3. **She sets the terms.** Laura suspects, Ryan knows, Mark is losing the table. Outside: she names
   her price, sets Hale's hour and Zoe's dares; Jake wants more.
4. **Her name on campus.** Campus knows her, and home has to deal with a woman it no longer
   recognises.
