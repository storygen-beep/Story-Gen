# Scout card — a weekly party (system)

> Scouted 2026-10-07 for first_term. The skill: near, no rule. `templates/cards/greek_life.md` has CoT's Thursday Greek-house parties (hours, 7 activities, renown); `templates/cards/reputation.md` has the quad party as a gossip hub (groped at promiscuity 100, threesome offer at 200). Neither has the invite, the drinks, the dare, who is there, the weekly reset, the way home or hosting.
> Games used: Course of Temptation (CoT), In Her Own Hands (IHOH), Shady Deals (SD). Counts only: 38 of the 76 games off the pass list have a nightlife/party/drinking system (round 8 census), among them degrees-of-lewdity (925 passages touched) and college-daze (427).
> Model: CoT, the Friday quad party at Helleborine Hall (a residence hall's weekly party, reached by invite). Contrast: IHOH, the Friday-night bottle game at a sleepover she hosts in her own apartment (she is the host; dares and drinks are counted inside the game). Second contrast: SD, the yacht party she pays to host (the only player-hosted party with a cost and a cooldown).
