## What is deliberately NOT in a sheet

**The prose.** A sheet carries labels, gates and consequences — not the paragraphs. One rung per
proposal is written out as a **voice sample**, so the shape and the writing are approved separately
and neither hides the other.

**One exception, and it is why the sheets above are long: every explicit beat is written out where
it sits.** The pivot rule is a reading test and no label answers it. Each carries its own measurement
line — word count, the counted words, the rungs named, and the body-words-by-sentence shape.

⚠️ **The incident behind that exception.** Three beats were labelled `[explicit]` and scored **0, 0
and 1** against the gate's own word list. `hard` and `wet` are not on it — it is anatomy and acts.
Two of them were also coy: one said *"what looking at you has done to him"*, which gestures at his
cock rather than saying it. **The word list caught a craft failure, not an arithmetic one.**

## The same thing happened here, and it is recorded

The first draft of `simone_r_go_up`'s four beats scored **0 of 4 explicit**, with median sentences of
**21 to 32 against a ceiling of 14**. They read fine. They were long-sentenced and coy, and the
instrument said so in one run. The rewrite is what is on the sheet: 3 of 4 registering, median
sentence 11.

**And beat 3 pivoted.** `--beat` flagged that its last sentence carried no body word — the drift off
the body that precedes a pivot. Rewritten to end on the act.

⚠️ **Assume you are doing this.** The register pivot was authored three increments running, each time
by someone who had just re-read the rule against it.
