## The rule

**A6 — a garment is a rung.** This step asks her to do **nothing at all.** It asks her to *wear*
something, and the arc will not pass until she does.

The field's version lists what qualifies with a checkmark each — *a skirt that can flip up*, *a top
that might expose your nipples*, *a top that shows cleavage*. Ours does the same, in Simone's mouth,
and the qualifying predicate is **`worn_exposure`**.

⚠️ **`worn_exposure` is the only one of the four worn predicates that reads an EMPTY slot**, which is
what makes it the right one here.

## A6b — somebody else sets the dress code

This is not her choosing. **The house tells her what the girls wear on a Thursday**, which makes it a
ladder she is *put on* rather than one she climbs — and the arc content is her reaction to each rung,
not the choosing of it.

## What money has to do with it

**R1b — what money buys has to STAY bought and be READ.** The outfit is bought at `the_quad` and it
is read **here**, and later on the ambient roll.

⚠️ **Money buys the KEY to a rung, never a meter point.** A shop that sells an arc's prerequisite is
doing more work than a shop that sells a stat. That is A4b's third key kind — *a preparation bought
and endured* — arriving from the economy side.

## The gate it feeds
`the wardrobe is read` — a game declaring `[[clothing]]` must read it somewhere, or she can dress and
the world does not look. And **A6's other half**: `worn_exposure` moves the **floor of the ambient
roll** at `the_quad` and `the_pledge_house`. Dressed ordinarily something happens ~36% of the time;
dressed for it, ~71%. **Same scenes, twice as much world.**
