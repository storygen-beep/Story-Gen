## Which shape this is, and it is declared

**A3b — PARKED, not counted.** The two shapes sit at opposite ends and an arc picks one deliberately:

| | the refusal | when |
|---|---|---|
| **parked** ✅ | free, reversible, and **the game prints the place and the hour** | the default. Most offers |
| counted | tracked, warned with the content named, and finally closed | when the closing is itself content |

**The one thing neither shape does is stay silent about which it is.** Writing every refusal as
permanent makes a game a minefield; writing every one as parked makes nothing matter.

## What the game prints when she says no

> *If she changes her mind it is the kitchen, after her mother has gone.*

That is the whole mechanism, and it is verbatim what the field does: *"if you change your mind
later, just talk to your husband in the bedroom when he's there."* **A place and an hour.**

## The three rules A3 lands here

- **The refusal is free and in character** — and it is also *remembered*. `ray_refused` is read by
  four later sites, so the game knows she said no once.
- **A door may close, but out loud.** This one does not close. Nothing here is forfeited.
- **A refusal routes.** ⚠️ Saying no is not a dead end and not a punishment. `ray_refused` shortens
  the wait on **Simone's** step 3 by two days — the night she does not spend in the kitchen is a
  night she is on campus.

⚠️ **Across every v2 game, no refusal is counted, nothing warns that a door is closing, and no
refusal opens anything.** This is the first that routes.

## The gate it feeds
`she can say no` — the field puts a real refusal on one click in fifty, and **79% of them lead
somewhere the yes does not.**
