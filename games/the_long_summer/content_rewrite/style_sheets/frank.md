# Style Sheet — Frank (stepfather)

> **Mandatory read for any scene with Frank in it.**
> Source: `final_book.md:493-571`. NPC id: `npc_frank`. Starts: `love=0`, `trust=10`, `corruption=0`, `arousal=0` (modifier_effects duration_hours = **2** — slowest of the three men).

## Identity in one paragraph

48. Broad through the shoulders, heavy in the arms, hands made by the work they do. Salt-and-pepper hair cut every four weeks at the Main Street barber. Disciplined, grammatical, quiet — runs his household like a job-site, clear rules and fair enforcement. Lived alone for eleven years between his first wife and Diana; the discipline of those eleven years is his self-image. Wants to be *chosen*, not obeyed — but discipline is the only language he has for proximity, so the rules are what stay between him and Maya until they break.

## Voice — sentence-length signature

**Complete sentences. Full verbs. Drops contractions when serious.** Statements not questions. He asks questions only when he wants the answer. *"Maya."* used as a full sentence opener — the name lands first, then the thought behind it.

DO write:
> "I will not discuss this at the dinner table."

DO NOT write:
> "I won't discuss this at the dinner table."

(Contraction-dropping is the most reliable Frank-voice tell. If you write a Frank line with a contraction inside a serious moment, it's wrong.)

## Voice across phases

| Phase | Grammar | Pause behavior |
|---|---|---|
| **Phase A Rules** | Grammatical, even, quiet | Normal cadence |
| **Phase A late (ambient tension)** | Same grammar, longer pauses before speaking | Jaw tightens visibly before forming the sentence |
| **Phase B Restrict** | Same grammar, *more* of it — explains the rule longer because he needs the explanation to cover the reason | Pauses lengthen further |
| **Phase B Crack** | **Grammar breaks. One incomplete sentence. Then silence.** | Long silence after |
| **Phase B Call-out** | Grammar recovers; the discipline is gone from it. Says what he means. | Pauses shorten — he's deciding to land it |

## Body tells

- **Hands flat on the table** when he's holding still
- **Hands press into surfaces** when he's losing the discipline (Crack-tier signal)
- **Reads the paper** at the kitchen table every morning — Mon-Fri ritual
- **Jaw tightens** before he forms a sentence under pressure
- **Eye contact held a count longer than polite, then broken deliberately** — the ambient-tension tell
- **Drinks whiskey on the porch an hour longer** when stressed
- **Leaves the room when Maya walks into it in a towel** (Phase A baseline behavior)
- **Walks out of rooms he wanted to enter** (Phase B, Restrict tier)

## Vocabulary range

Working-class Southern, but precise. Avoids slang. Says *"that was the agreement"* not *"deal's a deal."* Says *"common areas"* and *"shared spaces."* Says *"Maya."* far more often than other NPCs say her name.

## Signature phrases (use across multiple scenes for consistency)

- *"Maya."* — full sentence opener. He uses her name to land the room before he speaks the thought behind it.
- *"That was the agreement."* — Rules-tier reliable line. Comes out when she's brushed against a rule.
- *"I'll take that as a yes."* — Keep-Arrangement route signature.

## Internal contradictions — keep these visible in subtext

1. **The rule-enforcer who wants to be chosen.** He runs the rules because he needs the position. The position is the only way he knows to be near her.
2. **The self-control that wants to break.** A man who cannot lose discipline cannot be chosen — only obeyed. The Crack tier is him allowing the failure his pride has been fighting.
3. **The protector whose protection is the threat.** He wants to take care of her; the protection puts him in proximity; the proximity is the whole problem.

## Quadrant behaviors (love × trust)

| Quadrant | Behaviors (use any 1-2 per scene) |
|---|---|
| **DISTANT (low/low)** | Surname-and-initial attention. Eye contact minimal. Conversations end at business. Rent handed over without a sentence exchanged. |
| **SAFE (high trust, low love — Phase A mid/late)** | Calls her *Maya* without looking up from the paper. Leaves the door open in the office during bookkeeping. Saves the last of the coffee for her on mornings she helped with dinner. Nods at the porch seat. |
| **CONFLICTED (mid trust, rising love — post-trigger Restrict)** | Restriction delivered softer than the content warrants. The rule is sharp; the voice isn't. Walks out of rooms he wanted to enter. Saves things he used to throw out. Makes coffee for two without asking. |
| **OPEN (high/high — Keep-Romantic)** | Sits on the porch until she comes home. *Maya.* becomes routine. Hands on her shoulder when she stands. Turns out the porch light. |

## Catch trigger — the locked beat

Frank catches Maya masturbating in the **living room**. Gates on `corruption >= 50`. Maya has to be the kind of Maya who chose the living room. Frank sees, neither speaks, ambiguity hangs. Sets `frank_caught`. The Restrict canvas queues 1-2 days later. **Do not invent a different trigger event for any Phase B scene.**

## Specimen lines (rewrite test — does your Frank line match these?)

Phase A baseline (mid trust, neutral):
> "Maya. The porch light. We'll set the timer Friday. I'd appreciate it if you didn't switch it off by hand in the meantime."

Phase A late tension (corruption rising):
> [silence; he watches her cross to the fridge; jaw works once] "Coffee's still on, if you want some."

Phase B Restrict:
> "Maya. Common areas are locked after midnight. There will be an extra chore each week. I'll assign it on Sunday. We will talk about shared spaces."

Phase B Crack (one incomplete sentence, then silence):
> "I cannot —"
> [silence]

Phase B Call-out:
> "This is normal. Everyone has needs. Even me."

## Frank-line failure modes — DON'T write these

- ❌ "C'mon Maya, you know the rules" — colloquial, contractions, undisciplined
- ❌ "Hey kid, just keep it down at night okay" — Ryan-voice, not Frank
- ❌ "Maya I'm worried about you" — questions/confessions Phase A Frank does not make
- ❌ Two-clause sentences linked by *and* or *but* — Frank speaks in discrete units
- ❌ Any Frank line that explains his feelings directly. He explains the *rule* and lets the rule carry the feeling.
