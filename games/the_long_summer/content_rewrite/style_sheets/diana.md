# Style Sheet — Diana (mother / household anchor)

> **Mandatory read for any scene with Diana present.**
> Source: `final_book.md:213-216` + locked constraint at `concept.md:39-40`. NPC id: `npc_diana`. **No Phase 1 arc.** Structural anchor only. `diana_awareness` accumulates silently — Diana does not confront in Phase 1.

## Identity in one paragraph

44. Maya's mother. Widow remarried to Frank. Warm, present, not distant — Maya trusts her. Strict in a *father-shaped* way: rules, routines, schedules. Dinner at 6:30. Chores have owners. Not a moralist or hypocrite. She's holding her own line — and Maya's — because that's what she learned after the first husband died. The voice you don't notice until she's already been heard.

## Voice — sentence-length signature

**Quiet sentences that finish.** Doesn't trail off. Doesn't rush. Uses Maya's name often and directly — *"Maya, the table."* not *"Hey, can someone set the table?"*

She **does not ask the question she does not want the answer to**. This is the load-bearing rule. She *could* ask Maya about Sunday afternoon, about the late shift, about the look across the table at dinner — and she does not. Her silence is the heaviest pressure in the house.

## Body tells (per band of `diana_awareness`)

These are **ambient-only** — they never appear in a sidebar; they live in prose only.

| `diana_awareness` band | Behavior |
|---|---|
| **Low (0-24)** | Doesn't look up when Maya comes in late |
| **Mid-low (25-49)** | Looks up, smiles, doesn't ask |
| **Mid-high (50-74)** | Looks up and doesn't smile |
| **High (75-100)** | Doesn't look up at all. Kitchen quieter. |

The high-band tell is the heaviest because *she returned to not-looking-up by choice*. The kitchen-quieter sentence is canon — write that.

## Vocabulary range

Mother-vocabulary, not lecture-vocabulary. *"Plate's hot." "Wash up." "There's leftovers if you want them."* No therapy speech. No advice volunteered. Cooks with her hands — *"a half-cup of flour," "until it pulls back from the side of the pan,"* shown in action not spoken.

## Signature phrases

- *"Maya."* — uses the name to land before requesting anything. (Frank does the same with different intent — Frank's lands authority; Diana's lands warmth.)
- *"Plate's hot."*
- *"There's leftovers."*
- *"Sit with me a minute."* — Sunday-afternoon-on-the-porch invitation. Reserved.

## What Diana NEVER does in Phase 1

- Confront Maya about anything sexual or moral
- Ask where she was Sunday
- Notice the new outfit out loud
- Inquire about the diner shift after 10pm
- Mention Frank's name in any way that suggests she knows something
- Comment on Maya's drawing if Maya is sketching across the room
- Cry. Get angry visibly. Threaten consequences.

The diana_awareness arc opens **Phase 2+**. Phase 1 is Diana being warm and silent. Hold the line.

## Quadrant behaviors

There aren't traditional quadrants for Diana — `love` and `trust` are not tracked because there's no romance arc and trust is presupposed. Use `diana_awareness` instead (see body tells above).

## Specimen lines

Ordinary Sunday morning (low awareness):
> "Maya. Coffee's ready. Frank's got the porch."

Mid-low awareness (~30) — looking up, smiling, not asking:
> [looks up from the cutting board, smiles]
> "There's eggs. You want eggs?"

Mid-high awareness (~60) — looking up, not smiling:
> [looks up, doesn't smile, holds the look one beat]
> "Plate's there if you want it."

High awareness (~80) — doesn't look up:
> [doesn't look up; the kitchen is quieter than usual]
> "Leftovers in the fridge."

Sunday porch (any band) — the recurring beat:
> "Sit with me a minute."

## Diana-line failure modes — DON'T write these

- ❌ "Maya, are you okay? You seem different lately." — explicit awareness verbalized
- ❌ "Frank told me you..." — Diana is never the conduit for Frank's information
- ❌ "I've been worried about you." — Diana doesn't volunteer worry
- ❌ Long sentences explaining her feelings — she doesn't explain
- ❌ Any direct confrontation about sex, money, or NPC relationships in Phase 1
- ❌ "I love you" as a spoken line. (It might land in Phase 2+. Not Phase 1.)
