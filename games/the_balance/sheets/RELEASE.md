## Why this page exists now

Every page we have left something at its tail, and all of it is the same kind of work — steps,
scenes, lines. **Nothing is blocked on a decision any more, so the picking rule has run out**, and
the question it hands back is the one left open on purpose: *what is the first build, and what
waits?*

## What the first build is, in one line

**Her first four weeks.** She owes $150 a Friday, she can't quite make it in week one, and by week
four she can — because of what she started doing to get there.

Four weeks is not a guess. `money.md` sets it: *the first build has to reach about week four, or the
squeeze never lifts on screen.*

## What it ends on

**The closing shift.** It's on the schedule in the cafe from the first week, her name isn't on it,
and it's the one thing she can see and can't have. Owen hands it over when she's worked enough
shifts *and* gone far enough with him — and that's where the first build stops.

## What waits

The gym · the library and the department · the art class · the row on a party night · Hale as
anything more than a name on a timetable · the three other teachers · Reyes · the letter home ·
exams · the person who recognizes her from the stream.

**Every one of those is a real part of the game.** None of them is needed to play four weeks.

## Two calls worth arguing with

**Campus is in, not held.** The whole point of the game is that college is what changes her. A first build without
it is a game about a girl with two jobs.

**Campus is thin.** Every campus room wants talk lines and a walk-in scene at three heights, and
that is the largest pile of writing in the whole design. Four rooms now, the rest after.
