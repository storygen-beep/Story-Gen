## The places

| place | what it's for | who's there |
|---|---|---|
| **The quad** | outdoors, the middle of campus, everything hangs off it. **Where being known is read** — people look at her, or they don't | everyone, between classes |
| **The lecture hall** | a hundred people and she's nobody. Only the sheet knows whether she came | her year, and whoever sits down next to her |
| **The classroom** | a dozen people with Hale in the room. Not having done the homework is visible to everybody. **Empty between classes, and that is the one campus room she can walk in on people in** | Hale and Sam, mornings |
| **The department** | his office and the corridor outside it. A different place once it empties | Hale, late afternoon |
| **The library** | quiet work, and a third floor nobody uses after eight | the serious ones, and almost nobody late |
| **The union** | cheap food and tables where people sit and talk. Where she's either with someone or on her own | Cara, and the crowd |
| **The gym** | being looked at while doing something ordinary. Showers, and a locker room with no doors on anything | strangers, and people who take it seriously |
| **The row** | the party houses, a walk from the quad. Where being known turns into being somewhere | the crowd, Tasha's world, Nate's year |

## Getting there and back

Forty minutes and $2 on the bus, or an hour on foot. **The row is walking distance from the quad**,
so being known pays off where she earned it — and the way home from it at night is through the
park, in the dark, against a ten o'clock that doesn't move.

## What campus never gives her

**Money.** Every hour here is hours and bus fare going out. That's deliberate: it's what makes the
cafe and the phone feel like the only two ways out of the Friday payment.

## What it hands to the steps after it

- three of the four campus faces are filled: **Cara** is the friend, **Jules** runs the parties, and
  Nate's friend was **cut** — Bree and Paige are in his year and already carry the quad home.
  **The one who recognizes her is held on purpose** for a later build
- **there is no private room on campus.** Everything here happens where someone could walk past
- the gym is the only room where being looked at costs her nothing — it needs something at stake
- the third floor of the library after eight, and the department once it empties, are both written
  as *quiet* and neither has a reason to go there yet
