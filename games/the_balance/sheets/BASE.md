## The test for which block something belongs to

**If it has no condition, it belongs to blocks 1 to 4.** It's what exists.

**If it changes because of what she's done, it belongs to a ladder.** A card is block 2; a choice
inside it that only opens further up is that person's ladder. A random event's hour is block 3; what
it turns into later is a ladder.

That includes the week itself. Owen on the closing shift once she's moved up, or a door left open
once she's been caught looking — those look like the base, and they're ladders. The base is the week
before anything has happened.

## The three rules

1. **Every row has a card.** If someone is in a room at an hour, that room has their card for that
   hour, and the card itself has no limit. Anything that should only happen once a day, or only past
   a certain point, is a choice inside the card. Someone who is away has no row.
2. **A random event with someone in it happens where and when they are.** If Nate is in the kitchen
   from eight to nine, his random events in the kitchen are eight to nine, and nowhere else.
3. **Walking in works the same way.** She can only walk in on somebody who is there.

## The locks

- **Later blocks add. They never move.** A ladder can add a new version of a random event; it can't
  change the event's hour or its room. If it needs to, that's a change to the earlier page, and it
  comes back there first.
- **Once this page is `[READY]`, nothing moves a row in the week** without this page changing first.
