## Screen one — her bedroom

Early, the first Monday of term. Nobody else on screen, and nobody is named yet.

Three facts and no more:

- she turned eighteen and starts college today
- her step dad paid her tuition, and the first payment back is due Friday
- her mum does not know he paid

The last line points her at the door.

## Screen two — the hallway

A shower running. A closed bedroom door. Her mum's bag already gone from the hook.

Still nobody named — the house is simply full of people she hasn't dealt with yet.

## Then the game opens — the kitchen, about seven thirty

Her step dad is at the table. The chore list is on the fridge. Her bus to campus goes in
twenty minutes.

**He says one thing about a job going at the cafe, and he never says it again.** He isn't asking
her for anything and he doesn't mention Friday. It is the only push the game ever gives her, and
she can ignore it.

## Her first free choice

Do a chore for the money · sit and eat with him · leave now and be early for her first class.

Money against time, on the very first click — the whole game in small. And the man she owes
has a face before she has spent a minute on her own.

## Everything below the words is the skill's job

How many screens this is cut into, what is written on each button, the age gate and the
character screen that sit in front of it, and the check that something is actually open where
she lands — all of that follows the starting-canvas rules we already have. **This page only
decides what happens.**

## What this page does not decide

Meeting the other seven people · what a chore pays · which locked door the first release ends
on · anything about the campus · anything about the cafe beyond the one line at the table.

## What it hands to the steps after it

- her bedroom, the hallway and the kitchen are the first rooms that have to exist
- a chore has to pay something
- classes need a time her bus can make
- the cafe has to be open and Owen has to be in it on that first day, or his one line points at a
  shut door
