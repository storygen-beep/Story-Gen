## The shape

Setup → problem → a person → conflict → a choice → temptation → the first goal → play.

The old opening watched her morning. This one puts people in front of her, lets them want things,
and ends on a question.

## Scene 1 — her room, Monday 07:00

Nobody else on screen. Said loudly, in her voice:

- she's eighteen, and college starts today
- her step dad paid her tuition, and she owes him $150 every Friday
- she has $10
- her mum doesn't know he paid, and if she finds out, the house blows up

Her thoughts are on screen from the first line. *He paid my tuition. That doesn't make him generous.
It makes me his.*

## Scene 2 — the kitchen, 07:30: Gil

Gil at the table with the paper. Her mum is there too: it's a ward day, so she's in the kitchen
until eight.

**Gil pushes.** He names the job at the cafe, says she's going to need it, and names Friday. Short,
blunt lines. Her mum says one thing, worried, and doesn't follow it up.

**The first choice**, and what she sees after it:

| she says | what she sees |
|---|---|
| she'll get the job today | *Gil nods. Once.* |
| he'll have his money | *He almost smiles. He doesn't believe her.* |
| it's her business, not his | *His face goes hard.* |

Each choice moves Gil's hidden score. **No number is shown.** She only sees the reaction.

## Scene 3 — the kitchen, 07:45: Nate

Nate comes in, hair wet from the shower, and eats standing up. He can't stand her and says so.

**The temptation:** his eyes drop, then snap back up. Too late, she saw it. *He hates me. So why
does he keep looking?*

**A second choice:**

| she does | what she sees |
|---|---|
| smiles at him | *He goes red and walks out.* |
| ignores him | *He grabs his toast and goes without a word.* |
| "Stop staring, then." | *"I wasn't—" The door slams.* |

Each moves Nate's hidden score. The player learns that attraction is in this game, and it's in the
house.

## Scene 4 — the cafe: Owen

The card is in the window. Owen is behind the counter and doesn't smile.

**Owen is strict and a little frightening.** He says what the job is: three dollars, three hours, be
on time, mess up and you're gone.

| she does | what she sees |
|---|---|
| takes the job | *He points at the apron.* The quest card updates |
| asks for more | *"Ask me again in a month."* |
| walks out | *The card stays in the window. She can come back.* |

## Scene 5 — the first shift

The shift is played, not summed up: a tray, a table, a customer.

Then a girl in the same apron grabs her arm by the coffee machine. *"Nell, right?"* Nell never told
her.

She winks and goes back to her table.

## Scene 6 — the hook

She walks out with three dollars. $150 by Friday. Three dollars a shift. And a girl she's never met
knows her name.

Her phone buzzes. A message under last night's stream: *"See you at work tomorrow, Nell."*
