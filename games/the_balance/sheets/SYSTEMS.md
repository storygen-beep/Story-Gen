## Three things this list caught

**1 · Gossip had no source. FIXED 2026-09-14.** Two pages depended on her knowing where people
are and nothing produced it. It's now a system of its own — *talk* — with six rooms that produce it
and a rule that it goes stale and is sometimes wrong.

**2 · Four of these are the ones that make a room matter.** Followers only move on the phone.
Shifts and the move up only move at the cafe. Marks only move in class. Where she stands with the
crowd only moves on the quad and at the row. **Each is fed in one place and checked all over the
game — that's what makes those places worth going back to**, and it's the thing our worst game
didn't have.

**3 · One of those two is settled. 2026-09-14.** *Dares taken* as a number was a second copy of
where she stands with the crowd, and it's gone. What stays is her **position in the dare chain**,
which is a different object — it decides which dare comes next. *Streams done* and *followers*
still go up on the same act, and I'd cut *streams done* unless something needs it on its own.
