## The day

She wakes at **seven**. The game starts at **seven thirty**, in the kitchen. The day ends when she
sleeps, and she can stay up as late as she likes — it costs her the next morning.

## Classes and shifts collide on purpose

```text
              07:00        09:00        12:00        17:00        22:00
  classes                  |========== 9 to 12 =========|   four subjects across the week
  early shift   |=== 7 to 12 ===|                              misses class
  middle shift               |======= 12 to 5 =======|         fits around it
  closing                                  |==== 5 to 10 ====| home after his ten
```

**Three shifts exist and she can't have all of them.** The early one costs her a class. The middle
one fits. Closing gets her home after ten, which is the one rule of Gil's she can't quietly get
around — and it's the shift where Owen is the only other person in the building.

Weekends have no classes, so all three are free, and the house is fuller instead.

## Who's in the house, and when

**This is the building only.** Which room each of them is standing in, hour by hour, is its own
page now — and so is what that costs her.

| | |
|---|---|
| **Gil** | kitchen until eight, back by six, and in the garage late |

So the house is at its quietest **weekday afternoons**, and at its fullest **Sunday**.

## The semester

**Sixteen weeks**, the same sixteen that clear the debt. Exams in week eight, finals in week
sixteen, and then the next semester posts for more.

## Friday

The $150 is handed over in the kitchen on Friday evening. It's the same room the chore list is in,
and Gil is there because he's always there at six.

## Getting anywhere

The bus is **forty minutes and $2 each way.** Walking is free and takes an hour. A lift from Gil
costs nothing and is a favour, which is its own kind of price.
