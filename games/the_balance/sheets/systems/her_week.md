## Sleep, and the alarm

- She goes to bed when she likes and **sets the alarm herself.** Or sets none, and sleeps until she
  wakes.
- **Nothing wakes her early.** Her mum up at 06:45, Nate in the bathroom at seven, a car on the
  street — she sleeps through all of it.
- **Rest comes from the hours she slept, not from the alarm. Eight hours is full: 100.**

| hours | rest |
|---|---|
| **eight or more** | **100** |
| seven | 88 |
| six | 75 |
| five | 63 |
| four | 50 |

  About **12.5 an hour**, and over eight gives nothing.

- **The alarm is set fresh every night.** It never carries over.

So staying up is always allowed and always costs. Bed at midnight, up at seven, she is fine. Bed at
two for the same seven o'clock and she is at 63 all day — **and she was the one who set it.**

## The cafe

- **Open seven in the morning until ten at night, seven days a week.** That is already the hour
  Owen stands on the floor.
- **Five shifts a day, three hours each:**

| | | | | |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **07–10** | **10–13** | **13–16** | **16–19** | **19–22** |

- **The same five every day.** Nothing is nailed to a weekday.
- **One shift a day.**
- **A shift pays $3.** That is the wage, and it is all this page says about money.
- **Nothing counts how many she works.** The pay is the only limit there is.
- **She clocks on at the top of the shift or it's gone.** Owen is exact about the work; he doesn't
  take her at half two.
- **All five are hers from day one**, the late one included.

**What a shift earns on top of the wage is the tips talk, and it is not on this page.** Whether she
can ever make Friday is that conversation, not this one.

## Getting there

**The bus is forty minutes and $2 each way. Walking is free and takes an hour.**

**Home sits in the middle** — campus one way, town the other — so **campus to the cafe is eighty
minutes**, and there is no way across that misses the house.

## What fits in one day

| she does | and then |
|---|---|
| **class, nine to twelve** | home about 12:40 |
| **class, then the 16–19 shift** | in town by four, home at 19:40. It costs her dinner |
| **any other shift** | that day's class is gone |
| **stays home** | chores, her mum, the bathroom, her own door |

**On a class day the 16–19 shift is the only one that fits.** Out at twelve, in town at 13:20 — too
late for the 13:00 one, and he doesn't take her late.
