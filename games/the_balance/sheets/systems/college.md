**Campus measures two things — her marks, and how known she is.** They pull against each other, she
only has hours for one, and the cafe is eating those hours too. This page is the first one.

## Marks

- **Four subjects, four teachers, classes nine to twelve on weekdays.** Nobody chases her for
  missing one — it just goes on the sheet, and the sheet turns into her marks.
- **Attendance and marks are counted per subject**, so she can be fine in one class and sinking in
  another.
- **Attendance doesn't earn her marks — it stops her losing them.** Each subject meets twice a
  week. **Miss one and nothing happens. Miss two and it's noticed. Miss three and the mark can't
  come back on homework alone** — the only way up from there is asking the teacher for something.
  **Which is exactly what Hale is for**, and it is why the lever on her is sleep before it is
  anything else.
- **Her teachers set the homework and grade the exams themselves.** A mark is a person's opinion
  with a number on it, and which person it is matters.
- **Hale teaches one of the four**, and his is the one where being liked changes the number. The
  other three are names on a timetable for now, and any of them can become a real character later.
- **Exams in week eight, and finals in week sixteen, in every subject.** The same sixteen weeks that
  clear the debt.

**And this is what makes it bite:** if she fails, Gil's money was wasted. Nobody has to threaten
her with anything — the tuition and her marks are the same object.

⚠️ **A warning after bad exams goes to the house.** That's the letter that can tell her mum
what was paid and by whom. **Failing college is the cheapest way in the game to lose the secret.**
