## The one call on this page

**Everything lives on her, not on each person.** She has three, and everybody in the game reads them.
The other way is to give all eight their own set.

**Why hers:** she's the one being changed, and a door at the cafe and a door at home should open
off the same climb. Give each person their own and the game becomes eight separate climbs that
never touch, and nothing ever changes her — the game just runs eight tabs.

**If you want it the other way, now is the moment.** After this, every gate in the game hangs off
this answer.

## The three

| | what it means | what it's for |
|---|---|---|
| **Exposure** | how far out in the open she's willing to be, and how much of her has been seen | opens places and hours — the door left open, the room with people in it |
| **Corruption** | what she'll take, and whether she's the one asking | opens acts, and eventually she starts them |
| **Reputation** | how much of the campus has heard of her | brings people to her. It almost never says no — it delivers |

**All three show as words, not scores.** A number invites farming, and these aren't a scoreboard.

**Five steps each, and the same five numbers underneath all three: 5 · 20 · 40 · 65 · 85.** She
starts at 5 on all three.

| | 5 | 20 | 40 | 65 | 85 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Exposure** | nobody has seen her face | a few strangers have | people on campus place her | somebody she knows has seen it | she can't be casual anywhere |
| **Corruption** | she wouldn't | she might | she has, once | she does | she's the one asking |
| **Reputation** | nobody knows her | they know the name | she gets spoken to | she gets asked | she's the one people arrange themselves around |

**The steps get further apart on purpose.** The first move is cheap and the last one is a season's
work, so the game opens fast and never runs out of room.

**Exposure carries the price as well as the nerve.** The more of her has been out where it can be
seen — her face on the stream, her room, the campus hoodie, the toilet at work — the more people can
place her. That isn't only a punishment: being recognized opens things a hidden stream can never
reach, and it closes being casual anywhere on campus. **It only climbs.** There's nothing she can do
to become unseen again.

**Followers stay on the phone**, where she can see the count. They're the same idea pointed
outward — how many are watching — so there's no second meter for it here.

## The two she has to look after

| | falls | fills | what it shuts |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Rest** | every day | her own bed | low, she misses the nine o'clock — and attendance is what Hale runs on |
| **Clean** | every day | the bathroom, the gym showers, the laundry | low, she won't be looked at. Nothing on exposure opens, and **she will not go live** |

**One bathroom, shared with Nate and Tasha**, and the hours it is free are the hours she is meant
to be somewhere else. *Clean* is the meter with something standing in its way, and the house page
is where that is written.

No battery on the phone. No hunger, no money-per-meal, nothing else to top up.

## What this page does not decide

Which door each step opens — that's the places and scenes steps · what anybody wants from her · how
fast any of it moves · what the sidebar looks like.

## What it hands to the steps after it

- every place and person now needs to say which of the three it reads, and at what step
- the five steps are named. **What is not decided is what each one opens** — that belongs to the
  rooms, one at a time
- exposure has to be *met* somewhere — the first person who recognizes her is unwritten
- reputation is the only one that delivers people, so the campus has to have people to deliver
