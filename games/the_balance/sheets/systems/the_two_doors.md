## The two doors

| | when it's open | what's behind it | who knows she was there |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Nate's room** | evenings and the odd hours he keeps. His door isn't always shut | him, on his own | **he does, every time** |
| **The master bedroom** | the nights her mum is home off the ward, before ten | **Gil and her mum** | **nobody** |

## How she knows when

**There is no gossip at home.** Campus has to tell her where people are — the house doesn't, because
she lives in it.

- **Her mum's shift times are on the wall in that bedroom.** She has read them a hundred times for
  ordinary reasons. Now they are a timetable for something else.
- **Nate's door not always being shut** is the whole invitation. Nothing announces it.

**That's the contrast worth keeping:** on campus she has to be told. At home she already knows, and
knowing is what makes it hers.

## Nate's door — the cost comes first

**He knows, every single time.** That is the difference between this door and every other one in the
game.

- **The first thing it buys her is a worse week.** He says no by getting worse: he tells Gil about
  the chores, he is louder on the landing, and he is still in the next room tomorrow.
- **Then it cracks.** He can't stand her and he can't stop looking, and being walked in on is the
  first thing that makes him show it. His climb is the biggest one in the house and this door is
  where it starts.
- **The right shape for the biggest climb is to cost before it pays.** If it paid first, nothing in
  the house would be worth the risk.

## The master bedroom — she saw, and he doesn't know

- **Gil never opens a step. She opens all of them** — and until now nothing gave her a way to open
  one. This is it.
- **She watched her step dad, and he has no idea.** It is the first thing in the game she holds over
  anybody, and holding it is a move in itself. She can sit at the kitchen table on Friday with it.
- **Her mum is in the room.** That is the whole weight of the door: the one person she can't tell,
  and can't stop taking from.

**How far this door goes is Gil.** The top of it is her in that room with him. Where her mum goes
is her own ladder, block 5, and it isn't decided here.

**Does he ever find out she watched?** My lean, and it is only a lean: **not in this build** — and
when he does, **she tells him.** He is the man who never makes the first move, so him catching her
would hand him a move the whole design says he doesn't have.

## What it costs her

- **Watching costs the hour and nothing else.** Same as everywhere.
- **Being caught at home does not move exposure.** Exposure is strangers knowing things. At home it
  is one person with a name, and what it moves is where she stands with *him* — which is the harder
  currency, because he is at breakfast.

## The first build — one step each

- **Nate's is the first walk-in in the whole game, and it is written for her.** She opens the door,
  she sees, she leaves, and no choice is offered. It teaches what this is and it makes the second
  one mean something.
- **The master bedroom is the first one she gets a choice in**, and at the start the choice is back
  out or stand there. Watching properly isn't on the menu yet — she can't make herself.
- Everything above those two waits.
