## What a dare is

**Jules says it out loud, on the quad, in front of people.** Not a message and not a job she picks
up off a list. It happens to her while she is standing there.

- **She answers on the spot.** Yes or no, with everyone watching. No going away to think about it —
  that is what makes it a decision instead of a button.
- **Given on the quad, done somewhere else, the same day.** The dare is what walks her into a room
  she would otherwise never have a reason to enter.
- **Bree does the laughing while Jules watches.** Jules never raises her voice, which is why saying
  no to her is hard in a way that saying no to Bree would not be.

## The first two

| | what she is asked to do | what it costs her |
|---|---|---|
| **One** | say something stupid to a stranger at the union, while the three of them watch from a table | her face, and nothing else. It is over in a minute |
| **Two** | the jacket goes in her bag on the quad, and she stays like that until she goes home | the rest of the day. It is the first one that does not end when it ends |

**That gap is the climb.** The first is a minute. The second is four hours of being looked at, and
she agreed to all four of them in the two seconds she had to answer.

**Two is shut until one is done.** It is a chain, not a number she can reach some other way.

## Saying no

- **She can always say no, and no door shuts.** Nothing on this page is ever the only way through
  anything.
- **What it costs:** the laughing gets louder for a while, and the same dare comes back about a week
  later, in front of more people.
- **No is written as loudly as yes.** If the page only writes what happens when she does it, it was
  never a choice.

## What a dare actually buys her

**The week after a dare, the random picking-on goes quiet.** Strangers stop. Nobody says why, and
nobody announces it — she just gets a week where the day is only a day. Then it starts again.

**That is the whole payoff in the first build, and it is on purpose.** The row, the parties and
being let in are all waiting, so the only thing a dare can honestly pay in four weeks is the one
thing that is already running: *being picked on*, turned down for a week.

**Two things come with it for free:**

- She is visibly further than she was last week, and the next dare is worse.
- **Bree and Paige are in Nate's year**, so what she does on the quad is at the dinner table that
  night. A dare reaches the house without anyone writing a new room for it.

## What it writes

| | |
|---|---|
| **Which dares she has done** | a position in a chain, not a tally. It is what decides which one comes next |
| **Where she stands with the crowd** | every dare done, and every dare turned down |
| **Exposure** | dare two only, because dare two is the first one about her body |
| **Corruption** | the body ones only, so it stays the same meter that the walk-ins move |

**This settles one of the two things flagged as counted twice.** *Dares taken* as a number was a
second copy of where she stands with the crowd, and it goes. A position in a chain is not the same
object and it stays.
