## Being known

Earned on the quad, spent at the row. **Two crowds, and she starts outside both.**

| | what it asks | what it gives |
|---|---|---|
| **The crowd** | say yes to things, in front of people | invitations, the row, and being someone |
| **The serious ones** | hours in the library | her teachers' approval, and marks that hold up |

### Jules runs the crowd, and Jules is the one making her small

**One person, both jobs, and that's what makes this work.** She and her two — Bree and Paige — make Nell the joke
on the quad — nothing violent, just small, in front of everybody. Then the joke turns into a
challenge: *do this and we'll stop laughing.*

**So being pushed around and being let in are the same channel.** Every dare is a humiliation and an
invitation at once, and the player can't take one without the other.

**The dares have their own page now** — what the first two actually are, what saying no costs, and
the one thing a dare buys in the first build.

**Three rules, or it's just cruelty:**

1. **Every round visibly moves her closer.** If it only costs, the player stops playing.
2. **She can always walk away — and it costs standing.** That's what makes saying yes a decision
   instead of a button.
3. **Getting in doesn't end it.** The dares carry on, they just come from inside the circle now, and
   she's the one at the edge watching them start on somebody else.

**Bree and Paige are in Nate's year**, so the sneering on campus and his behaviour at home are one
thing rather than two.

**And Jules ends up a target herself.** That's the payoff for what she put Nell through, and it's the
only thread in the game between two women.

### Why Nell is out wandering at all

**Somebody has to tell her where people are**, or she goes to class and goes home and nothing on
this page ever happens. That's its own system now — *talk* — and the parties are one of the things
it points at.

## The three things worth doing

| | what it costs | what it moves |
|---|---|---|
| **Parties at the row** | a night, and being home after ten | being known, fast, and dares are how it climbs |
| **The gym** | an hour, and she comes out needing a shower | being looked at while doing something ordinary |
| **Posing for the art class** | an afternoon | being looked at **on purpose, by people who are allowed to.** Pays nothing but standing |

**No sign-up sheets, no clubs, no study group.** If it doesn't put her in front of somebody, it
isn't in the game.
