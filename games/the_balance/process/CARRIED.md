## 12 · The three arcs — block 5

An arc is a numbered ladder of one-time steps, each gated on the flag the one before it set, whose
**last step converts the act into something she can simply do**. Across this repo: 1,396 canvases,
and no character anywhere has a second thing that happens — every hub is authored in its converted
state on day one. Assume you are doing it.

**Nate — the door.** Walks in on him and backs straight out → walks in again and does not back out as
fast → stays in the doorway and he does not stop → she is why he is doing it → **converts:** she can
simply go in.

**Lynn and Gil — the master bedroom.** The door is not shut and she sees → she stays longer than she
should → Gil works out she watched → the door starts being left open and nobody says anything. Live
only on **Lynn's nights off**, which is what gives the week its shape.

**The cafe — the floor.** A hand on her on a busy Friday → she does not move away → extra, for money,
from customers → extra for Owen, for the schedule → **converts:** it is part of the shift.

**A promotion must buy doors, not a raise:** floor → counter → closing shifts → **her own keys** →
she opens alone. A promotion worth $2 an hour is the accumulation ladder that is measured as the
*male*-protagonist shape.

⚠️ **She can say no, and the no goes somewhere.** The field puts a real refusal on one click in fifty
and 79% of them lead somewhere the yes does not. Slapping a hand away is a path — the regulars talk,
Owen notices — not a dead end. A yes means nothing if the no was decorative.

## 13 · The deleted start choice — DEAD, recorded so it is not re-invented

Three flags — `past_camera` / `past_counter` / `past_quiet` — set by Tasha asking what she did all
summer. **Deleted 2026-09-11 at LO's call.** Do not rebuild it.

**Why:** it was three difficulty settings wearing a costume — the cam opens sooner, better shifts,
less noticed. And the number it was sold on does not support it. *Freedom is 25.9% of why players
love these games, premise is 0%* — but the quote behind that figure is *"farmer, slave merchant,
bounty hunter, cage fighter, cannibal? You can be whatever you want."* **That is freedom during
play, not a three-way choice at minute zero**, and this game already has it: six ways to earn, four
meters, 29 places, three ladders climbable in any order.

**What remains:** the creation screen still asks *what Gil is to her* and *what Nate is to her*.
That is the player defining the household, which in this genre is the setting.

**Consequence, declared:** the gate *"the start choice is read"* reports **n/a** forever, which the
format says is not a pass. Correct — a declared absence, not an oversight.
