# The Want — The Balance

> Everyone in this game is eighteen or older. Nell is eighteen and has started college.

**Six things. Nothing here is a number, because if the game can count it, it is a system.**
What was cut, and which block now owns it: `process/CARRIED.md`.

---

## 1 · Who she is

**Nell, 18.** Her parents divorced. Her mother married Gil three years ago and the house came with
him, with his son Nate, 20, and his daughter Tasha, 21. College started this week.

**Gil paid her tuition. She pays him back weekly, and it is most of an honest week's work.**

**It never finishes** — the next semester posts.

**Lynn does not know he paid.**

What she has to lose: nobody in that house has revised their picture of her since she was fifteen.

## 2 · The appetite

**To be looked at by people who were not supposed to be looking, and to be the one who decided they
would.**

It cannot complete. Every room she gets into has a room behind it.

## 3 · The world

**A college town.** The house on the near side, the campus across town, the strip downtown where the
cafe is.

## 4 · The charge

**Taboo** — the household. Legally family, three years in, and the debt is what makes it move.

**Transformation** — she becomes someone the girl who filled in the forms in August would not
recognize, and the college is what does it, not the house.

Both, deliberately. Taboo drives the house, transformation drives the campus.

## 5 · The seven people

| | why she wants them, or why being wanted by them lands |
|---|---|
| **Gil** | he paid for her and has never once mentioned it, and she cannot work out whether that is decency or a debt she has not been shown the size of. Strict, and he does not drink |
| **Nate** | a senior at her college who was fine before she moved in and has never pretended otherwise. He cannot stand her, and underneath it he wants her, and hiding that is the only thing he is any good at |
| **Tasha** | two years ahead and already does all of it, without apology. She is what the player is being shown she could be |
| **Lynn** | not wanted — **she is the price tag.** Every hour Gil is not in the kitchen is an hour Lynn is on a ward, and that is what makes it cost |
| **Owen** | he decides what her week is worth, in hours, and he is the first person to want something from her that was not free |
| **Hale** | the one man whose approval is worth something on paper, and the only person on that campus who can hand her money that is not a shift |
| **Reyes** | he is the consequence. Strict, and he does not want anything from her, which is what makes him different from every other man in the game |
