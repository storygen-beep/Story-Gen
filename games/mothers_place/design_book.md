# Mother's Place — Design Book

> The user's review surface. This is **intent in plain language** — the engine/TOML is the faithful
> translation of what's written here. Grown one section per pipeline step.
>
> Working title: **Mother's Place** (double meaning: the role she takes, and the bed). Rename freely.

---

## World setup

**POV.** Female PC. Cascade-native: she corrupts *herself* — her own duty, her own shame — before she
corrupts the household. The reversal is the spine: **dependent returnee → the matriarch the family answers to.**

**The fantasy (clears the 3-part bar).**
> You come home with nothing — broke, jobless, your life in pieces — to the house your mother walked out of
> a year ago. The place is rotting without her: your father drinking through the quiet, your older brother
> holding it together with both fists and hating every minute, your kid sister going soft then wild. They
> need a woman in this house. You start by just filling in — cooking, smoothing things over, being needed.
> And somewhere in there you feel it: the *power* in being the one they all want, the one they can't do
> without. So you take it. Her place at the stove, then at the head of the table, then in the bed — your
> father first, then your brother, then your sister — until the house doesn't run on her memory anymore. It
> runs on you. And when she finally comes crawling back, she'll find her family belongs to you.

- **POV-fit** ✓ — female-PC self-corruption → becoming-the-one-in-power (matriarch). The reversal is
  **powerless returnee → the one everyone orbits and obeys.**
- **Sharp charge** ✓ — three at once: a **reversal** (came home with nothing → owns the house and everyone
  in it), a **taboo** (blood incest — father, brother, sister — plus literally *taking the mother's place*:
  her kitchen, her bed, her husband, her children), and a **transformation** (the dutiful caretaker who
  discovers being-wanted is power and weaponizes it). The specific hook that beats every other
  family-corruption game: it isn't "seduce your relatives," it's **"fill the vacancy your mother left and
  become her — better than her — until the family is yours,"** with the mother's eventual return as the
  thing the whole game has been building toward.
- **Built-in two-act shape** ✓ — **Act 1 (fill the hole):** caretaker → seductress. Take over the running
  of the house; corrupt the father (the tender, tangled first), then the brother (a power struggle won in
  bed), then pull the sister down with you (grooming the protégé). **Act 2 (rule it):** you ARE the woman of
  the house now — the family runs on you, the corruption deepens and *combines* (two at once, the house as
  your domain) — and **Lynn comes home** to find she's been replaced. The final conquest is putting your
  mother in her place.

**Desire span (declared, not stumbled into).**
- **Targets:** the blood family — **father Roy + older brother Wes (men), younger sister Maddie (woman)** =
  both genders, all incest. **Act 2 may add the mother Lynn** (woman; conquest/displacement capstone) and
  possibly an uncle. The span is family, by blood, on purpose.
- **Flavor across acts:** opens **needy + tangled-tender** — she has nothing, and *being needed* is the
  first hit; with Roy there's real grieving-husband warmth knotted into it. It escalates to **control /
  conquest (break-and-own)** as she becomes the matriarch. With **Wes** it's a **power struggle** (friction
  → he submits in bed). With **Maddie** it's **corruption / grooming** (she drags her kid sister down the
  same road she went). With **Lynn** (Act 2) it's **pure displacement** — taking what was hers and making
  her watch.

**Premise (what the player starts inside).** You're in your mid-twenties and your life just collapsed — job
gone, money gone, nowhere to land. So you go back to the house you grew up in. Your mother (**Lynn**) ran off
with another man about a year ago; no calls, no address. The house has been coming apart since: your father
(**Roy**) runs a failing shop and drinks the evenings away, your older brother (**Wes**) has been holding the
family together and resents that it fell on him, your little sister (**Maddie**), just eighteen, drifts
between sullen and reckless. You move back into your old room. The family needs a woman. You start by filling
in — and filling in becomes taking over.

**Player.** **Customizable** — name + appearance (default name **Cora**). A self-insert corruption fantasy
wants the player in the seat, and the engine's customization system carries it. (The wiring is authoring;
the *decision* here is: named-default, fully customizable name + look.)

**Systems in use (scope only — wiring decided at authoring):**
- **Phone — YES.** The family group chat, late-night one-to-ones, sneaking and sending photos, the
  escalation threads that run on real arc flags. Incest lives in secret channels; the phone is one.
- **Clothing — YES.** Dressing *for* the family; wearing **Lynn's left-behind clothes** (charged — literally
  putting on the mother); exhibitionism in a cramped house with one bathroom and thin walls; public
  reactions in town. The reactive world (Step 2) rides clothing.
- **Rent — NO.** She lives at home. Money still matters — the household is broke and she has to earn — but
  that's the plain money economy + flags, not a landlord/eviction system.
- **Customization — YES.** Name + look (see Player).

## Locations (the map — creative geography only; structured at Step 2b)

The **house** is the heart (the household sandbox); a small **town floor** around it gives the map somewhere
to earn, to be seen, and to run into the father's world.

**The house** (the childhood home — working-class, a little run-down):
- **Cora's room** — her old bedroom. Base/hub; where the day resets.
- **The kitchen** — Lynn's old domain. Symbolically charged; where "filling in" starts.
- **The living room** — the family common space; the TV, where Roy drinks.
- **The master bedroom** — Roy's room, Lynn's old bed. The symbolic prize.
- **The garage / Wes's space** — the brother's territory; he works on cars, half-lives out there.
- **Maddie's room** — the sister's space.
- **The bathroom** — one shared bathroom. Thin walls, no lock that holds. The peep/occupancy mechanic lives
  here.
- **The backyard / porch** — the private outdoor spot; smoking, the quiet talks.

**The town floor:**
- **Roy's shop** — the father's failing business. His world; a money/economy node; maybe Wes works there
  too.
- **The bar (Cora's work)** — a bar in town where she takes a job to earn (the feeder economy — money + the
  corruption/exhibitionism the work feeds).
- **The neighborhood** — the connective strip: the street, a corner store, a nosy neighbor's porch. Where
  clothing/exhibitionism gets seen in public.

> Tree (rough, pre-map-design): `loc_home` → cora_room · kitchen · living_room · master_bedroom · garage ·
> maddie_room · bathroom · backyard; `loc_town` → roys_shop · the_bar · neighborhood. Topology + access
> locks + naming + travel-friction are Step 2b's job.

## Top-level design — the engine, economy & machine (Step 2)

### 1. The cascade + the double lock
**You must corrupt yourself before you can corrupt the family.** Cora's own corruption is the master key:
every *incest* beat stays locked until she's fallen far enough. She corrupts herself by **filling in** (the
caretaker role getting charged), **the bar** (dressing down for tips), and **the house's exposure economy**
(one bathroom, thin walls — being seen, then wanting to be). Caretaking and befriending the family is **NOT
corruption-gated** — it's how she raises each person's lock in Act 1, in parallel with falling herself.

**The DOUBLE LOCK on every lewd family beat:**
1. **Cora's `corruption` ≥ the tier for that KIND of act** — the *door* ("am I far enough gone to do this
   with my own father?"). Opens for the whole family at once; built by her self-corruption feeders.
2. **That family member's own trait ≥ the rung's threshold** — the *individual lock* ("is HE/SHE ready?").
   Built by interacting with that person:
   - **Roy** (father) — **`relation`** (his dependence on her as the woman who fills Lynn's place) + his own
     `arousal`. Slow-burn, tender; the lock is *attachment*.
   - **Wes** (brother) — **`relation`**, but the build runs *through friction* (a power struggle won, not a
     warmth accrued). The lock is *who runs this house*.
   - **Maddie** (sister) — **her own `corruption`**, which Cora raises by grooming her. This is the cascade
     literally extending: Cora falls, then pulls Maddie down. The lock is *the protégé's own fall*.
   - **Lynn** (mother, Act 2) — `relation`/displacement; designed at her late on-ramp.

The convergence: by the time Cora's corruption opens the incest door, the family she's been caretaking is
already primed. **Non-lewd interaction is always reachable** (she lives there); **lewd is hard-gated**
(absent/locked until both locks clear — when low, "this isn't in the picture").

### 2. The stat set (each leg gates real content — no dead stats)
Four player legs, each owning one job:
- **`corruption`** (built-in, always-on) — *the incest door.* Gates every family lewd tier + the
  embracing-the-role solo content. The cascade.
- **`money`** (built-in, always-on) — *the broke-household pressure + purchases.* The house is failing; food
  and bills and clothing all cost; the better money is down the lewd path (§5). Drives the fall.
- **`energy`** (built-in, always-on) — *paces the day.* Spent via `costs`, restored by sleep.
- **`exhibitionism`** (built-in) — *the being-seen domain.* This game is thick with it: the one-bathroom
  house, the bar floor, public clothing reactions. Gates the voyeur/exposure ladder (§3, §4).

**Cut on purpose:** `fitness` (no body-grind — "outshining Lynn" is carried by clothing's `worn_beauty` +
the matriarch arc, not a stat), `intelligence` (no academic domain), `charisma`/`social` (the family engages
her because she lives there — no social door), no career stat (the bar ladder is carried by `corruption` +
the income ladder). **`beauty` is not a leg** — it's derived read-only from worn clothing.

### 3. The desire ladder (the chain of named wants — the player-facing spine)
The meter is backstage; **desire is onstage.** Each rung is a concrete *want*; pursuing it raises the meters
that open the next. The active "Story Goals" card always names the current want **+ the next action with
PLACE + TIME + REQUIREMENT.**

- **W0 — "Get back on your feet."** *(cold start)* Land home broke. Find work (the bar, daytime) and start
  making yourself useful (the kitchen's a wreck). → teaches the loop + the income path.
- **W1 — "Become the woman of the house."** Fill the vacancy — cook, clean, smooth the fights. Raises every
  family `relation` (non-lewd). **Payoff:** they start to *rely* on you; Roy: "God, you're just like your
  mother." → unlocks the realization that *being needed is power.*
- **W2 — "Stop hiding."** Self-corruption feeders: dress down at the bar for tips; the house exposure
  economy (the bathroom, being glimpsed). Raises `corruption` + `exhibitionism`. **Payoff:** you catch a
  family member looking — and you *like* it. → the door cracks.
- **W3 — "Take Dad."** *(first incest capstone — Roy.)* `corruption` door + Roy's `relation` high. Become
  the grieving man's comfort → his wife-replacement → his bed. **Payoff:** the first incest capstone; you've
  taken Lynn's place. Sets the master flag the later arcs ride.
- **W4 — "Put Wes in his place."** *(brother.)* The power struggle — he resents you running *his* house.
  Turn it. **Payoff:** the friction breaks into sex; he submits. *(Cross-gated on W3 — telegraphed.)*
- **W5 — "Bring Maddie in."** *(sister, protégé.)* Groom her; pull her down the road you walked.
  **Payoff:** Maddie joins the fall — your first *recruit*. *(Cross-gated on Cora's corruption + an ambient
  where Maddie clocks you.)*
- **W6 — "Run the house."** *(Act 2.)* You're the matriarch. Combined beats (two at once), the house as your
  domain, deepening repeatable loops; the reactive world peaks.
- **W7 — "When she comes home."** *(frontier.)* Lynn returns to find she's been replaced. The displacement
  capstone — the family chooses you over her. Then drop to the livable plateau (§7).

### 4. The reactive world (clothing-driven)
The world reacts to **what she's WEARING** (`worn_corruption`), never a hidden number — for legibility +
player control. Every tone shift goes **lewder / bolder / more predatory, never warmer.**
- **Places × ceilings:** **the house** — family glances scaled to outfit, disposition-gated (Roy flustered →
  hungry as his arc opens; Wes banks it as leverage/friction; Maddie watches and mirrors). **The bar** — a
  semi-lawless public floor: stares → groping-in-passing → cornered as she dresses down (the exhibitionism +
  income feeder). **The neighborhood** — civilized public, caps at stares/comments (the nosy neighbor).
- **The charged clothing thread:** **Lynn's left-behind clothes.** Wearing the mother's dress triggers
  family reactions no store-bought outfit does ("…is that your mother's?") — a special exposure item that
  *is* taking her place, worn.
- **Three modes:** *sought* (she dressed for it) · *choice* (refuse/accept) · **forced** (no branch).
  **Forced is ACT-SCOPED** — prey early (a drunk corners her at the bar when she's powerless), recedes as
  power rises (by Act 2 she's the predator). Gated on the power tier, not place×exposure alone.
- **Engine truth:** gate on `worn_corruption`; author as **Lane 2/3** canvases — the PUBLIC/ambient content
  clothing is *allowed* to gate. **Clothing NEVER gates a family arc's spine** (those are the double lock).
  Forced = auto-fire capstone-shape canvas (`priority ≥ 9`, single Continue). Per-place ceiling =
  per-canvas `conditions`. Progression = access to the clothing (bought/unlocked), not a trait.

### 5. The economy (a corruption ladder, anti-grind)
**One wallet.** Income runs legit-low → lewd-high, so **earning money and corrupting herself are the same
act**, and broke-pressure pulls her down the lewd path *because that's where the money is.*
- **The bar (the ladder):** regular shifts (low) → dress revealing / flash for tips (mid) → backroom
  (high). Every paying rung IS a reactive-world/lewd scene, never a chore-click.
- **Other paths (anti-grind):** **the phone** (sell photos — a secondary lewd income, escalating with
  corruption); **Roy's shop** (legit low-pay help — the honest alternative when she won't go lewd that day).
- **Sinks (pressure via desire):** **the household** (food + bills — being the woman of the house means
  *paying* for it; a recurring money event, NOT the rent system); **clothing** (the reactive-world dial costs
  money); **gifts** (raise a family member's relation / spoil Maddie).
- **Late-act pressure escalates in FORM:** early = keep the household afloat (survival); late = **Roy's shop
  facing foreclosure** — a money threat the matriarch must solve (or let die and take control another way).
  Pressure is constant; its shape grows.

### 6. The machine (cross-wiring — core loop + wires)
**The core loop (the spine):** *caretaking → being-needed (relation + the dawning sense of power) →
self-corruption opens the door → take a family member → that conquest deepens your hold on the house → which
opens the next member + raises the household → matriarch.* The economic circuit underneath: *broke-pressure →
the bar (corruption-ladder income) → money funds clothing (the reactive dial) + the household (keeps the
family fed = relation held) → which fuels the arcs.*

Every core NPC has a place:
- **Roy** = the **foundation conquest.** Taking him = taking Lynn's place = the base of the matriarch role.
  His capstone sets the master player flag **`took_dads_place`**.
- **Wes** = the **power-struggle conquest.** *(Form 1 — arc→arc depth gate.)* His late rung (full
  submission) is gated on **`took_dads_place`**: *"Wes won't bend while he still defers to Dad's word — once
  YOU are the authority in this house, he's got no ground to stand on."* (Milestone → player flag.) His
  *entry* (friction, talk) is ungated (D1).
- **Maddie** = the **protégé / first recruit.** *(Form 1.)* Her grooming rungs gated on Cora's `corruption`
  (the door) + a flag **`maddie_clocked_you`** set by an earlier ambient (she catches Cora at something).
  Her *entry* (sister talk) is ungated (D1).
- **The economy** = **Form 2a** connective tissue: bar money gates the clothing that drives the reactive
  world and keeps the household fed (relation). **Form 2b** flourish: a banded payout where a family member's
  trait bands a tip/gift outcome (authored as band-gated sibling choices at blueprint).
- **Lynn** = a **late-act on-ramp** (her schedule begins in Act 2 — the staggered-entry pattern, not a D1
  violation). The frontier capstone.

**Disciplines:** **D1** — no arc's *entry* is cross-gated (every family member is cold-start talkable; only
mid/late lewd rungs carry cross-gates). **D2** — the F1 wires form a DAG (`took_dads_place` → Wes-late;
`corruption` + `maddie_clocked_you` → Maddie-grooming; Roy depends on no one). **D3** — every cross-gate is a
locked-visible telegraph naming the other arc's state (see Wes above). *(The full `## The machine` block is
finalized at Step 5 as the synthesis of every arc's wiring; the core loop + nodes are fixed here.)*

### 7. Pacing & frontier
- **Pacing = climb → plateau → climb.** Alternate big beats (capstones) and small (ambients, bar shifts,
  charged glances). Don't dump incest early — it's double-locked. Cap the gap between payoffs (a near payoff
  always visible: a locked-visible next rung).
- **The endgame escalates in CONTENT, not management** — Act 2 is the *hottest* beats (combined family
  scenes, the household as domain, Lynn's displacement), never a +income dashboard.
- **The frontier (W7's three jobs):** (1) **payoff at the charge ceiling** — Lynn comes home and the family
  chooses Cora; (2) **drop to a livable steady-state** — every repeatable loop (each family member, combined
  scenes, the bar, the reactive world) stays playable; (3) **a greyed next-hook seed** — *"Uncle Gary's
  coming to stay"* / *"word's getting around the neighborhood about your house."* The tracker says so
  honestly: *"You've taken the house — run your family. More to come."* **Local arc endings kept** (each
  member's terminal capstone ends a thread); **no hard game-ending.**

### 8. The opening / systems / fail-state (§8 declarations)
- **The opening / cold start.** First screen: Cora steps off the bus onto the porch of her childhood home,
  duffel in hand — inside, Roy half-drunk at the TV, Wes wary in the garage, Maddie shut in her room; the
  house gone to seed without a woman in it for a year. **Immediately she can:** (1) take over the kitchen /
  start cleaning — the *filling-in* nudge (down without grinding); (2) talk to each family member (build
  relation); (3) head into town for work (the bar — money). **First want:** *"Get back on your feet — and
  find your place in this house."* **Teaches with no tutorial:** the wrecked house + empty fridge + the
  family's neediness make the loop obvious; the first charged line (*"you're just like your mother"*) and
  Lynn's clothes still hanging in the master closet preview the charge. **10-min taste:** a new player leaves
  knowing this is about coming home, filling the absent mother's role, and the dawning pull that the family
  *wants* you — the charge previewed, not handed over. **Customization speak-back:** a creator at the start
  (name + look, default **Cora**); the family speaks her name (`@player`) and her look feeds descriptions
  (`@player.<field>`) — tokens, never baked into labels.
- **Systems in play:** **Phone — ON** · **Clothing — ON** · **Customization — ON** · **Rent-debt clock —
  OFF** (no landlord; the household money pressure is the plain `money` trait + a recurring groceries/bills
  *event*, not the rent system).
- **Fail-state — SOFT, real, recoverable (chosen, not defaulted).** Two teeth that actually bite: (1)
  **broke** — if money hits zero and the groceries/bills event lapses, a *lean-household* state cools every
  family `relation` and makes the day harder until she earns her way out; (2) **recklessness** — escalating a
  family beat in the wrong place/time (a sibling can walk in — the occupancy mechanic) triggers an exposure
  setback (a relation hit + a cooldown). **No game-over, no decay death-spiral** — forward is the dominant
  direction; the friction is real but the household is a power fantasy, not a punishment box.

### Decisions (resolved — Step 2 locked)
- **Stats:** `corruption`, `money`, `energy`, `exhibitionism` (4 legs). No fitness/intelligence/charisma/
  career; beauty derived from clothing.
- **Double lock:** Cora's corruption (door) + per-member trait (Roy `relation`, Wes `relation`-via-friction,
  Maddie own-`corruption`). Non-lewd ungated.
- **Economy:** bar (legit→lewd ladder) + phone pics + Roy's shop; sinks = household/clothing/gifts; late
  pressure = the shop's foreclosure. One wallet. Rent system OFF.
- **Machine:** core loop fixed; `took_dads_place` gates Wes-late; `corruption`+`maddie_clocked_you` gate
  Maddie-grooming; economy = Form 2a tissue. DAG holds.
- **Frontier:** Lynn's return capstone → livable plateau → greyed Uncle-Gary/neighborhood hook. No
  game-ending.
- **Systems:** phone/clothing/customization ON; rent OFF. **Fail-state:** soft + real + recoverable.

## Spatial graph & location model (Step 2b)

### Topology — nested-zones, two roots (Home + Town)
The premise is home-anchored with a small feeder town, so the map is **nested-zones with two roots** — the
genre floor (a private unit + a public layer), sized to a tiny cast. **Home** (the house) is the heart;
**Town** is the feeder floor where she earns, gets seen, and crosses Roy's world. The two are **separate
roots bridged by a walk activity** (no `entry_from` between them) — household NPCs (Roy, Wes, Maddie) live
*inside* the home; bar patrons and the nosy neighbor belong to the *town* layer, never the house. A flat
one-storey house = one home hub → rooms (shallow nav, no dead container levels).

### The spatial graph
```
ROOT: Home  (loc_home — the house; central hub, you stand in the hall)
  ├─ Your Room        (loc_cora_room)      — base; sleep / day-reset
  ├─ Kitchen          (loc_kitchen)        — Lynn's old domain
  ├─ Living Room      (loc_living_room)    — the family common space
  ├─ Master Bedroom   (loc_master_bedroom) — Roy's room / Lynn's bed / Lynn's closet
  ├─ Garage           (loc_garage)         — Wes's territory
  ├─ Maddie's Room    (loc_maddie_room)    — the sister's space
  ├─ Bathroom         (loc_bathroom)       — the ONE shared bathroom
  └─ Backyard         (loc_backyard)       — the private outdoor spot

ROOT: Town  (loc_town — the main street / neighborhood; public hub)
  ├─ Roy's Shop       (loc_roys_shop)      — the father's failing business
  ├─ Bar              (loc_bar)            — Cora's work (the income ladder)
  └─ Corner Store     (loc_corner_store)   — groceries + cheap clothes (the sinks)

BRIDGE: walk Home ⇄ Town  (a walk activity, not an entry_from link)
```

### Each location's dramatic job + access (the room-content floor — no dead rooms)
**Home (all reachable** — she lives here; lewd content is gated by the double lock / occupancy, never by
locking the door):
- **loc_home** — the connective hub + a *barometer of her takeover* (the house goes from gone-to-seed →
  running-on-her as Act 2 lands). Day-reset anchor.
- **loc_cora_room** — *so the player can* sleep/reset, do solo feeders (try on clothes, the mirror/creator,
  masturbate, the phone), plan. Base.
- **loc_kitchen** — *so the player can* cook (the **filling-in** activity — the W1 relation engine), eat,
  and get caught/charged while doing it (Roy/Wes drift in). Heavy use; Lynn's-domain symbolism.
- **loc_living_room** — *so the player can* sit with Roy while he drinks (the comfort-Roy arc), watch TV in
  proximity, and run combined-family beats in Act 2.
- **loc_master_bedroom** — *so the player can* reach the Roy capstone (Lynn's bed), open **Lynn's closet**
  (the charged clothing thread), and in Act 2 make her bed yours. The symbolic prize.
- **loc_garage** — *so the player can* work the **Wes** arc (friction → seduction) where he half-lives.
- **loc_maddie_room** — *so the player can* run the **Maddie** grooming arc.
- **loc_bathroom** — *so the player can* shower (hygiene solo) and trade in the **exposure/occupancy**
  economy (catch / be caught / peep — the one-bathroom, thin-walls mechanic; `npc_at_location`).
- **loc_backyard** — *so the player can* have the quiet outdoor talks (a cigarette with Wes, Roy confessing
  about Lynn) — a private-but-outside register distinct from the bedrooms. (Lighter, but earns ≥1 canvas.)

**Town (all reachable):**
- **loc_town** — the public hub; where clothing/exhibition gets **seen in public** (the neighbor, passers-by;
  Lane 2/3 reactive world capped at stares/comments) + the walk bridge anchor.
- **loc_roys_shop** — *so the player can* help Roy for legit low pay (the honest income path) + run Roy-arc
  beats in his world; the late-act **foreclosure** pressure lives here.
- **loc_bar** — *so the player can* work the income **corruption ladder** (shifts → revealing → backroom,
  gated as canvases not a door) — the player-corruption + exhibitionism + money feeder.
- **loc_corner_store** — *so the player can* buy **groceries** (the household food sink) + **cheap clothes**
  (the low rung of the clothing ladder). Two sinks, one stop. *(Revealing outfits also come free-but-charged
  from Lynn's closet, and ordered via the phone — the clothing ladder's other rungs.)*

### Naming contract
- **Shared / public spaces = bare nouns:** `Kitchen`, `Living Room`, `Bathroom`, `Garage`, `Backyard`,
  `Master Bedroom`, `Bar`, `Corner Store`, `Town`.
- **Individually-owned personal spaces = possessive:** `Your Room`, `Maddie's Room`, `Roy's Shop`.
- Hierarchy rides the **header / nav-depth**, not the label (you know you're in the house). Branded/charged
  flavor ("your mother's old room", "the back room") lives in **body copy**, not the structural label.

### Travel friction
**Costed bridge, free intra-zone.** The Home⇄Town walk **costs ~30 min + a little energy** (so NPC
schedules bite — Roy at the shop by day vs. home by evening, the bar shift at night, Maddie out by day; the
player can't be everywhere). **Room-to-room inside a zone is free** (you don't pay to walk to the kitchen);
the day clock advances via activities (cook, shift, shower, sleep) + the commute. **Release valve (later):**
a faster ride (Wes's car / Roy's truck) unlocks as an instant fast-travel so friction never becomes tedium.

### The player thread (Step 4 · §2) — Cora, before any NPC

**2A — bootstrap (getting off zero).** Her drive is **two-layered**: the *survival* want is **reactive** —
her life collapsed (job gone, money gone, evicted), and that cause is stated flat in the opening (she's
crawled home at twenty-five because there was nowhere else). The *corruption* drive is **pure appetite that
grows from being needed** — no tragedy bolted on; it emerges from the caretaker-power discovery. First night,
alone in her childhood bed: humiliated, wrung out, and aware in a way she doesn't like of how a house full of
men who need a woman *feels*. The off-zero solo act — she touches herself, half for relief, half over the way
Roy's face changed when she put a plate in front of him. The **corruption-zero non-solo nudge:** filling in
(cooking the first breakfast, watching Roy's grief crack open at the sight of a woman at the stove) — a story
moment that opens her before she's chosen to escalate anything.

**2B — exhibition backbone (being seen).** Two stages: **the bar** (she dresses bolder for tips — wants the
money, then the rush, then to prove her own nerve) and **the house** (the one bathroom, the thin walls — at
first an accident, then not). The bolder-per-stage ladder: *bar* — a daring top → braless and short → the
backroom topless; *home* — a towel that slips → the door left cracked → crossing the hall in almost nothing
while she knows someone's awake. Being seen sets a **state the world reads** (the clothing exposure value) —
confirmed to exist; who-reacts-how is §5/§4.

**2C — earning IS corrupting (the money story).** Broke→rich, the better money always further down: lowest
respectable = **regular bar shifts** + **helping at Roy's shop** (legit, low). Dirtiest best-paying by the
end = **the bar backroom** (private work for Vic's patrons) + **selling photos on the phone**. Blocked or
sick of one → the others (three real scenes, not one button reskinned: shop=legit, bar=lewd, phone=pics).
**Buys she stays hungry for:** revealing clothes (the reactive dial), groceries/the household kept afloat
(being the woman who *provides*), gifts (spoil Maddie, soften Roy/Wes). **Key non-clothing items:** **Lynn's
left-behind clothes** (free, charged — found in the master closet, the highest-charge "outfit" in the game);
**booze** (a drink shared with Roy/Wes that lowers a guard); **the phone** (the channel); **gifts**. **The
squeeze escalates in form:** broke household (early) → **Roy's shop facing foreclosure** (mid) → **Lynn's
return** (late). Income endgame stays HOT (the matriarch's combined household beats, not a dashboard —
confirmed; owned by §1F).

**2D — the ceiling + other ladders.** Her own-depravity ceiling (about HER, not one relationship): **the bar
backroom at full tilt** — used by strangers for money with Vic watching, and/or a fully-nude display — the
top of her own depravity, unthinkable night one. **Early prey-liberty:** a drunk corners her at the bar when
she's still powerless and broke (a forced beat, no refusal) — and it **recedes as she rises** (by Act 2 she's
the one who corners). **No separate non-corruption stat ladder** (fitness/intelligence/charisma/career were
cut) — the one "rank" she climbs is her **standing as matriarch**, which is the corruption arc's structural
spine (the flag-chain `took_dads_place` → Wes → Maddie → `household_is_yours`), surfaced as story, not a
stat. Her solo loops (touch herself, the bar, exhibition) survive the plateau (confirmed; §1F).

**The daily-routine moments (where the world walks in — the fused solo-host + hijack units):**
- **Cooking (Kitchen)** — the *filling-in* chore + the relation feeder; Roy/Wes/Maddie drift in (the Lane-3
  hijack). One moment, two outcomes.
- **Showering (Bathroom)** — hygiene restore + the exhibition feeder + the §5H occupancy (she catches one of
  them / one walks in on her / the room names who's already in there).
- **A bar shift (Bar)** — earning + the exhibition feeder + a patron or Vic intrudes.
- **A quiet hour (Your Room)** — the solo/phone feeder + Maddie sometimes knocks.
These are the routines the world is allowed to intrude on — named here before any NPC arc claims them.

### Roy — story brief (Step 4 · §3) — core · foundation conquest · slow-burn (tender)

1. **End-state fantasy.** Roy stops grieving a wife who left and starts living for the daughter who stayed.
   By the end he doesn't pretend it's a substitute — he wants *Cora*, openly, in Lynn's bed, calling her
   the woman of the house and meaning all of it; he defers to her, provides what she asks for, and the worst
   secret in the house is the one he's most at peace with. The grief is gone because she filled the hole so
   completely there's no hole left.
2. **Voice.** Low, worn, a man who's run out of words for what's wrong. Calls her "sweetheart," then her
   name, then — the tell that he's fallen — "honey" the way he used to say it to Lynn. Apologizes a lot
   early (for the mess, for the drinking, for "putting this on you"). The private thought she glimpses: *she
   even stands like her mother did.* As he falls the apologies stop; the voice gets steady, certain, a man
   who's decided.
3. **The arc as moments.** The first breakfast (his face when a woman cooks again) → the first night she
   sits with him while he drinks and he talks about Lynn → the first time his hand stays on her shoulder too
   long and neither of them moves it → catching his eyes on her (the towel, the dress) and *letting* him →
   the night the talk about Lynn turns into a confession about *her* → the first kiss that isn't fatherly →
   the first time, in Lynn's bed → he stops calling it a mistake.
4. **Per-moment pretexts.** Grief gives every early crossing cover: comforting a crying drunk man; "you
   should eat something, Dad"; falling asleep against him on the couch; "I found Mom's old dress, does it
   still—" The pretext is always *care*, until care is the lie they both stop telling.
5. **The big nights.** (a) **The confession** — he says out loud that she's not her mother, she's *better*,
   and he's ashamed of what that means. (b) **The first time** — in the master bed, slow, devastated,
   tender; he cries and doesn't stop. (c) **Taking his place** — the morning she sits at the head of the
   table and he lets her; the household tips. *(This sets the fact the rest of the house reads.)*
6. **What changes after.** He brightens — shaved, drinking less, working again. Greets her at the door. Stops
   asking permission to need her and starts simply needing her. New everyday content opens (his bed, his lap,
   the shop visits get charged); the grief-comfort beats retire (he's not grieving anymore — she fixed it).
7. **Anti-patterns.** Don't make him a creep who wanted this all along — the heat is that he *resists*, that
   it's grief and love getting twisted, not a leer. Don't gate his arc on the bar/clothing (his spine is his
   own willingness + her corruption floor, never a public-exposure number). Don't let him stay sad after the
   capstone (status regression).
8. **Acceptance.** The hook is visible (a grieving man with a want of his own — to not be alone — who
   resists); the climb is legible (you can feel the line coming); the first time lands as the game's first
   gut-punch payoff; the explicit ceiling is honored (full, tender, crude where it counts).

### Wes — story brief (Step 4 · §3) — core · power-struggle conquest · antagonist→owned (rivalry)

1. **End-state fantasy.** The brother who ran this house surrenders it — and himself. By the end Wes, who
   fought her for every inch, comes to *her* door; the man who resented carrying the family now wants nothing
   more than to be told what to do by his little sister, relieved of the weight at last. The conquest is that
   she didn't soften him — she *won*, and he's happier losing.
2. **Voice.** Clipped, hard, sarcastic — a man who talks in jabs because he's too tired for anything else.
   Calls her nothing, or "princess" as an insult, until the insult turns into something else. The private
   thought she glimpses: *resentment that keeps curdling into something he won't name.* As he falls the jabs
   keep their shape but lose their edge — then he stops talking back at all.
3. **The arc as moments.** The first fight (she's barely in the door) → the cold war over who runs things →
   a fight that gets too close, too charged, both of them breathing hard → he catches himself wanting to win
   *differently* → the night the argument turns physical and isn't a fight → he tries to make it a
   one-time thing → he can't stay away → he comes to her, done fighting.
4. **Per-moment pretexts.** Friction is the cover: a screaming match that ends in a kiss; "fixing" something
   in the garage with her too close; competing over Roy, over money, over the house; a dare that goes too
   far. The pretext is always *winning*, until losing to her is the thing he wants.
5. **The big nights.** (a) **The first time** — angry, rough, a fight that flips; he hates that he started it
   and can't stop. (b) **The break** — the night he stops pretending it's about winning and gives her the
   house, the authority, himself. *(Cross-gated on Roy: once Cora has taken Dad's place, Wes has no ground —
   the fiction names it.)*
6. **What changes after.** The jabs go fond. He defers — checks with her, brings her things, takes orders in
   and out of bed. The rivalry content retires; the "man of the house" posture is gone (she's the house
   now). New everyday content: the garage as her turf, his hands when she wants them.
7. **Anti-patterns.** Don't make him warm too early — the whole charge is the fight. Don't resolve him before
   Roy (the cross-gate is load-bearing — telegraph it, never silent-lock). Don't write his surrender as
   defeat-and-sulk; it's *relief*.
8. **Acceptance.** Hook visible (a resentful man with his own want — to be in charge / to be relieved — and
   real teeth); the climb legible (friction you can feel heating); the break lands as a power payoff; ceiling
   honored.

### Maddie — story brief (Step 4 · §3) — core · protégé / first recruit · slow-burn (grooming)

1. **End-state fantasy.** The kid sister becomes Cora's accomplice and mirror — pulled all the way down the
   road Cora walked, and *grateful*. By the end Maddie isn't a target Cora took; she's a partner Cora *made*
   — eager, corrupted, in on it, the second woman of the house who shares the men and shares the secret.
   The charge is the making: watching the soft one go from flinching to hungry because Cora taught her.
2. **Voice.** Eighteen — clipped texts, eye-rolls, too-cool deflection over a kid who wants to be let in.
   Calls her "Cor." The private thought she glimpses: *envy of how easily her sister takes up space, and a
   want she doesn't have a word for yet.* As she falls the cool drops; she gets bold, then greedy, then
   starts initiating — the student outpacing the teacher.
3. **The arc as moments.** Sullen distance → she catches Cora at something (a glimpse of Roy-and-Cora, or
   Cora at the bar) and can't stop thinking about it → curiosity she frames as questions → Cora *shows* her
   (the first shared secret) → the first time Cora touches her → teaching her on the others → she's in.
4. **Per-moment pretexts.** Mentorship is the cover: "let me do your makeup," "I'll show you," advice that
   gets explicit, a shared bed during a thunderstorm like when they were kids, "don't tell Dad." The pretext
   is always *big-sister*, until big-sister means something else.
5. **The big nights.** (a) **The catch** — Maddie sees what Cora is and chooses to want in instead of
   recoiling *(sets the fact that opens her arc)*. (b) **The first time** — Cora's hands, slow, the soft one
   coming apart. (c) **The first share** — Cora brings her into a scene with Roy or Wes; Maddie's no longer a
   secret kept *from*, she's a secret *in on*.
6. **What changes after.** The sullenness is gone — she's lit up, conspiratorial, seeks Cora out. New
   content: she initiates, she helps, she's a piece on the board Cora can move (combined scenes). The
   distance/curiosity beats retire.
7. **Anti-patterns.** Don't skip the grooming — the heat is the *making*, the slow turn, not a sister who was
   secretly down from line one. Don't gate her *entry* on another arc (sister talk is cold-start); only her
   lewd rungs carry the corruption door + the catch. Keep her the protégé, not a peer/dating chase.
8. **Acceptance.** Hook visible (a kid with her own want — to be seen, to belong — who has to be *turned*);
   the fall is believable, beat by beat (no jump from sullen to hungry); the making-of-her lands as the
   distinct payoff; ceiling honored (sapphic incest, full).

### Lynn — story brief (Step 4 · §3) — pivotal-but-light · late-act pressure + frontier conquest · antagonist (displaced, NOT seduced)

1. **End-state fantasy.** The mother who walked out comes home expecting to slot back into her life and finds
   every piece of it already belongs to her daughter — her husband, her children, her bed, her kitchen, her
   name as "the woman of this house." The end-state isn't seducing Lynn; it's **making her watch**: the
   family chooses Cora, to her face, and Lynn either breaks and leaves for good or is kept around as a guest
   in the house she used to run. Pure displacement.
2. **Voice.** Brittle-bright, self-justifying, the practiced warmth of a woman used to being forgiven. Calls
   Cora "baby" like nothing happened. The voice cracks as she realizes the room has moved on without her —
   from charming to pleading to cold.
3. **The arc as moments (a capstone sequence, not a grind).** She returns (the call, the knock) → she tries
   to take her place back (the kitchen, the table, the bed) and meets a family that defers to *Cora* → the
   reckoning, member by member (Roy chooses Cora; Wes; Maddie) → the displacement capstone.
4. **Pretext.** Her own guilt and the family's poverty give her the opening to come back; Cora's grip on the
   household gives the door its lock — she literally cannot reclaim what's already owned.
5. **The big night.** **The reckoning** — Lynn forces the issue at the dinner table and the family lines up
   behind Cora; the frontier payoff. *(Requires the household already be Cora's.)*
6. **What changes after.** The plateau: Lynn gone (or kept, broken), the household fully Cora's, every loop
   still running. The greyed next-hook (Uncle Gary / the neighborhood) seeds here.
7. **Anti-patterns.** Don't seduce Lynn into warmth (she's the displacement target, not a recruit — keep her
   cold/broken). Don't let her return arrive before the household is earned (it's the frontier, gated on the
   whole machine). Don't make it a villain-monologue — keep it RTS-flat, the cruelty in the specifics.
8. **Acceptance.** She reads as a real person with a real want (her family back) and real teeth (she almost
   re-charms Roy) — so beating her *means* something; the capstone is the hottest, most-earned beat; honored
   at the charge ceiling.

5. **Big beat.** **The backroom** — the first time she does the high-paying lewd work; her own-depravity

8. **Acceptance.** He gives the self-corruption feeder a face + agency (he pushes, he profits); the income

### Reactivity as experience (Step 4 · §4) — what becomes different

**4A — when CORA's own state crosses a line.** Crossing a corruption band makes a *new kind of thing*
possible at once — the first band opens the bathroom-peek and the bar's revealing work; a deeper band opens
the incest door across the whole family at once (the door half of the double lock). And her **standing**
reacts independently of depravity: as she takes the family one by one and clearly becomes the one *winning*,
the others clock it unprompted — Wes gets warier of how the house defers to her *before* she's touched him;
Maddie watches her sister run things and wants in; Roy starts looking to her for decisions. The hub *choices*
change with her climb; the constant greeting paragraph does **not** tier on a stat (anti-pattern guarded).

**4B — when a family member falls.** Each capstone changes that same person's everyday hub (greeting, voice,
new repeatable content, retired beats — detailed per-brief §6) AND ripples to the others: **taking Roy**
sets the fact that leaves **Wes** no ground (his late rung lights up — telegraphed); **Maddie** gets easier
once she's *heard*/seen what Cora is (her catch-beat is reachable); in Act 2, owning two of them opens the
**combined scenes**. When Cora wins someone, a third person has **less**: each conquest strips a piece of
**Lynn's** possible reclamation (the frontier gets more locked-in) — named at the reckoning. **Greyed-rung
telegraphs:** a too-early Wes-late rung says, in fiction, "take Dad's place first."

**4C — when the outfit changes.** Same revealing outfit, three reads: the **Bar** lands hands (predator
patrons); **Town** lands stares + Brenda's gossip (prude/schemer); **home** lands a family member's eyes
(disposition-gated — Roy flustered→hungry, Wes banking it, Maddie mirroring). The **early prey window** (a
drunk takes a liberty she can't refuse) exists at the Bar and **recedes** as her power tier rises — the woman
who runs the house is no longer prey to the same dress in the same room. **Lynn's clothes** are the
top-charge outfit and read hardest at home (especially on Roy).

**4D — wallet / day / place / body-state.** **Money/leverage:** when Cora takes over the household's money
(pays the bills, saves or buries the shop), Roy's whole register flips — the provider becomes the provided-for
(a re-pricing of the house's power). **Day/time:** night opens the bar backroom and the late-house quiet
(charged hallway crossings); a new day advances the foreclosure clock; Lynn's return is a day-gated arrival.
**Body-state (designed, build-decision deferred to blueprint):** **pregnancy** — the ultimate displacement
("you're carrying his child, not hers"). A frontier-scoped optional branch (Roy's or Wes's), it would open
new late scenes and re-color the household; **flagged here, the build/no-build call is Step 5's** (kept out of
the core spine so the game ships without it).

**4E — the machine as one circuit.** Broke-pressure → the bar (corruption-ladder income) → money funds the
clothing (the reactive dial) + keeps the household fed (family relation held) → caretaking + self-corruption
opens the door → take Roy (`took_dads_place`) → Wes's break unlocks → groom Maddie → all three →
`household_is_yours` → Lynn's reckoning (the hottest beat) → the livable plateau (loops + bar + reactive
world still running) + the greyed Uncle-Gary hook. **The circle closes** — the income that sustains the
household comes from scenes the player actually plays (the bar), and the frontier reads into a steady-state,
not a dead end. No meter climbs that gates nothing (every odometer is read by the milestone it earns; checked
at Blueprint/feedback).

**4F — when she loses ground (the fail-state ripples — soft, real, recoverable).** Declared at §1C as soft +
real + recoverable; the ripples: (1) **broke** — money at zero with the groceries/bills event lapsed drops
the house into a **lean state** that cools every family relation and makes the day harder until she earns out
of it (it bites, then recovers); (2) **recklessness** — a mistimed family escalation triggers the **walk-in
setback** (relation hit + cooldown), and reckless public exposure raises **Brenda's suspicion** toward a
gossip setback. **No game-over, no decay spiral** — neglect freezes an arc (it doesn't rot), forward stays
the dominant direction.
