# media_lab — the study key

**⚠️ Don't read this before you've hunted the ten slots.** It names the hidden control, and
knowing which queries are which would bias the comparison — which is the only thing this
rig exists to produce.

## What this rig is

Round 2 of the query study. Round 1 had a flaw: item 1 was run against a slot that already
had a shipped clip I had seen, so my "blind" hunt was not blind. These ten beats are fresh —
neither of us has hunted them.

Two experiments run at once:

1. **The taste study.** LO hunts his way, the find-media skill hunts its way, traces get
   compared per `games/vesper/.find-media/route_study/TRACE_TEMPLATE.md`. Output: the heat
   axis in `references/scoring_rubric.md` filled in with earned rules. Today it rests on
   exactly one proven rule (eye contact holding across the loop) plus a rejection history.

2. **The doctrine control.** On 2026-07-27 the query doctrine changed: act-led instead of
   setting-first, setting words only when the setting carries meaning, no story or character
   words, anti-studio modifiers where the beat is grimy. That change rests on a single
   measurement. So three of these ten slots carry deliberately **old-style** queries.

## The control

| Slot | File | Style |
|---|---|---|
| 3 | `scenes/lab_flash_t4.webm` | **OLD** — `bedroom flashing tits playful quick reveal` |
| 6 | `scenes/lab_finish_facial_t5.webm` | **OLD** — `bedroom tender facial cumshot gentle` |
| 9 | `scenes/lab_passive_t5.webm` | **OLD** — `bedroom woman riding passive man slow` |

All three follow the retired rules exactly: **setting word first**, 3–5 words, mood and
story adjectives permitted (`playful`, `tender`, `gentle`, `slow`, `lazy`).

They sit at one light-band slot, one finish, and one position/POV slot — spread on purpose,
so the old style is tested across difficulty rather than only where it was always going to
lose.

The other seven are new-style: act and position lead; setting words appear **only** on slot
4 (a dark alley, where the dark is the meaning) and slot 10 (a location image, where the
place is the subject).

## What counts as a result

For each slot, per style, record: how many candidates the query surfaced, how many survived
the frame strip, and whether the installed pick was one LO would keep. If the new style
wins across bands the doctrine is earned. **If it doesn't, say so and roll it back** — the
whole point of writing a control is being willing to lose.
