PIECE P04 · place, Campus, and the way there: the bus and the walk

When / where: campus is one area; the quad is its hub. Hours below; the quad is always open. The
  only ways in and out are the bus and the walk, from her street and from the quad.
What the player sees:
  On Your Street, two buttons:
    "Take the bus to campus ($2, 20 min)": dimmed, with the engine's "(2 Money)", when she has under $2
    "Walk to campus (45 min)": costs 10 energy; dimmed, with "(10 Energy)", when she has under 10
  Bus: "The bus is packed. You stand the whole way, pressed between strangers, and step off at the
    campus gates."  Choice: "Get off at campus" (to The Quad)
  Walk: "It's a long walk: past the park, over the bridge, along the main road. By the campus gates
    your feet ache."  Choice: "Walk through the gates" (to The Quad)
  On The Quad, two buttons: "Take the bus home ($2, 20 min)" · "Walk home (45 min)", same costs.
  Bus home: "The bus home is half empty. You get the back seat and watch campus go."
    Choice: "Get off on your street" (to Your Street)
  Walk home: "The walk home takes forever. Your street, finally."  Choice: "Walk up your street"
  The Quad (passes through): "The quad. Paths cut across the grass to every building on campus."
    Links: Cafeteria · Faculty Offices · Lecture Hall · Library · Theatre
  Lecture Hall (weekdays 8am to 6pm): "The lecture hall: rows of seats rising to the back, a screen,
    a lectern at the bottom. Your classes are here. Nothing to do here right now."
    Link: Women's Bathroom. Closed: "The lecture hall is locked. It opens at 8am on weekdays."
  Women's Bathroom (off the lecture hall, same hours): "The women's bathroom off the lecture-hall
    corridor: three stalls and one long mirror. Nothing to do here right now."
    Closed: "The bathroom is locked with the lecture hall."
  Library (every day, 8am to 10pm): "The library: long tables, quiet floors, and the desk by the
    door. Nothing to do here right now."  Closed: "The library is closed. It opens at 8am."
  Cafeteria (weekdays 8am to 4pm): "The cafeteria: trays, long tables, the lunch counter. Nothing to
    do here right now."  Closed: "The cafeteria is closed. It's open weekdays, 8am to 4pm."
  Faculty Offices (weekdays 8am to 6pm): "The faculty corridor: a row of office doors with name
    plates, and the dean's office at the end. Nothing to do here right now."
    Closed: "The faculty corridor is locked. It opens at 8am on weekdays."
  Theatre (weekdays 9am to 9pm): "The theatre: a black stage, rows of folding seats, and the lights
    rigged overhead. Nothing to do here right now."
    Closed: "The theatre is dark. It's open weekdays, 9am to 9pm."
  Every place ends with "Leave <place>", back to where it opens from.
Choices: as above, one per travel screen.
