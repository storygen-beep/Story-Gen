PIECE P06 · system, the daily loop: sleep, eat, wash

When / where: at home, every day. Sleep in Your Bedroom (9pm to 3am), eat in the Kitchen (three times
  a day), shower in the Bathroom (any time).
What the player sees:
  Your Bedroom: "Sleep (8 hours)"; outside 9pm to 3am it shows greyed: "Sleep — you're not tired
    enough yet. From 9pm."
    Screen: "You crawl into the single bed, and you're gone before you've finished thinking about
    tomorrow."  Choice: "Wake up" (back to Your Bedroom; 8 hours pass; energy back to full)
  Kitchen: "Eat something (20 min)"; after three in one day it shows greyed: "Eat something — you've
    eaten enough today."
    Screen: "You eat standing at the counter: toast, whatever's in the fridge, coffee from the pot."
    Choice: "Done" (back to the Kitchen; 20 minutes pass; energy +15)
  Bathroom: "Shower (15 min)"
    Screen: "The water takes forever to run hot. You stand under it until it does, and you come out
    pink and awake."  Choice: "Done" (back to the Bathroom; 15 minutes pass; energy +5)
  The rooms' "Nothing to do here right now." lines come off Your Bedroom, Kitchen and Bathroom.
Choices: as above, one per screen.
Variants: none.
Changes: energy: sleep sets it to 100; eating adds 15; a shower adds 5; it never goes past 100.
  The day turns over at midnight on the engine's clock; sleeping across it is how a day ends. No flags.
Media: 3 spots. Sleep: her in her single bed, lights off, tier 0, her view. Eat: the kitchen counter,
  toast and coffee, tier 0, her view. Shower: the shower running, steam on the glass, tier 0, her view.
  No files until LO says "release ready".
Earlier releases: none. Earlier pieces: Your Bedroom, Kitchen and Bathroom (P02) lose "Nothing to do
  here right now."; the rest of their text stays.
Out of scope: anything that happens during a shower or in bed (walk-ins, touching herself: later
  pieces, inside these same screens, C782); who else is in the bathroom or kitchen (P32, P28); hunger
  or hygiene meters (none in this game yet); a nap.
