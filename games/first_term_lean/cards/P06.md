  times a day), shower in the Bathroom (twice a day).
What the player sees:

    hours, energy to full) — not until 9pm."

    Picture (Media, below), then: "You're gone before you've finished thinking about tomorrow."
    Choice: "Wake up" (back to Your Bedroom; 8 hours pass; energy to 100)

    greyed line from `cooldown_message` with `show_when_blocked` (v2.py:6700-6703).
