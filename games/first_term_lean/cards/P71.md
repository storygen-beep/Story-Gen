What a watch costs (read, and measured): a canvas is counted when its first screen shows
  (setup.markCanvasTriggered on node 0, v2.py:17063-17064), and the list is home_tv's first screen. So opening Watch TV
  spends one of the two a day, and "Turn the TV off" straight away still uses it. Measured headless on the current
  build: dayCount 0, then 1 on opening.
  - The row's grey line says what was counted: "you've had the TV on twice today." (it said "you've watched enough TV
    for one day.", false after two openings turned straight off; reader pass 1).
  - The engine can't avoid that without a change: the count is made on entry, not on a Done. Moving it to a Done is an
    engine change, LO's call. Until then the choice stays, and the cost is named here: turning it off at once still
    counts as one of today's two watches.
  - The same mark fires again whenever the first screen is shown again. Channel 5's "Change the channel" goes back to
    it, so it spends a SECOND watch (measured: 1 then 2). That makes P64's card false ("Changing channel after Channel 9
    stays inside the same Watch TV, so it doesn't use the second of the two a day."). My P64 headless check missed it:
    it looked for a link where the greyed row is not one.
    Fix, no engine change: "Change the channel" goes to a second copy of the list (node watch_more, not the first
    screen), with the same five channels and "Turn the TV off", so it doesn't count again. P64's card is corrected.
