  (7am–9pm), whenever Tom or Chris is behind the counter at the start and someone is there to pay her
  at the end (LO 2026-11-24, relayed by ENI: "shifts are independent of the classes, she can choose
  between things, its players freedom"; the shape is CoT's QuickieBurger, work any open hour, one
  click an hour). Their rows: Tom Monday–Saturday 10:00–21:00; Chris weekdays 07:00–15:00 and weekends
  10:00–20:00 (who_is_where; a row's end hour is the first minute he's gone).
  So she can start:
    an hour: weekdays 7:00–7:55pm; Saturday 10:00–7:55pm; Sunday 10:00–6:55pm.
    a shift: weekdays 7:00–4:55pm; Saturday 10:00–4:55pm; Sunday 10:00–3:55pm.
  Sunday before 10am nobody's in, so there are no rows; the Café's own hours still show.
  Classes stay where they are: working through 9–11 means she misses it, and that's her choice.

    7am and weekends from 10am; you can take an hour or a whole shift, as long as one of them is in till you're done."
