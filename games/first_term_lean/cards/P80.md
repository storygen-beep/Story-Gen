PIECE P80 · the Quests and People pages, redesigned (same words, new look)

When / where: the sidebar's Quests and People buttons, always. LO, typed in main-lean 2026-11-27: "So we did
  copied CoT's quests page and peoples page, I like the content of those pages but I dont really like the UI of
  it. How would you redesign it". He asked for a mockup first ("make the mockup first"), then trimmed the person
  page ("only schedule"; "remove talk, only schedule"), then "go ahead, make the card". The mockup he signed off:
  https://claude.ai/artifact/L8VowjA987iixCVKpYaGz5 (three screens: Quests, People, View Person). Engine work: LO's
  yes on this card is the yes the engine rule asks for.

Why: the pages copied P46 (CoT's Hints page and People dialog) and look like a different app from the sidebar:
  flat black boxes with thin grey lines. Quests is one long wall of 13 equal boxes under a checkbox form, with done
  and open mixed. People opens on "There are no people here." under a large "Filter Options" bar. The person page
  has two Back buttons, an "Overview" tab with nothing else beside it, and its bar labels sit far from their bars.

What the player sees (every quest, hint, person, role, tip and empty-tab line is the game's text, unchanged):
  1. Both pages, and the person page:
     - The sidebar's look: its surface, border and muted-text colours and its rounded corners, read from the
       theme variables (v2.py ThemeStyles, --theme-surface, --theme-border, --theme-text-muted, --theme-accent),
       never new hard-coded colours. Small uppercase labels, as the sidebar's.
     - "← Back" moves to the top left of the page, beside the title, in the same place on all three pages. The
       bottom "← Back" link goes.
     - Below about 700px wide, two and three columns become one.
  2. Quests:
     - Header: "Quests", and on the right "<n> open · <n> done".
     - "Next up" box at the top, larger: the first open storyline whose category starts "Storyline", else the first
       open one in file order. It shows the title, the category tag, the description, the hint in large text, and
       "Walkthrough →" when the storyline has one. That storyline is not repeated in the list below.
     - Category chips in one row in place of the checkbox table: "All" first, then each category as written in the
       game (with its emoji), each with a coloured dot. One tap shows only that category; "All" shows every one.
       The choice is remembered for the session ($hintCat).
     - Open storylines in two columns. Each card: the title in bold, the category tag at the right, the
       description in one small muted line, then the hint as the main text. The "Hint:" label goes. "see here"
       becomes a small "Walkthrough →" button.
     - An "Updated" tag on a card whose hint text changed since the page was last opened ("New" on a storyline
       that has just appeared; a finished one in the Done fold keeps its tag too). The page remembers each
       storyline's last-seen text ($hints_seen); the first opening of all marks everything seen with no tags.
       (Built 2026-11-27; the "New" wording and the tag on finished cards were added in the build, told to LO.)
       The sidebar's Quests button keeps its glow until the first opening (CoT's, P46) and after that shows
       "<n> new" while any storyline has a changed hint.
     - Finished storylines fold into "▸ Done (<n>)" at the bottom, closed at first, each greyed with its last
       line ("You have reached the end of this storyline... for now! ...").
     - The In Progress / Completed checkboxes go: the fold does their job.
  3. People:
     - Header: "People", a search box at the right of the header (search by shown name, as now). The "Filter
       Options" bar and the sort links go.
     - The tabs become pills with a count: "Here 2", "Friends 1", ... A tab with no one in it yet is greyed but
       still opens and shows its empty line, as now. Tabs that no person is filed under stay hidden, as now.
     - The page opens on Here when someone is here, else on the first tab with someone in it.
     - Person cards, three across: a circle with their initials ("??" for a stranger), the name (or stranger
       name), the role under it, a green "Here" dot when they are in her room, and the meters as short bars, each
       label right beside its bar. The whole card opens the person. "Click a person to see more." goes.
     - Paging (16 a page) stays.
     - The sidebar button reads "People" with "<n> here" on its right, in place of "People (<n>)".
  4. View Person:
     - One "← People" button at the top. The blue ◀, the "View Person" heading and the "Overview" tab go.
     - A large initials circle, the name, the role, the relationship line, and the stranger line ("A guy with a
       crooked grin.") in italics under them. A stranger shows "??", "???", the stranger line and "You're not
       sure who this is.", as now.
     - One button: "Schedule", which opens the same day-by-day pop-up as now (setup.castOpenSchedule).
     - The meters as labelled bars, label beside bar.
     - Personality bubbles stay where the people system puts them (off in this game, show_traits = false).
     - A "Where to find <him/her>" box: their card's tip, and the "Phone contact" badge under it when she has
       their number.

What the player loses (say it plainly):
  - "💬 Talk to …" on the person page (LO 2026-11-27: "remove talk, only schedule"). Talking is still in the room,
    on the person's portrait or row, as it is today.
  - The sort links (by name, by a meter). With about 25 people, search and the tabs do the job.
  - The In Progress / Completed filters; the Done fold replaces them.

Engine (all opt-in, so every other game builds byte for byte as before):
  - apps/game_generation/twee_comprehensive/generators/cot_pages.py: the Hints and People markup, styles and
    handlers above. Both stay behind `[ui] quests_layout = "hints"` and `[ui] people_page = "cot"`; only
    first_term_lean sets them (grep, 2026-11-27).
  - generators/people_system.py: its splice anchor `var talk = talkOf(slug);` goes with Talk; the personality
    splice moves to a new anchor, so the build still fails loudly if the anchor is ever lost.
  - The sidebar buttons' "N new" and "N here" labels (cot_pages.py journalButton and castButton).
  - Tests: apps/game_generation/tests/test_cot_hints.py and test_cot_people.py updated to the new markup, with
    new cases: Next up picks the storyline entry; a chip filters; Done folds the finished; "Updated" shows after
    a hint changes and not on the first opening; the opening tab; no Talk link; Schedule opens the pop-up.
    test_people_system.py still passes.
  - Docs: .claude/skills/author-game-v2/references/engine.md (the two keys' pages), CHANGELOG lines.
