PIECE P81 · going back no longer makes Quests glow again (the page's notes survive a rewind)

When / where: the sidebar's Quests button and the Quests page, always. LO, typed in main-lean 2026-11-28:
  "quests in the sidebar always keeps on glowing, see what is going on, and explain in simple words, dont
  make any changes yet". Told the cause and three fixes, with option 1 recommended; LO: "resolve it". Engine
  work: that is the yes the engine rule asks for.

Why: opening Quests writes "opened" ($hints_opened), and the glow stops. But "← Back" from a scene (the
  opening, which itself says to try the Quests button) and the sidebar's ← arrow go back through history
  (setup.smartBack's Engine.goTo, Engine.backward), and that puts every variable back as it was before the
  page was opened. So the button glowed again. The same rewind undid $hints_seen (old changes showed as
  "Updated" / "N new" again) and $hintCat (the chosen chip). Since 2026-11-26 (b1e9024b), when Quests
  became its own page instead of a pop-up. Reproduced headless before the fix.

What the player sees:
  - Once she has opened Quests, the button stops glowing for good, however she leaves the page: ← Back
    from a room, ← Back from a scene, or the sidebar's ← arrow.
  - A storyline she has seen stays seen: going back does not bring its "Updated" tag or the "N new" back.
  - The chip she picked stays picked.
  - Loading an older save shows that save's own state (a save from before she opened Quests still glows).
  - Going back still does not replay the scene she left; nothing else about going back changes.

What the player loses: nothing.

Engine (opt-in, behind `[ui] quests_layout = "hints"`; every other game builds byte for byte as before):
  - apps/game_generation/twee_comprehensive/generators/cot_pages.py, the hints script: the three keys
    (setup.pageMemoryKeys) are noted at each :passageend and on a chip click, and written back on a
    :passageinit that lands on another moment of the same history, into the live variables and that moment.
  - Test: apps/game_generation/tests/test_cot_hints.py::test_going_back_does_not_undo_what_the_page_remembers
    (fails on the old code, passes on the new).
  - Docs: .claude/skills/author-game-v2/references/engine.md ("Page memory"), CHANGELOG line.

Earlier releases: none shipped. Old saves: unchanged; the notes they hold are read as before.
Out of scope: how going back works for anything else; the People page (it keeps no notes in the save).
Builds: none in the game (engine only: generators/cot_pages.py)
