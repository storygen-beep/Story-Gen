# The idea — Members Only: her moment

> Written after the Want (`WANT.md`), before the world. Doctrine: `references/the-want.md` §0, §6;
> the eight lines: `references/the-release.md`, "The next step". LO picks it before the spine starts.

---

## 1. The fantasy — what the player comes here to feel

**Shape:** mix — **mystery**, paid for like **fall by need**. She is hunting her sister, and every
answer at the Linden has a price, and the price is her.

**In one sentence:** she came to pull her sister out of a private club, and the club is doing to her,
page by page, exactly what it did to her sister.

**The model to beat:** Course of Temptation — **what ours does better:** its players say there is
*"no driving plot or even a MacGuffin"*, that *"nothing really bad can ever happen"*, and that *"the
men in the game are not very aggressive"*. Ours has a missing sister with a date on it, and members
who ask first and ask again.

**Moments this game promises:** being seen · her body as the price for something she needs (a page) ·
her firsts · a consequence she has to live with. Not taboo at home.

## 2. The promise

- **The goal:** find out what happened to Dana — it lands on a **moment**, not a date: the night she
  opens the members' book in Julian's office (the top `nerve` rung, plus the page that says where the
  key is). What the book says opens the next goal: find Dana herself, and turn it on Julian.
- **The pressure:** Dana's tab, $300 every Sunday, counted by Julian. It repeats; it never ends.
- **The mystery:** why Dana stopped writing, and where she went — **it pays out** one diary page per
  release; each page names a person, a room or a night.
- **The rival:** Noor (27), who wants Dana's old place and the members who came with it.

## 3. The people who carry it

**The companion:** Noor (27), one step ahead. She shows her the dressing room, the rules nobody writes
down and which members tip. She is also the rival: she is helping her up to see how high she gets.

**The pressure-man:** Kessler (63). He holds page one and he'll hold more. His demand names the act,
he asks again after a no, and she can always walk out of the library — the page just stays with him.

**Her face:** one look for the whole game — dark hair to the shoulders, a gap between her front
teeth, freckles she covers for work. The dress changes; the face never does.

**What each man visibly wants** (`the-arc.md` A13 — the template does not ask; written anyway):
Kessler wants her voice and her standing in his light · Julian wants her to be Dana · Ade wants her
and is scared of it.

## 4. The first step — Kessler, step 1: page one

**Before — what it pays.** Nothing; this is **step 1**. His want is shown first: from her first shift,
his hub in the library reads *"He's in the armchair by the lamp with a page on his knee, holding it
too close to his face. When you come in, he stops pretending to read and watches you instead."* It
repeats on every visit until step 1 fires.

**The eight lines:**

1. **The fantasy.** The mystery, paid for with her body: the first page of Dana's diary is in an old
   man's lap, and the price is being looked at.
2. **The temptation, and his want before it.** Kessler moves first and names it: *"Read it to me.
   Standing, in the light, slowly."* She wants the page; he wants her mouth on her sister's words
   while he watches. The leak (above) is on his hub before he says a word.
3. **Her answers, and his "no" branch.** *Read it all, standing where he says* (full page, `shown`
   up) · *read it sitting down* (he allows it and stops her halfway: half the page) · *"Give me the
   page first"* (he laughs and lets her read one paragraph) · *walk out* — the page stays in his lap,
   he asks again the next night, and he remembers: *"You walked out on me once. Don't do it tonight."*
4. **Her voice at her level.** Low: *"I'm not here for you. I'm here for her page."* High, later:
   *"Do you want me to read it slower, or do you want me to take the dress off first?"*
5. **Who notices.** Ade. When she comes down at one a.m. he's drying glasses, and the next shift he
   pours her drink before she asks and says: *"Library, huh. Dana used to come down from there at one
   too."*
6. **What sticks, and he remembers.** `kessler_stage` 0→1, `kessler_page_1` set, `shown` up. His hub
   line changes to *"He taps the arm of the chair. 'The one who stands. Good.'"* — or *"sits"*, or
   *"walks out"*, after how she did it. And she learns what he hides: his eyes are going and he can't
   read handwriting any more, which is why he needs her. That is what she earns (A2).
7. **The moment to remember.** Being seen. The line: *"Stand in the light. I want to see your mouth
   when you say her words."*
8. **The door it opens, and the clip.** The mystery moves: page one names *"the red room"* upstairs
   and says *"J. keeps the book."* The clip: a woman in a short black dress reading from a page in a
   dim room, a man in an armchair watching — **intent only**: no clip is on disk and no search has
   been run.

**Leads to — what it opens.**
