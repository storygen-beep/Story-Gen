# [READY] System — Money
> Signed by LO 2026-09-29, the day it was drafted — LO bypassed the day-after rule for this test.

**What it tracks:** her cash, in dollars. **Kind:** ambient — the floor and upstairs pay it; the
tab, the shop and the docks take it. **The number:** `money`, shown.

**The rising tab (settled 2026-09-29):** Sunday stays $300; page two and page four each switch on
$15 a day, shown every morning; Julian names the new figure at the count. About $405 a week, then
about $510.

## Where it comes in

| where | the row | effect | how often |
|---|---|---|---|
| Bar | the shift | `money` add 50 | once a day |
| Bar | a member's table (red dress or her own dress) | `money` add 30 more | on the shift |
| Upstairs | a paid visit | `money` add 100 | once a day |

**A full week at most:** $600.

## Where it goes

| where | what | effect | why it matters |
|---|---|---|---|
| Julian's office, Sunday | Dana's tab | `money` add -300, taken by the engine | miss it and she's off the floor for the week |
| every morning, after page two | Dana's other debts | `money` add -15 | the tab rising |
| every morning, after page four | more of Dana's debts | `money` add -15 | stops rising here |
| Town | a dress of her own | $150 | a member's table on the floor |
| Town | a charger for Dana's phone | $40 | Dana's messages — a clue that stays hers |
| Docks | a boat to where Dana went | $1,000 | the next goal, after v0.1 |

**Money that opens a door:** the boat. The button shows locked until she has $1,000, and says why.
**A missed Sunday:** the floor is shut for the week; upstairs still pays. How the floor reopens is
not settled yet — decide before the build.
**Brake:** one shift and one visit upstairs a day.
