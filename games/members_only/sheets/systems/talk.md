# [READY] System — The talk (who knows she goes upstairs)
> Signed by LO 2026-09-29, the day it was drafted — LO bypassed the day-after rule for this test.

**What it tracks:** how far the word has spread that she goes upstairs. **Kind:** sourced — only the
members' rooms feed it; staff, members and the town read it. **The number:** `reputation`, 0–100,
never shown.

## Where it is filled

| where | the row | effect |
|---|---|---|
| Upstairs | every paid visit | `reputation` add 1 |

## Who reads it — mostly the words, almost never a door

| where | what changes |
|---|---|
| Ade, at the bar | three lines as it rises: doesn't know · heard · stops asking |
| Noor | from rival to "fine, you win the old man" |
| members at the Bar | a stranger says her name before she gives it |
| the Dressing Room | the other girls stop talking when she walks in |
| Town and Docks | a look, then a line, then a man asking her price |
| **the one mechanical read** | higher up, members ask for her more often on the floor |

**Refuses:** nothing.
**What she sees:** people's lines only. No number, no bar.
**Brake:** it only rises on paid visits, and those are one a night.
