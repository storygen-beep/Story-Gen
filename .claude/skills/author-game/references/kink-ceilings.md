# Per-arc kink / vocabulary ceilings — what each arc is allowed to say at full intensity

Read this when you write an NPC's **voice spec** (Step 4 story brief §2 / `content-framework.md` §3F), commit
its **per-tier register column** (Step 5 blueprint Pass 2 stat ladder), or author **explicit prose** at a
Tier 4+ canvas (`beat-authoring.md` voice carriage). The lanes file forwards here for the register model
(`references/lanes.md` "Voice register"); the sex-loop file forwards here for the in-loop label ceiling
(`references/sex-loop.md`); Step 6 audits against it (`step-6-feedback.md` "ceilings honored").

This file owns the **per-NPC vocabulary ceiling** — *how crude the prose for THIS arc is allowed to get at the
top of its road*. It is a CONTENT-DOCTRINE file (almost no engine surface). It is also the file that governs
explicit register, so it doesn't flinch: the examples below use the real words because the prose you author
must use the real words.

## §1 — The default: deliver at the ceiling, don't soft-pedal

Each NPC arc declares a **per-tier vocabulary ceiling** — the crudest the prose for that arc is permitted to
get at its top tier. The headline rule:

> **The default is to DELIVER at the ceiling, not soft-pedal toward it.** A revealing scene with a declared
> "full explicit" ceiling that ships with vague body-response prose ("you feel yourself responding," "time
> seems to slow") has *failed the ceiling*, not played it safe.

Why this is the default and not "be moderate": left without a per-arc ceiling, default authoring sits at
**medium-explicit** — anatomically named but soft on cum, named relationships but soft on the taboo callout —
and medium-explicit DRIFTS soft under repetition. The corrective is the ceiling: name what full intensity is
for this arc, then write to it.

The register stays **RTS-flat** at the everyday tiers (Lane 1/2/3 — ~35–40 words per beat, re-readable, no
literary ritual; `references/lanes.md`). Flat is about DENSITY, not euphemism. At the explicit tiers the flat
prose goes **crude and anatomical** — real terms (`cock`, `cunt`, `tits`, `ass`, `cum`), not euphemism. Flat
+ crude is the target; flat + coy is the failure.

**Deliver, don't soft-pedal** — the one line to carry into every Tier 4+ scene.

### A character truth is a writing LENS, not a content GATE

One soft-pedal disguises itself as good writing: using a character's nature to *narrow the content.* "She's cold
— she feels nothing but the sex" becomes the excuse to collapse a cheer-him-up-with-sex beat into "cold help
only," or to bolt a *"but never 'I care about you'"* asterisk onto a hot scene for character-purity. That reflex
is a **brake on the product** — this is an adult game built for player experience; **lead with the hot version.**

The split: a character's emotional truth (cold, detached, broken, feels only the sex) is a **LENS** — it colors
HOW a scene reads (her interior blankness, the flat register, no tenderness in her *voice*). It is **never a
GATE** on WHAT content exists or how explicit it gets. Coldness changes the delivery, not the deliverable. Same
shape as the arousal rule (`references/trait-design.md`: throttle-keyed prose stays *heat-of-the-moment*, never
rewrites the permanent content) — a state colors the prose, it doesn't cut it.

This does **not** loosen the **declared** caps — the vocab ceiling (§2), the place ceiling (§5), the tier gate
(§4), an arc's honest empty cells (`references/lanes.md`). Those are chosen on purpose at brief/blueprint time and
they stay. What the rule bans is the *ad-hoc, keyboard-time* narrowing you invent in the name of character purity.
If a scene genuinely shouldn't exist, that's a design call made in the open (a ceiling, a budget) — never a purity
reflex mid-draft.

## §2 — The kink-area ceiling table (the spec)

Each row pairs a kink area to its **full-intensity ceiling** (what's permitted at the TOP tier) plus
**examples ALLOWED** and **examples NOT ALLOWED**. These are the worked precedents — the registers the cast's
arcs come back at when the player asks for "full / as explicit as it goes." A NPC's row is the spec its
Tier 4+ prose is held to.

| Kink area | Full-intensity ceiling | Examples ALLOWED | Examples NOT ALLOWED |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Paternal / "daddy" framing** | **FULL DADDY FRAMING** | She calls him "daddy" during sex; he calls her "good girl" / "baby girl"; explicit father-figure dialogue ("come to daddy," "daddy's going to take care of you"); paternal authority is part of the kink at ALL tiers, implicit early, explicit at 4+ | Vanilla "honey" / first-name framing during sex; ignoring the paternal frame entirely; "warm older man" register that drops the authority |
| **Breeding / cum-inside (pregnancy NOT in scope)** | **CUM-INSIDE WITHOUT BREEDING TALK** | "Cum inside me" / "don't pull out" / "I want to feel you" / bareback intimacy framing | "Breed me" / "knock me up" / "fill me with your cum" / explicit pregnancy talk — deferred until the pregnancy system is live |
| **Breeding (pregnancy in scope)** | **FULL BREEDING TALK** | "Breed me" / "knock me up" / "fill me with your cum" / "put a baby in me" / "I want to carry your child" — retrofitted into the scenes once the pregnancy mechanic is wired | — (no restriction when pregnancy is in scope) |
| **Anatomical + cum + facial / creampie / squirt detail** | **MAXIMUM CRUDE DETAIL** | "His cock" / "your cunt" / "your tits"; explicit cum descriptions (load size, where it lands, what it feels like); facials with cum-on-face detail; creampies with the cum dripping out; squirt described graphically | Euphemism / vague anatomical references; "between your legs" instead of "your cunt"; "he finished" instead of "he came inside you, his cum dripping out as you stood up" |
| **Roughness / dom-sub / verbal degradation** | **FULL ROUGH + DEGRADATION** | Hair-pull / spit / choke / slap / face-fuck mechanics; degradation talk ("good girl," "such a slut," "made for this," "use you"); explicit power dynamic — he's dominant, she's told what she is | Vanilla equal-partnership sex; refusing the degradation vocabulary in a scene that calls for it; softening "slut" to "naughty" |
| **Sibling / family incest framing** | **FULL INCEST CALLOUTS** | "Brother" / "sis" / "little sister" callouts during sex; explicit reference to the taboo ("this is so fucking wrong," "my own brother," "we shouldn't be doing this"); the incest IS the kink — named and dwelt on at all tiers (implicit early, explicit at 3+) | Avoiding the taboo; generic dialogue that could be any two characters; never naming the relationship during sex |
| **Confrontation + cuckold framing** | **FULL CUCKOLD FRAMING** | The wife/partner watches / listens / participates; explicit cuckold dialogue ("watch your husband fuck me," "your wife is my second wife," "she gets to feel it inside her"); cuckold IS the resolution kink for the brought-in branch | Reframing the brought-in branch as wholesome polyamory ("they all became close friends"); ignoring the cuckold dynamic |
| **Public / outdoor / exhibitionism** | **FULL RISK + ONLOOKER AWARENESS** | Outdoor scenes name the risk explicitly ("someone could see," "the neighbors," "if she looks out the kitchen window"); at high scandal, town-NPC onlookers are acknowledged in scene; the exhibitionism IS the kink — the risk gets her off | Treating outdoor as just a location label ("he takes her in the yard") without exploiting the risk-frame; no visibility, no audience awareness |
| **Lesbian / same-sex initiation** | **(declare before authoring)** | — | — |
| **Non-con / dubcon register** (where the premise allows it) | **(declare per arc — name the floor)** | At the declared floor: refusal that doesn't stop it, the no-choice liberty, the prose holding her reaction without softening it — where the PLACE ceiling permits forced content (§5) | Sanitizing a forced beat into reluctant-but-into-it when the design called for non-con; OR running non-con register where neither the arc NOR the place ceiling permits it |

**Reading the table.** A row's ceiling is the **maximum at full intensity** for any arc that carries that
kink. Frank-shaped paternal arc → FULL DADDY at its top tier. A sibling arc → FULL INCEST CALLOUTS. The
"NOT allowed" column is the soft-drift to avoid, not a separate softer mode — it's what failing the ceiling
looks like.

**A blank ceiling = OUT OF SCOPE until declared.** The lesbian-initiation and non-con rows ship with a
`(declare before authoring)` ceiling on purpose — they need the user's explicit floor before any scene uses
them. **A scene that touches a kink area whose ceiling is blank does NOT ship until the user fills the
ceiling.** Don't author against an undeclared ceiling — that's exactly where soft-drift creeps in. Stub the
canvas (`(placeholder — ceiling undeclared)`) and surface it.

## §3 — The maximum-explicit default (when a NEW kink area surfaces)

The pattern across every filled row above is **maximum-explicit**. So when a kink area surfaces during
authoring that isn't in the table — a new arc's framing, a dom-sub register for a non-central NPC, a kink the
brief didn't anticipate — the default is:

> **Default to the most explicit interpretation in any ambiguity. Anything softer is the WRONG default.**
> Only soften if the user explicitly says so.

Concrete:
- New arc proposed with "tender father-figure" register → default to **FULL DADDY**, not a softer "warm older
  man."
- New scene with creampie + breeding, pregnancy live → default to **FULL BREEDING TALK**, not "intimacy
  framing."
- New cuckold scene with a secondary witness → default to **FULL CUCKOLD**, not wholesome polyamory.
- Public exposure scene → default to **FULL RISK + ONLOOKER AWARENESS**, not "scenic outdoor location."

This is a DEFAULT, not a mandate to escalate past the user's call. If the user set a softer ceiling for an
arc, honor it. The default only governs the *unstated* case — and the unstated case resolves explicit.

## §4 — Tier-GATED, not tier-LINEAR (the ceiling is a cap, not a forced grid)

The ceiling is **what's permitted at the TOP tier** — it is a **gate**, not a forced linear escalation grid.
Two failures this distinction kills:

- **Not tier-LINEAR.** The ceiling does NOT mean "Tier 0 = 0% of the register, Tier 5 = 100%, interpolate."
  The crude vocabulary doesn't *fade in* proportionally. It's **gated**: the register doesn't appear at all
  until the arc reaches the tier that unlocks it, then steps up, then becomes default at the top. Below that
  gate, the lower tiers use a *softer* register — not a diluted fraction of the ceiling.

- **The ceiling is a CAP, not a quota.** A scene below the top tier isn't *required* to push toward the
  ceiling. A Tier-1 warmth beat in a daddy arc doesn't have to gesture at "daddy" — it just can't run vanilla
  past where the gate opens. The ceiling caps the top; it doesn't force every rung to climb toward it on a
  schedule.

Worked, for a paternal arc whose ceiling is FULL DADDY + the cum-inside language gated by player corruption:

| Tier (player corruption) | Daddy register | Cum-inside language |
|---|---|---|
| 0–2 (corr 0–14) | None — he's still the father-figure, voice is warm/authoritative, not sexual | None |
| 3 (corr 25+) | She MAY use "daddy"; he MAY answer it | "Cum inside me" emerges |
| 4 (corr 35+) | She routinely uses "daddy"; he starts "good girl" | "Don't pull out" / "I want to feel you" |
| 5 (corr 50+) | DEFAULT register — the daddy frame is baseline in every sex scene | "Cum inside me" baseline (+ full breeding talk once pregnancy is live) |

The register **doesn't appear** until tier 3, **steps up** at 4, becomes **default** at 5. Pre-tier-3 sex (if
the arc has any) uses the softer register; post-tier-3 sex uses the full register. That's tier-gated. A
linear grid that puts 20% of the crude vocab at tier 1 is the wrong shape — it leaks the ceiling early and
makes the gate meaningless.

## §5 — Two different ceilings: PLACE ceiling vs NPC vocab ceiling — don't conflate

There are **two** ceilings in play and they answer different questions. Keep them separate.

| | **PLACE ceiling** | **NPC vocab ceiling** (this file) |
|---|---|---|
| **Question it answers** | WHERE is forced / explicit content *allowed to happen* | HOW CRUDE is the *language* for this arc at full intensity |
| **Scoped to** | a location (its privacy / exposure) | a per-NPC arc (its voice) |
| **Owner** | `content-framework.md` §5B (the per-place ceiling) | this file (declared in the NPC's voice spec) |
| **Example** | "in the lobby a hand on her back is the ceiling; in the back office she gets used on the spot" | "Frank's arc says FULL DADDY at tier 4+; Marcus's stays peer/school register" |

They compose, they don't override each other:
- The **place ceiling** decides whether a forced / no-refusal beat is even *permitted* in this room (the
  prey-floor allowance, §5B). It gates the EVENT.
- The **NPC vocab ceiling** decides the crudeness of the LANGUAGE once a permitted scene plays. It gates the
  WORDS.

So: a high-ceiling NPC arc (FULL DADDY) in a low-ceiling PUBLIC place still doesn't get a forced sex scene
*there* — the place forbids the event regardless of the arc's vocab ceiling. And a permitted scene in a
high-ceiling private place still runs the LANGUAGE at the arc's vocab ceiling, not the place's. **Don't read
a permissive place as license to crank a soft arc's vocabulary, and don't read a crude arc as license to run
forced content in a place that forbids it.** Two ceilings, two jobs.

(The place ceiling is also distinct from the **exposure-tier ceiling** on a Lane 1 hub — which *rungs* a
hub's privacy permits (`references/lanes.md`, the "Exposure-tier ceiling" bullet under *Lane 1 — hub button*).
That's a third, location-privacy thing; it caps which escalation choices appear, not the prose register.)

## §6 — How the ceiling is DECLARED (and where it's enforced)

The vocab ceiling is **declared at brief-time, committed at blueprint-time, enforced at canvas-time.** It is
never a per-canvas decision — by the time you write a Tier 4+ scene, the ceiling is already set and you
*translate* it.

**1 — Declared in the NPC's voice spec (Step 4 story brief).** The qualitative ceiling — "how explicit do the
scenes get at the peak, what acts are on the table, what is the crudest word the prose will use, will it
deliver or soft-pedal" — is answered in the **voice spec** (`step-4-deep-design.md` Pass 2 item 2) against
`content-framework.md` §3F ("Their voice and their ceiling"). This is where FULL DADDY / FULL INCEST / peer
register gets named for the arc. Pick the spine and the *trait* facts elsewhere — `references/trait-design.md`
(which axis drives the arc), `references/trait-catalog.md` (the trait data); this file is the *vocabulary*
layer that rides on top of those, not a re-derivation of them.

**2 — Committed in the blueprint stat ladder (Step 5 Pass 2).** The **per-tier register column** — which tier
the register turns on, steps up, becomes default (the §4 grid) — is committed in the NPC's blueprint stat
ladder (`step-5-blueprint.md` Pass 2), alongside the gate thresholds. The story names the ceiling; the
blueprint pins it to the corruption/relation tiers that gate it.

**3 — Recorded in the ledger.** Each NPC carries a `vocab_ceiling` field in its ledger entry
(`references/ledger-schema.md` — `"vocab_ceiling": "explicit"`, default max-explicit). Keep it current so the
continue loop authors to the right register without re-deriving it.

**4 — Enforced at canvas-authoring time.** When you write a Tier 4+ canvas (`beat-authoring.md` voice
carriage), you read the NPC's row in §2 + its voice spec and **translate** the ceiling into the prose — you do
NOT re-decide it (a missing ceiling is a stop: bounce to the brief, don't improvise a register). Per-tier
register variants within one scene are carried by **`[group]` blocks** keyed on the gating trait — consecutive
group blocks with `conditions` render as a `<<if>>/<<elseif>>/<<else>>` variant chain at runtime
(`v2.py:12998-13038` — `_render_group_chain`), so a single sex canvas can run the softer register below the gate and the full register
above it from the same canvas.

## §7 — The per-canvas grep audit

After authoring a Tier 4+ canvas, grep its body to confirm the register actually landed — a green build does
NOT check this. Each arc-kind has a tell:

```bash
# Paternal arc, tier 4+ → daddy register present
grep -n "daddy\|good girl\|baby girl" <canvas_body>

# Incest arc, tier 3+ → the taboo is named during sex
grep -n "brother\|sis\|little sister\|stepbrother\|stepsister" <canvas_body>

# Any explicit scene → crude anatomical terms, not euphemism
grep -n "cock\|cunt\|tits\|cum\|ass\b" <canvas_body>

# Outdoor / exhibitionism scene → the risk is named
grep -n "see\|onlooker\|neighbor\|window\|caught\|watch" <canvas_body>   # outdoor canvases only

# Cuckold branch → the dynamic is named, not reframed
grep -n "watch\|husband\|wife\|inside her" <canvas_body>   # brought-in / cuckold canvases
```

- A paternal Tier 4+ canvas with **zero** `daddy` / `good girl` / `baby girl` hits → register is soft.
  Rewrite.
- An incest Tier 3+ canvas with **zero** relationship callouts → the taboo is missing. Rewrite.
- A canvas declared "MAXIMUM CRUDE DETAIL" with **zero** crude anatomical hits → it soft-pedaled the cum /
  anatomy. Rewrite.

The grep is a *floor*, not a *proof* — hits don't guarantee the scene delivers, but zero hits on a canvas
whose ceiling demands them is a positive failure. Run it; it's the cheapest catch.

## §8 — Anti-patterns

- **Default to medium-explicit.** The most common drift: anatomically named but soft on cum, relationships
  named but soft on the taboo callout. *Fix:* §1 + §3 — deliver at the ceiling; default to maximum-explicit.

- **Vanilla register at sexual register.** Frank using "honey" in a Tier 4 sex scene; a sibling arc fucking
  with no callouts; a degradation arc that won't say "slut." *Fix:* §2 is the spec — if the row says FULL
  DADDY, FULL DADDY at the gated tier.

- **Authoring against an undeclared ceiling.** Writing a scene for a kink area whose §2 row is blank (lesbian
  initiation, non-con) before the user filled the ceiling. *Fix:* blank rows don't ship — stub the canvas and
  surface the ceiling question first.

- **Soft-pedaled cum / creampie / squirt.** "Between your legs" for "your cunt"; "he finished" for "he came
  inside you." *Fix:* the anatomical row says MAXIMUM CRUDE DETAIL — crude, specific, visceral.

- **Reframing the kink as a wholesome dynamic.** A cuckold branch resolved as "they became close friends"; a
  non-con beat softened into reluctant-but-into-it. *Fix:* the kink IS the resolution — play it at the row's
  ceiling, don't sand it down.

- **Character-purity restraint reflex.** Using a character truth ("she's cold / feels only the sex / never
  comfort") to VETO or narrow a hot beat — collapsing a cheer-him-up-with-sex scene to "cold help only," or
  tacking a *"but never 'I care about you'"* asterisk onto explicit content. *Fix:* §1 — a character truth is a
  writing LENS (it colors the voice), never a content GATE; lead with the hot version, let the coldness color the
  delivery, not cut it. (Distinct from the DECLARED caps in §2/§4/§5, which stay.)

- **Treating outdoor as a location label.** "He takes her in the yard" with no risk-frame. *Fix:* the public
  row says FULL RISK + ONLOOKER AWARENESS — name the visibility; the risk is the kink.

- **Ignoring the frame in every beat of the arc.** Paternal authority dropped in the early tiers; the incest
  relationship never present in the prose interior. *Fix:* the frame rides at ALL tiers (implicit early,
  explicit at the gate) — the early beats reference it without the crude vocab; the gated beats say it
  outright.

- **Mixing ceilings across NPCs.** Her register suddenly running "daddy" with a peer NPC because she uses it
  with the paternal NPC. *Fix:* the ceiling is **per-NPC** — her register adapts to who she's with;
  per-NPC consistency throughout.

- **Tier-linear leak.** Putting 20% of the crude vocabulary at tier 1 because the ceiling is FULL. *Fix:* §4 —
  tier-GATED, not linear; the register is absent below its gate, not diluted.

- **Conflating the two ceilings.** Cranking a soft arc's vocabulary because the *place* is permissive, or
  running forced content in a place that forbids it because the *arc* is crude. *Fix:* §5 — place gates the
  EVENT, NPC vocab gates the WORDS; they compose, neither overrides the other.
