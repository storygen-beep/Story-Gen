## The shape

1. **Name the moment.** Any screen where she showers, dresses, undresses, drops or ties a towel, gets into or out of bed, or stands at a mirror is a body moment. The card says so on its `Media:` line.
2. **Stay on her body, at her level.** Named parts of her and what she feels: the water on her tits, the towel's edge at her thighs, cold air on her bare ass. At stage 0–1 it happens to her and she reacts; it never becomes her choosing an act her stage hasn't opened (L71).
3. **No skip.** A moment the piece builds is written, not jumped over. "Back in your room, dressed" after a towel scene skips two body moments and moves no garment (C719, C1158).
4. **One picture of her,** right after the line that says the body (C1262). Her in it, never the empty room or the steam. Something happens (water running down her, the towel falling): a gif with "gif" in the search words; a held pose: a still (C1275). Tier `_t4` when she's bare, `_t3` when it's a look at her body covered (C1274). Name the place, and a mirror says "No phone" (C1277, C1279).
5. **Pool size.** On a repeat button (the daily shower): 4. In a one-time scene (the opening's shower, towel and dressing): 1 (C1280; LO, relayed 2026-11-22: "4. And for onetime scenes like opening it should have only one.").
6. **No meter moves** while meters are still. The moment pays nothing; it's there because it's hot.
7. **The events stay scoped.** Touching herself and a walk-in during the moment stay where the plan scopes them; touching is a choice inside the moment's own screen when it opens (C782). Out of scope means those events, never the moment's body.
