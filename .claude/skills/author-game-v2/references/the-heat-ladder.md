Added 2026-11-01. This file owns the rungs before sex: which kind of act opens at which stage of her meters,
in what order, what a scene of each kind must contain to be hot, and what it pays. `the-arc.md` owns how one
person's steps chain; `register.md` owns the voice and the explicit beat; `the-meters.md` owns the numbers.

## H1 · The order, and the stage each kind opens at

**The order across an arc** is the same in every signed game that builds toward a group (`HEAT_STUDY.md:544-555`,
strength "strong"): things that happen to her, then she watches, then a chosen act in front of one person she
knows, then one-on-one with him, then paid or on camera, then D/s, then known people together, then sharing,
then a group. Players notice when it breaks: IHOH put camming before a braless park dare, which a player
called "rather counterintuitive" (`HEAT_STUDY.md:538`).

**The default stage table.** Five stages on her two meters (Corruption · Exhibitionism). This is the table LO
signed for first_term on 2026-10-06 (`SP2_ladders.md:34-44`), with the kinds the 2026-11-01 study added placed
where the field gates them. A game's SP2 page may move a row; it may not drop the order.

**Audience size sets the stage** (LO, 2026-10-07): one person is stage 2 (a flash) or 3 (naked, private); a
group, known or not, is stage 4. Known against stranger is not the test (`SKILL_CHANGES_FROM_FIRST_TERM.md`
B24).

**A sex act a group watches is a mixed act** (LO, 2026-11-03, PICKS A68 a). The act uses Corruption and the
audience uses Exhibitionism, so it needs both meters at stage 4 (Corruption 60 and Exhibitionism 60) and pays
both. An act with one watcher keeps the act's own meter and stage.

**Two meters, one act** (first_term DECISIONS 103; LO, 2026-11-03). With two player tiers, each act names the
meter it uses and pays that meter. A mixed act needs both meters at its stage. Her first time out at a stage
needs either one. Checked: gate *an act pays the meter it uses*.

**A release that writes only some stages** (LO, 2026-11-03). A release that writes stages up to N caps every pay
at the top of stage N, shows no button for a stage it does not write, and names the next stage only on a quest
card.

**Small rungs inside a stage** (LO, 2026-11-03, PICKS A69 a). A stage may carry small rungs at fixed steps,
5, 10 and 15 inside each 20 (Corruption 5, 10, 15; then 25, 30, 35; and so on). Each small rung is a small step of
the same kind as its stage: one aisle closer, the sound turned up, the door left unlocked, a few seconds longer.
It is still unseen, so it never moves her to the next stage's kind: staying to watch is still stage 1, and a
peek she goes after is still stage 2. A small rung may show greyed inside the scene as a preview of the next bolder
act (`engine.md` §15 rule 1 has the exception, and lint *which refusals are shown at all* skips it).

**A kind climbs inside itself.** Its low version opens low and its high version waits for its stage: CoT's
flash ladder runs belly 1, bra 2, underwear 3, breasts 4, everything 5, and the payment code uses the same
ladder (`cot.md:127`, `cot_40_events.js:1395-1398`); the photo menu runs face to nude (`out_kinds.md:466`).

## H4 · What every low-heat scene contains

Counted across 31 flirt, tease and flash scenes and about 55 more of the other kinds. Talk-only scenes are the
only ones missing the first two.

1. **A named part of her body** on the page: 10/10 in CoT, 10/10 in IHOH and CW, 9/11 in SD, NL and UM; the two
   without it are talk-only (`cot.md`, `ihoh_cw.md`, `sd_nl_um.md`).
2. **His reaction, written**: a stare, a blush, a bulge named (9/10 IHOH and CW; 10/10 CoT).
3. **A "not yet" or a price.** The withheld act said out loud (*"You don't get to touch . . . yet."*,
   `ihoh:43257`), an excuse (*"Sorry, I'm kinda itchy"*, `um:1702`, `sd_nl_um.md:60`), an interruption, or a price on the yes
   (*"Not for free!"*, `ihoh:57449`).
4. **A hook**: the next rung as a choice, a flag, an unlock (10/11 SD, NL and UM; 8/10 IHOH and CW).

**Per kind, also:** an object marks the line; the risk is a sentence (*"I would just be caught, plain and
simple."*, `ihoh:4770`); the sound comes before the body; a record outlives the scene; an exit on every rung
(*"End this whole dare now"*, `ihoh:16886`). Her clothes pick what is shown wherever she is the one seen.

**Her line changes with her level** in fewer than half the scenes in every kind (`home_kinds.md:434-446`,
`out_kinds.md:482`). Write it anyway: it is the cheapest heat there is (`register.md`, "One event, several
levels"): *"Hi"* at Exhibitionism 1, *"if you want to look, you can just ask"* at 4 (`cot:L41386`).

> ⚠️ **The failure shape is talk only.** new-lust's wink and Cupid's Way's banter-only "Tease him." with Liam name no
> body and write no reaction, and Liam's pays charisma, not corruption (`sd_nl_um.md`, `ihoh_cw.md:106`). So did
> first_term's doorway lean. Players say the same of scenes that are only an image and a number: *"They don't
> notice - dont take a hint - NADA."* (IHOH, `home_kinds.md:407`).

## H5 · What pays

**LO decided both on 2026-11-01**, from the field below.

> **His Want pays for what happened, never for a no** (LO, 2026-11-03). Put the raise on the moment it
> happens: the button that walks her into the accident (he sees her when she opens the door), or the act
> itself (the sex, not his terms after it). Never put it on her answer. A no adds 0, and so does a final
> no that says "(ends his path)". The engine has no node-entry effects, so "the moment" is the exit that
> leads into the screen where it happens.

- **An accident raises HIS meter and sets a flag. It pays her nothing.** The raise sits on the way in, so he
  gains the same whatever she answers after. CoT's roommate's partner gains Lust +50 from the walk-in itself
  (`cot:L72330`, `:72476`); CoT's engine halves what she gains from an accident against the same choice
  (`HEAT_STUDY.md:334`); new-lust's walk-ins set nothing (`home_kinds.md:91`).
- **Her button to go further pays her**: a second look, staying a moment longer. +1, capped at the top of her
  stage (`the-arc.md` A4d).
- **Her no never raises her climb, and never raises his Want or any other meter of his** (LO, 2026-11-03; the
  same rule as `the-arc.md` A3). A no may cost Warmth (CoT: friendship −10 polite, −25 annoyed, `cot:L72330`,
  `:72343`), move a counter the world keeps (CoT's `discouraged++`, `cot.md:131`), or cost nothing. The no is
  always on the screen.
- **Where pressure is written, the no can fail.** A friend or a crowd pushing (*"Come on, do it,"*
  `cot:L67794`) turns the no into a contest: CoT's Willpower check can fail and she gives in
  (`cot:L67797-67799`). Build it as a no gated on her meter, shown locked above it (`show_when_locked`), and
  only where the push is on the page.

## H6 · The bridge from an accident to her choice

After the accident, give her a beat in her own voice that turns it into a wish, and set a flag on it. That flag,
with her stage 2, opens her first chosen act with that person.

> ⚠️ **EVIDENCE — NOT A TEMPLATE.** UM, after the stairs: *"He was smiling again! I did it! Maybe I should tease
> him to completely cheer him up?"* (`um:10274`), and the next line unlocks teasing (`um:10276`).

## H7 · His leak

Before he acts, his want shows **on his body, and she sees it**. At stage 1 a man she lives with leaks and
covers it: he looks, catches himself, looks away; the bulge he turns from her. She only notices.

**Only the men she lives with cover** (LO, 2026-11-03, PICKS A65 c). The cover belongs to the house (H2): a
housemate who is caught looking has to keep living with her, so he hides it. A man from outside the house (a
landlord, a neighbour, a stranger) may look openly from the first scene, as first_term's Vance does. A man she
lives with covers at stage 1 even when he is the one who walks in on her: first_term's Mark is a housemate, so his
walk-ins cover. It comes back in his mouth later:
*"You heard us. And I know you liked it."* (`cot:L72525`). This is `the-arc.md` A13's leak, said loud
(`SKILL_CHANGES_FROM_FIRST_TERM.md` B15, LO 2026-10-06).
