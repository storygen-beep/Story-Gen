## Steps in order
1. **List the live arcs first.** A sender is someone she already has a thread with: IHOH's 10 senders are all arc
   people; CoT picks by relationship type; SD calls only met contacts. No live arcs, nothing to say.
2. **Every message is caused** by a flag a scene set (IHOH 46/46; CW 77/79 read an arc stage).
3. **Every message is timed:** a delay after the cause (the next day by default) and an hour window.
4. **Keep it short:** 1–3 bubbles of 3–7 words. Long talk goes into a call or a meeting scene.
5. **Every message ends in a choice that books something** with a place and a time. Warm and cold answers move
   the relationship. **Ignoring costs more than saying no** (CoT −20/−20 vs −2/−3).
6. **The meeting happens in the world, and missing it costs** (CoT −25 ×3). Point to it everywhere the player
   looks: reminder, calendar, map marker, wake-up line (CoT); hint journal (IHOH).
7. **After the arc's sex step, the thread becomes a loop:** a repeatable invite with a cooldown.
8. **Give her the same doors to start herself.** Small-talk links that lead nowhere are filler.
9. **Each app is a door to sex, money or people, or it isn't there.** An alert can open the door when a stat is
   reached.
10. **Deliver by pull:** a sidebar line or a ❕ on the contact. Never cover the screen.

## Amounts (directions, never gates)
- One thread per live arc.
- 2–3 texts plus 1–2 calls or meetings before the loop (IHOH James: 3 texts, 2 calls).
- Delay: the next day by default; +2 to +3 days between the steps of a slow burn (CW Damien +2).
- One loop invite per sex-capable relationship, with a cooldown of 1–3 days.
- One door app.
- Random *timing* is fine; random *senders and content* are not. IHOH's 4 dice texts all sit on live threads;
  CoT's only random "hi" is the one it switched off.

## Touchpoints a thread must have
The scene that sets the cause · the text · the reply · the booked meeting (place and time) with a reminder · the
meeting scene · the no-show cost · the loop entry.

## Chains (pick at least one per release)
- text → date → relationship → sex → loop (CoT: sex → fuckbuddy → timed text → plan → reminders → sex; IHOH: coffee
  meet → text → call → date → text → call → FWB sex → booty-call loop).
- app match → booking → sex → a new contact (CoT: Findr tags → night match → sext → booty call today → sex).
- stat → alert → app → money → a bill (IHOH: porn taste → OnlyGirlz alert → cam shows → bank on the 1st → Dad's
  loan).
- call → meeting → his stats rise → new kinds of call (SD: hangouts → drug buyer → paid affair).
- reputation → call → shop tier (SD).

## First release
- Only the arcs in this release get threads; each gets 2–3 timed texts that book one meeting.
- One repeatable invite after the arc's sex step.
- One app that is a door, for money or sex. Nothing else on the phone: no dead apps, no "will be expanded" icons.
- This is IHOH's James shape, and SD's early "contacts first, calls later" (SD added calls in v0.9.0).

## Failure checklist
- [ ] Every text is caused by a flag a scene set (not random, not "hi").
- [ ] Every text has a delay and an hour window.
- [ ] 3–7 words a bubble, at most 3 bubbles.
- [ ] Every text ends in a booking or a choice that moves the relationship. Ignoring costs.
- [ ] The booked meeting is reminded somewhere the player looks.
- [ ] The thread ends in a loop, not a last text.
- [ ] Every app pays sex, money or people.
- [ ] No hidden phone gate: no buy, carry or PIN step (class b, 43 of 194 failures).
- [ ] Alerts are pulled, never a covering pop-up (class f, 8 failures).

## Our engine today (round 9a §8; `references/engine.md` §51)
- A conversation is caused by its `trigger.conditions` (version 1.0); `days_since_flag` is the delay and
  `time_of_day` the window, which on a conversation is checked once, at delivery (`v2.py:2608`, `:5045`).
- A loop invite is one conversation with `repeat_after_days` (`references/engine.md` §51); a chain of one-time conversations is for a sequence of different texts (`references/the-phone.md` P9).
- A reply's `flagEffects` and `scheduleEffects` book the meeting (`v2.py:2778`); a canvas holds the place and time.
- An unanswered text can cost (`ignore_after_days`), a dating match can set a flag (`on_match`), an app can
  wait on `conditions`, a phone action can take minutes (`time_cost`), and a call can ring, be answered,
  declined or missed (`[[phone.calls]]`).
- Delivery is pulled: the sidebar badge (`v2.py:3627`) and a three-second toast (`v2.py:2369`).
