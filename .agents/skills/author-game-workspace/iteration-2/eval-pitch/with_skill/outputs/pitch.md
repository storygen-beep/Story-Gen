# Next up for Vesper — the pitch

**Where we are.** 1a (The Archive) is authored and validated end-to-end — Calloway's belief-lever
seduction, the drain, the Colm sub-arc, the blackmail-Vane retrieval, the watcher-flush + Mercer-panic
close. `archive_1a_done` fires; she walks out holding her build file, still leashed, `core` still
**Failing**, Mercer bailing. Only chore left inside 1a is `beat_0065` — media + clean ship. That's
housekeeping, not content.

**What I'd build next: open 1b — The Deal.** 1a's whole close is a fuse pointed here: *"she'll help
Mercer run → 1b."* The controller is dangled, Vane's fled into the underworld, Bastien's named as the
buyer and never met, and her own file is in her hands but **unread**. Every one of those is a loaded gun
1a hung on the wall. 1b is where they go off. The single hottest thing on the board is the one we've been
saving for two arcs: **Bastien.** He owned her in Captivity. In 1b he's the man at the other end of the
deal — and this time she comes to *him*, on a job, off the leash he never held.

I'd build **one beat first** — the descent — not the whole arc. Here's the pitch for that beat, then two
forks I need your call on before I write a line.

## The beat: "The Undertow, from the wrong side of the bar"

**THE CHARGE.** She goes down into the Reach to run Mercer's flight and walks straight into the man who
kept her in a sealed room for three days. Last time Bastien had her with no exits and a faceless crew.
This time she's the one who needs something from *him* — the controller trade, the read on Vane — and she
has to sit across from him and *work* him, calm, while her body remembers exactly what he is. The want is
the reversal: the captive walks back in as the operator, and the whole beat is her holding that pose while
he tests whether it's real. (Areas named: **captor/captive power reversal**, **coerced-composure /
dubcon register** — the ceiling for Bastien's arc was declared in Captivity; I carry that floor forward,
I do not re-open it. If you want the reunion to go past the Captivity ceiling, that's a `kink-ceilings.md`
§2 decision to make out loud first — flagged, not assumed.)

**THE LADDER, and where it ends.** Rungs: she gets into the Undertow's back half (Colm/Sol vouch, carried
from 1a) → she's recognized — *he* clocks her before she clocks the room → the sit-down where she has to
transact with him without flinching → he makes her pay in the old currency to prove she's really here to
deal, not to run → she pays it, gets the thread to Vane, and leaves with the trade half-made. **It ends on
her walking out having let him put a hand back on what he used to own — and calling it the price of the
job.** That's the beat's floor. The build-file **read** (she is the guinea pig at the center of Vance's
humanoid program) is the *next* beat's Tier-3 payload, not this one — I want the reunion to land before the
origin does, so the horror hits a her we've already put back under his hand.

**THE PERSON.** Runs on **Bastien**. His one trait that makes this his and not a generic ex-captor: he is
**incurious about her defiance** — he never needed her to break, so her new composure doesn't threaten
him, it *amuses* him. That's the specific lever. A man who wanted to be resisted would escalate; Bastien
just waits, unbothered, for her to admit she came back to the one place she swore she'd never. The heat is
that he's *right* and she knows it. (Contrast held: Calloway starved to be believed; Bastien starves for
nothing — that's what made him terrifying and it's still the engine here.)

**THE DEPOSIT.** Feeds the live machine: it opens `npc_bastien` as an on-screen NPC for the first time
(hidden dialog-speaker → met), sets the 1b thread flags (the controller trade in motion — she takes hold
of the leash key without cutting it, `core` still Failing so **Kess** is still required), and hangs the
build-file read as the primed next hook. It advances her one standing want — **used → user** — without
resolving it: she operates on her old captor, but she leaves still leashed, still not free. Purely
in-fiction, no new sidebar meter (holds her §2 lean — dormant `corruption` stays dead, the inventory + the
leash are the ladder).

**THE COST.** This one is **not** safe, and that's right. The live fail-form is **danger** — the Chairman
(Aldous Vance) is inbound; being seen dealing in the Reach while Calloway's report climbs toward Vance is
exposure. And the personal cost is the one that matters: to buy the thread she has to hand Bastien a
liberty she spent an entire arc getting out from under. The bill is authored, not implied — she pays with
her own body and her own history, on the record.

level, not in adjectives. Dialogue-forward wherever he's present, per the mode axis.)

## Two forks — your call before I write it

**Fork A — does he recognize her, or does she get to choose the moment?**
- **A1 (recommend): he clocks her first.** She loses the reveal — he names her before she's set — and
  spends the beat on the back foot. Hotter: the reversal she planned collapses in the first ten seconds and
  she has to rebuild it while he watches. Fits his "incurious, unbothered" trait exactly.
- **A2: she comes in incognito** (the cover from 1a holds a while), and the recognition is a mid-beat trap
  door. More heist-tense, but it makes the beat about the disguise, not about the two of them — and the
  disguise isn't the fantasy here.

**Fork B — what's the currency of the sit-down?**
- **B1 (recommend): he makes her prove it in the old way.** The price of the thread is a liberty on his
  terms — coerced-composure, the Captivity dynamic re-lit with her now *choosing* to submit to it for the
  job. Stays at the declared Bastien ceiling; lands the "used → user, but not free" turn hard.
- **B2: pure leverage, no touch this beat** — she trades information/a Chairman warning for the Vane thread,
  and the physical reckoning waits. Cleaner, colder, keeps the body for a later beat. Weaker as an *opener*
  — it under-delivers the one thing the player has been promised since Captivity.
