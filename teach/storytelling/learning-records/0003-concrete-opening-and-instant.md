# Can now open on a concrete detail and land on one instant

On the third drill (2026-11-25) the learner, without being prompted that round, opened on a concrete habit (a college spreadsheet tracking every expense) and built to a single, sensory instant (a late-night paneer roll they still remember the taste of), with an "usually I'd just go to sleep" line that sets up the break from routine. Both Lesson 1 skills are in place.

**Evidence:** third drill answer; the opening and moment were fixed after feedback on drills 1 and 2.

**Implications:** Still states the moral up front ("spending on yourself is an investment"), and that moral does not quite match the instant (a treat, not an investment). Next: stakes (Lesson 2), then endings, where saying the lesson out loud versus letting it land gets taught directly.
