# Starting point: beginner, oral true stories

The learner has no formal storytelling craft and wants to tell true stories aloud (anecdotes, talks, pitches). Start from the single most load-bearing idea — a story is built around a change — before structure, stakes or delivery; do not assume any craft vocabulary.

**Implications:** First lessons draw on The Moth and Matthew Dicks (spoken, true, personal stories) rather than fiction or screenwriting frameworks.
