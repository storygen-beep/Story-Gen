## Knowledge

- [Book: _Storyworthy_ by Matthew Dicks](https://www.goodreads.com/work/quotes/59440320)
  Many-time Moth StorySLAM winner's manual for true, spoken stories. Source of "Homework for Life", the five-second moment, and "start with the opposite". Use for: finding stories, structuring a personal story around its turn. The core text for this mission.
- [Podcast/article: "How to tell better stories" — Matthew Dicks on Lenny's Newsletter](https://lennysnewsletter.com/p/how-to-tell-better-stories-matthew)
  Dicks explaining his method in his own words, free. Use for: a first, fast exposure to the Storyworthy ideas.
- [Book: _How to Tell a Story_ by The Moth (Burns, Jenness et al.)](https://www.npr.org/2022/04/26/1094947453/how-to-tell-a-good-story)
  The Moth's own guide; link is NPR Life Kit's interview with its directors. Use for: stakes, anecdote vs story, not memorizing, choosing a story, live delivery.
- [Article: "How to tell a story: The Moth" — Nieman Storyboard](https://niemanstoryboard.org/?p=893)
  Points to The Moth's tips page; quotes "A story without stakes is an essay." Use for: stakes.
- [Article: "Tell a story to build a connection" — Psychology Today (Moth tips)](https://www.psychologytoday.com/us/blog/between-cultures/202403/tell-a-story-to-build-a-connection)
  Ten Moth tips from Sarah Austin Jenness: start with the why, details only you can tell, avoid witness stories, stay in the story. Use for: delivery checklist.
- [Talk: "The clues to a great story" — Andrew Stanton, TED2012](https://www.ted.com/talks/andrew_stanton_the_clues_to_a_great_story)
  Pixar writer-director on making a promise to the audience and making them work for it. Use for: openings, tension, audience engagement.
- [Interview: Kenn Adams on the Story Spine — NPR](https://www.npr.org/transcripts/nx-s1-5750619)
  The 8-beat improv tool (Once upon a time / Every day / But one day / Because of that ×3 / Until finally / Ever since then). Use for: structure drills, quick plotting of a told story.

- [Book notes: _Storyworthy_ — Manas Saloi](https://www.manassaloi.com/booksummaries/2021/06/12/storyworthy-dicks.html)
  Secondary summary of Dicks's stakes tools (Elephant, Backpack, Breadcrumbs, Hourglass, Crystal Ball). Use for: quick check of tool definitions until the book itself is to hand.
- [Article: Moth live storytelling tips — journalism.co.uk](https://www.journalism.co.uk/feel-the-emotion-live-storytelling-tips-from-the-moth/) and [KNPR: What makes a Moth story a Moth story?](https://knpr.org/desert-companion/2018-11/onstage-story-time)
  Secondary coverage of The Moth's stakes guidance (what you stand to gain or lose; the lost-luggage example). Use for: stakes.

- [Book summary: The Moth's _How to Tell a Story_ — Readingraphics](https://readingraphics.com/book-summary-how-to-tell-a-story/)
  Secondary summary of The Moth's guide. Use for: first and last lines, memorize only those, endings rooted in change.

## Wisdom (Communities)

- [The Moth StorySLAMs](https://themoth.org)
  Open-mic story competitions in many cities; 5-minute true stories on a theme. Use for: the real-world test in the mission.
- Toastmasters (local clubs)
  Structured, low-stakes speaking practice with evaluations. Use for: repeated live reps with feedback.
