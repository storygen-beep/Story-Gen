# Mission: Storytelling

## Why
Tell true stories out loud — anecdotes at dinner, a story in a talk or a pitch — that hold a room instead of trailing off. Today stories come out by instinct, with no tools for choosing, shaping, or ending them.

## Success looks like
- Pick a moment from your own life and say in one sentence why it is a story, not an anecdote.
- Tell a 3–5 minute true story aloud with a clear beginning, the turn, and an ending that lands.
- Open a story fast (no throat-clearing) and keep stakes alive until the turn.
- Watch or hear someone else's story and name what made it work or fail.
- Tell one story in front of real people (friends, a meetup, or a StorySLAM open mic).

## Constraints
- Beginner: no prior craft study.
- Practice formats preferred: quick browser quizzes, short writing drills with feedback in chat, analyzing examples.
- Lessons must be short: one win per session.

## Out of scope
- Long-form fiction, novels, screenwriting (for now).
- Line-level prose style for the page.
- Presentation-slide design and voice/vocal coaching beyond what a story needs.
