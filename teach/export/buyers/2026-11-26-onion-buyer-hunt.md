# Buyer hunt 1: onion importers (2026-11-26)

Product: fresh onions (also garlic and potatoes) sourced around Indore, MP.
Role: sourcing agent (see learning-records/0001).
Method: public web search only. Every row is a **candidate, unverified** lead: a public page or news report says the company imports onions, usually from India. Nothing here confirms that they are buying now, how much, or from whom. Company websites could not be opened from this session (network policy blocks them), so the claims below come from search results, not from reading the pages.

Where India's onions go (APEDA, 2024-25, DGCIS data): Bangladesh, Malaysia, UAE, Sri Lanka, Nepal, Kuwait. Source: https://apeda.gov.in/FreshOnions

## Pitch angle for a sourcing agent

Several Dubai importers name Nashik (Maharashtra) as their source. Madhya Pradesh is the second-largest onion state and the largest garlic state, so the offer is a second origin: "graded onions and garlic from the Indore belt, checked before loading" (lesson 0003).

## Before contacting anyone

1. Open the company's own site and confirm the India-onion claim in their words.
2. Check company registration: UAE trade licence, SSM in Malaysia, Registrar of Companies in Sri Lanka.
3. Confirm Indian onion export policy is "free" today (DGFT) and the destination's import rules (Bangladesh permits).
4. Have an Indian exporter of record lined up before quoting, because you cannot ship yourself.
