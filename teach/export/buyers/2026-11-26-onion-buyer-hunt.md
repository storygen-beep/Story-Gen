# Buyer hunt 1: onion importers (2026-11-26)

Product: fresh onions (also garlic and potatoes) sourced around Indore, MP.
Role: sourcing agent (see learning-records/0001).
Method: public web search only. Every row is a **candidate, unverified** lead: a public page or news report says the company imports onions, usually from India. Nothing here confirms that they are buying now, how much, or from whom. On 2026-11-26 the UAE and Malaysia company sites were opened directly where they loaded; the tables say which claims come from the company's own page and which only from search results.

Where India's onions go (APEDA, 2024-25, DGCIS data): Bangladesh, Malaysia, UAE, Sri Lanka, Nepal, Kuwait. Source: https://apeda.gov.in/FreshOnions

## Pitch angle for a sourcing agent

GoodLuck and Euto both name Nashik (Maharashtra) as their source on their own sites, and GoodLuck publishes a spec: 55 mm and up, 18 kg mesh bags. Madhya Pradesh is the second-largest onion state and the largest garlic state, so the offer is a second origin: "graded onions and garlic from the Indore belt, checked before loading" (lesson 0003).

## Before contacting anyone

1. Open the company's own site and confirm the India-onion claim in their words.
2. Check company registration: UAE trade licence, SSM in Malaysia, Registrar of Companies in Sri Lanka.
3. Confirm Indian onion export policy is "free" today (DGFT) and the destination's import rules (Bangladesh permits).
4. Have an Indian exporter of record lined up before quoting, because you cannot ship yourself.
