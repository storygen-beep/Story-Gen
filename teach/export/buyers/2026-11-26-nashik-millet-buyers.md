# Buyer hunt 2: Nashik millet processors (2026-11-26)

Product: raw kodo and kutki (little millet) from Mandla/Dindori.
Why Nashik: a research brief says Mandla and Dindori millet is largely transported to Nashik, a major processing hub (https://www.researchgate.net/publication/338526798), and Mongabay (2024) reports aggregators truck unprocessed millet from the region to Nashik for processing, packaging and marketing (https://india.mongabay.com/2024/03/millet-tradition-loses-flavour-among-madhya-pradeshs-tribals/).

These are domestic buyers, not foreign ones. Selling to them needs GST registration, not IEC or APEDA.

Method: IndiaMART profile pages read directly on 2026-11-26. IndiaMART profiles are self-declared by the company. They show what the company **sells**; none of them publishes what it **buys**, so every buying requirement below is a question to ask them, not their stated policy.

The Nashik price is for cleaned and dehulled grain. Dehulling loses weight, and cleaning, packing and freight cost money, so the farm-to-Nashik gap is smaller than it looks. Ask a processor for their raw-grain buying price and recovery rate.

## Requirements: what is known, what to ask

Known (law, applies to millet sold as food): FSSAI's millet group standard, in force from 1 September 2023, covers kodo and little millet with limits on moisture, extraneous matter, other edible grains, defects, weevilled grains and immature or shrivelled grains. Reported figures: moisture not more than 13%, immature or shrivelled grains not more than 5%, other edible grains 2%, uric acid not more than 100 mg/kg (https://www.foodnavigator.com/Article/2023/04/05/india-sets-new-food-safety-standards-for-millets-in-anticipation-of-production-boom/). Confirm exact per-millet limits in the Gazette notification.

Ask each processor:
1. Do you buy raw (undehulled) kodo/kutki, and from MP?
2. Moisture and foreign-matter (stones, soil, straw) limits for raw grain?
3. Minimum quantity per truck, and bag type?
4. Price basis: delivered Nashik or picked up in Mandla? Who pays freight?
5. Payment terms: on delivery, or how many days?
6. Do you need organic certification, and do you pay more for it?
7. How do you pay agents who bring supply?
