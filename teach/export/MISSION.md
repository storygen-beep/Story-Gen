# Mission: Exporting vegetables from Indore

## Why
The learner lives in Indore (Madhya Pradesh) and wants to find out what they could actually do in the import/export of vegetables and related produce: which roles exist, what each one needs, and which one fits them. The outcome is a decision: pick one path and know the first concrete steps to start on it.

## Success looks like
- Can name the registrations an Indian vegetable exporter needs (IEC, APEDA RCMC, phytosanitary certificate) and say who issues each one.
- Can describe the main roles in the trade (grower-exporter, merchant exporter, sourcing agent, service provider) and the money, risk and skills each one needs.
- Can explain why Indore/MP is a sourcing base (onion, garlic, potato) and what that means for product choice.
- Has chosen one path and written a first-steps checklist for it. (Path chosen: sourcing agent, see learning-records/0001.)

## Constraints
- Lives in Mandla, MP (corrected in session 1; earlier lessons assumed Indore). Indian rules (DGFT, APEDA, Plant Protection, customs) apply. Family is sceptical that export is possible from Mandla.
- Interest so far: vegetables and related produce. Starting capital not yet stated.
- Prefers short, direct answers.

## Out of scope
- Importing into India (for now; may return once the export map is clear).
- Processed foods, spices and other non-fresh products, unless the learner asks.
