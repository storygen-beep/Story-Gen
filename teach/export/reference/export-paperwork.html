Before every deal

  1. Check DGFT notifications: is the product "free" to export today? Any minimum export price or duty?
  2. Check the buyer country's import rules for that vegetable (pests, extra declarations).
  3. Book phytosanitary inspection early; fumigation adds up to 3 days.
