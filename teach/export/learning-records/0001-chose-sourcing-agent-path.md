# Chose the sourcing agent path

After lesson 0001 mapped the four roles (grower-exporter, merchant exporter, sourcing agent, service provider), the learner chose sourcing agent. Future lessons should build agent skills (getting paid, quality inspection, finding buyers and exporters, agreements, GST) rather than exporter registrations, which belong to the exporter of record in this role.

**Evidence**: Stated choice in session 1 ("Lets say sourcing agent"). Quiz results from lesson 0001 were not reported, so lesson 0001 content is covered, not yet demonstrated.

**Implications**: Capital question is less urgent (agents hold no stock). Lesson 0002 covers commission and the two payment risks; 0003 planned on mandi quality checks.
