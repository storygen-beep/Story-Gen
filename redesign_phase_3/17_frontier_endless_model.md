# The frontier model — endless sandbox, not a finish line

What replaces "design the endings." We are **not** building limited games that stop at a win-screen — we're
building **endless sandboxes**, like every reference game (RTS, Lustbound …). They don't *end*; they have a
**frontier** — the edge of what's been authored — and the player lives in the sandbox at that edge until
more is written. So Step 2's last piece is not "endings"; it's **the open-frontier model.**

---

## The word "ending" hides three different things
Only the middle one is dropped.

1. **Local arc endings — KEEP.** A single NPC/thread *does* reach a terminal point (its last capstone):
   "you fully corrupted Rosa," "you completely broke Sal." That's a real, satisfying ending *for that
   thread* — and the game keeps going. These already exist (capstones, `07`/`57`).
2. **The game-ending (a hard finish line) — DROP.** No win-screen that closes the game. That's the
   limited-game model we're leaving behind.
3. **The frontier — DESIGN THIS.** The desire ladder (`09`) is **open-topped.** Its top rung isn't a wall —
   it's the furthest want authored *so far*. Reaching it = arriving at the current peak and *continuing to
   play*, not stopping. Later, the next rung is authored and bolted on.

---

## The frontier rung must do THREE jobs at once
This is the whole trick — what makes the edge feel like *arrival*, not *running out of content*:

- **(1) Land a real payoff.** The top rung is a genuine peak moment, fully earned, fully at the charge
  ceiling ("you became the madam"). Arriving is an achievement, not a fade-out.
- **(2) Drop into a steady-state you can live in.** The sandbox stays *alive*: the repeatable loops, the
  stable, the reactive world (`11`), the income (`13`) — all still playable. Reaching the top means "enjoy
  the kingdom," never "the screen goes blank."
- **(3) Seed the next hook (greyed / teased, not playable).** Leave ONE visible thread pointing outward —
  *"a rival madam across town has noticed you."* Not playable yet, but **shown.** It tells the player more
  is coming AND it is the **clip-point** the next rung bolts onto when authored. *Every frontier already
  points at its own next rung* — that's what makes the game extensible by construction.

---

## The trap: endless ≠ aimless (the RTS sin)
RTS players hit the edge of content and got *"I don't know what to do."* Endless must NOT mean directionless.
The legibility rule (`14` A) still holds — the tracker always names the current want — and **at the frontier
it must say so HONESTLY:** *"You've reached the current peak — run your empire, [the steady-state loop].
More to come."* That one honest line is the entire difference between a satisfying open sandbox and a
confusing empty one. The frontier is *narrated*, never a silent dead end.

---

## What this does to pacing: climb → PLATEAU → climb again
Escalation can't rise forever (you can't get infinitely more explicit or powerful). So the heat curve is not
a straight line — it's **steep climb up the authored ladder → a wide, sustainable PLATEAU at the top (the
sandbox you live in) → and when extended, the plateau lifts to a new height.** This matches RTS exactly: a
big climb, then a wide top where you play with everything you unlocked. (Folds into `14` P2 — escalation
rises *to the frontier*, then holds as a livable plateau, rather than promising endless escalation.)

---

## Why it fits the rest perfectly
The frontier model is the natural partner of incremental authoring (`16`): we build the ladder **up to a
frontier**, ship it as a *playable whole game*, then **grow** the frontier later by authoring the next rung
onto the seeded hook. The game is **built incrementally AND extended incrementally** — the same discipline,
continued. Adding content later needs no rewrite: you author the next want onto the greyed seed the last
frontier already left.

---

## Worked example (bar→empire)
- **Local ending:** Rosa's arc completes — her final capstone, a real satisfying close *for her thread*. The
  game continues.
- **Frontier (current top):** "become the madam" lands as a big payoff (job 1) → drops into the madam
  sandbox: run the bar, use the stable, all repeatable + reactive + income content live (job 2) → with a
  greyed seed: *"the city's vice boss has heard your name"* (job 3).
- **Later extension:** author "the city war" rung — it bolts straight onto the vice-boss seed. The plateau
  lifts; a new climb begins; a new frontier (with its own three jobs) sits on top.

---

## Self-check
- No hard game-ending / win-screen-that-closes (endless model).
- Local arc endings (terminal capstones) preserved — they end *threads*, not the game.
- The desire ladder is **open-topped** (`09`): the top rung is the *current frontier*, not a wall.
- The frontier rung does all **three jobs**: real payoff · livable steady-state · greyed next-hook seed.
- At the frontier the tracker is **honest and present** (`14` A), never blank — endless ≠ aimless.
- Pacing is **climb → plateau → climb**, not promised-infinite escalation (`14` P2).
- The frontier is **extensible** — the seeded hook is the clip-point for the next-authored rung (`16`).
