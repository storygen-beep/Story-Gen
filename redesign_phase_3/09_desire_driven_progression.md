# Desire-driven progression (the fix for Gap 1 — the deepest)

Closes the dry-run's deepest gap (`08` Gap 1): the cascade is a *gating* structure ("raise corruption →
unlock tier"), which is one inch from the #1 enemy — **grinding a bar.** What separates grind from good
is **why** the player acts. This doc makes "good" mandatory: **the player is always chasing a want;
the meter is just the path.** Same machinery as `04`, re-expressed so it's *felt* as pursuit, never bar-filling.

---

## The principle (one line)
**Every progression gain must be hung on a concrete WANT the player currently holds.** The player never
"raises corruption" — they *chase something* (a person, power, the next rung of the empire), and the
meter rising is the side-effect of the chase. Desire is onstage; the meter is backstage.

---

## The reframe: the cascade IS a DESIRE LADDER
Don't model the game as *a corruption meter with content bolted at thresholds.* Model it as **a chain of
escalating, named WANTS** — each one concrete, visible, and *wanted* — where pursuing a want raises the
meters that open the next want. The meters still exist underneath (they're the gate mechanism from `04`),
but the player **experiences a sequence of desires**, not a number climbing.

- The **cascade** (`04`: corrupt self → corrupt others → deepen) is the same thing, said as desire:
  *want to survive → want power over the man → want to take the bar → want the first girl → want the empire.*
- A meter **tier-crossing is experienced as CLEARING A WANT** ("I finally got bold enough to play him"),
  never as "corruption hit 30."

This is the structural heart of the fix: **author the desire ladder, not the meter.**

**The ladder is OPEN-TOPPED** (`17` — the frontier model). The top rung is not a terminal ending; it's the
**current frontier** — the furthest want authored *so far*. The game is an **endless sandbox** (like RTS),
not a limited game with a finish line. So the top rung is built to do three jobs (land a payoff · drop into a
livable steady-state · leave a greyed seed for the next rung — `17`), and later extensions add new rungs onto
that seed. Local *arc* endings (a single NPC's terminal capstone) still exist and are good — they end a
*thread*, never the game.

---

## Rules
- **R1 — Every gain serves a named want.** Any activity/scene that raises a meter must be, in the
  fiction, the player *pursuing a want she currently holds*. (The waitress flashes for tips *to afford
  the dress that'll get the owner's eye* — not "to raise corruption.")
- **R2 — The current want is always NAMED and VISIBLE.** The player can, at any moment, say what she's
  chasing. (This is the legibility surface — it doubles as the fix for Gap 3; see below.)
- **R3 — The meter is backstage.** Never surface "raise corruption to 15" as the goal. Surface the
  *want* ("get him to trust you with the books"); the meter quietly gates the scenes that fulfill it.
- **R4 — Ban the meter-exercise.** Any activity whose only justification is "this raises corruption" is
  the grind failure. The test for every activity: **"what does the player WANT that this serves?"** No
  answer → cut it or re-frame it under a want.
- **R5 — Want-completion = tier-crossing = a payoff.** Clearing a want is a *moment* (a payoff beat),
  and it reveals the next want. (This is the pacing rhythm — it doubles as the seed for Gap 4; see below.)
- **R6 — Per-NPC arcs are wants too** (desire flavor incl. CONQUEST — `15` Finding A). Each NPC arc is "you
  want them" — where *wanting* can be longing, transactional heat, OR **conquest** (you want to *break / own /
  take* this person, e.g. the man you're stealing the bar from). The double-lock (`04 §3`) is *felt* as "I want
  them → I must get bold enough (my corruption) AND win/break them (their lock)." Both gates are framed as the
  pursuit, never as two bars. A conquest-target is fine **only if the pursuit is hot and they have agency**
  (resist/scheme/crack) — a target you merely *use* with no charge is the cold-instrumental failure.
- **R7 — The feeders are desire-framed** (sharpens the content-design feeder economy): the self-corruption
  activities that raise the player meter are things she does *because she wants something* (money for a
  goal, a dare she wants to win, boldness to reach a person) — never "exercises to level up."

---

## How to author it
1. **Author the DESIRE LADDER first** — the chain of named wants from open to endgame. This *is* the
   player-facing spine/roadmap (a sharper version of the existing "loose roadmap"; surfaced via quest
   cards — Doc 49). Each rung: a concrete want + what fulfilling it unlocks (the next want).
2. **Map meter tiers to want-completions** — the corruption door tiers (`04`) line up with clearing
   wants, so a tier-cross is *narratively* "I took the bar," not "I hit 30."
3. **Hang every feeder/scene on the current want** — frame each in the fiction as pursuing it (R1/R7).
4. **Check legibility** — at every point the active want is shown (R2).

## Worked example — the desire ladder for the bar→empire fantasy (female PC)
> *Broke waitress → corrupt the man who owns the bar → take it → build your empire of corrupted women → madam.*

| # | The WANT (what she chases) | Pursuing it raises | Clearing it unlocks (next want) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Don't get thrown out** — make rent / keep the job | corruption (sluttier shifts, flashing for tips) + money | she's noticed — wants the owner's attention |
| 2 | **Get the owner to want you** — be the girl he can't ignore | corruption + her standing with him | he trusts you — wants leverage over him |
| 3 | **Take the bar from him** — out-seduce/out-scheme him | corruption (peak) + the leverage flags | you OWN it — wants to build it up |
| 4 | **Recruit your first girl** — corrupt another woman into your stable | the empire flags + standing | the stable opens — wants to grow it |
| 5 | **Become the madam the city answers to** — the empire | endgame meters | the **frontier** (current top, not a wall — `17`) |

Every rung is a concrete want she *feels*; the same flashing/seducing that raises corruption is, at each
rung, *pursuit of that want.* The player never thinks "fill the bar" — she thinks "I want to take this
place from him."

**A feeder, two ways (R1/R4/R7):**
- ✗ grind: "Flash at the bar (+1 corruption)."
- ✓ desire: "Work the floor topless tonight — the big tips get you closer to the dress you need to turn
  the owner's head." (Same +1 corruption; now it's pursuit of Want 2.)

---

## This fix also seeds Gaps 3 & 4 (bonus)
- **Gap 3 (legibility):** the desire ladder, with the active want always shown (R2), **IS** the legible
  objective thread. Fixing Gap 1 this way largely fixes Gap 3.
- **Gap 4 (pacing):** the chain of wants — chase → clear (payoff) → new want (R5) — **IS** the pacing
  rhythm (tension→release per want). The ladder seeds the pacing curve.
So doing Gap 1 as a *desire ladder* (not just a vague "make it motivated" note) is the high-leverage move.

---

## The check
- **Per activity:** name the want it serves (R4). None → grind, cut or re-frame.
- **Per moment:** the player can name what she's chasing right now (R2).
- **Per tier:** a meter tier-cross coincides with clearing a want (R5), framed narratively, not numerically (R3).
- **Per NPC:** the arc is felt as wanting *her*, the double-lock as the pursuit of her (R6).
- **Whole game:** there's a desire LADDER (chain of escalating wants), not a corruption bar with content at thresholds.
