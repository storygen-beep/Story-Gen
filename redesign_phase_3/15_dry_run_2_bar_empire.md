# Dry-run #2 — the bar→empire fantasy through the FIXED pipeline + re-critique

The validation-loop closure. Dry-run #1 (`08`, "The Estate") tested the front-end and found 6 gaps; docs
`09`–`14` fixed all 6. This re-run pushes the **canonical fantasy** (`05` Step-0a) through the *now-complete*
pipeline — Step 0a + cascade (`04`) + casting (`06`) + arcs (`07`) + **all 6 fixes** (desire ladder `09`,
reactive world `11`, economy `13`, legibility + pacing `14`) — and asks the only question that matters:
**does it now compose into a GOOD game, or does it still leak?**

The honest result: **the front-end and all 6 fixes hold — and they hold harder than #1 because this fantasy
clears the Step-0a bar that the estate failed.** But running the WHOLE arc (not just the opening) surfaces a
*new* cluster of findings the first dry-run never reached, because #1 only stress-tested Act 1. **Every gap we
ever found and fixed was a front-end gap. The back half of the game (Act 3 / the empire endgame) is the
under-designed half.** That's the real yield of this re-run.

## The run (compressed — only what each step produces)

### Step 0a — POV + fantasy bar (`05`)
- **POV:** female PC. ✓ (cascade is female-PC-native.)
- **Fantasy:** *"Broke waitress at a sleazy bar a man owns → seduce and corrupt **him** until you take the bar
  out from under him → then build it into your own empire, recruiting and corrupting other women into your
  stable until you're the madam the city answers to."*
- Three-part bar: **POV-fit** ✓ · **sharp charge** ✓ (power reversal + seduction-as-weapon) · **built-in two
  acts** ✓ (take the man → build the empire). Passes the "pick this over ten inherit-the-house games" test. ✓

### Step 1 — setup seed
- Player: broke waitress, one paycheck from the street.
- Cast (names+roles later): the owner (**Sal**), the shark (**Boyd**), the supplier (**Dee**), a regular
  (**Marcus**), the first recruit (**Rosa**), the gatekeeper regular.
- World: the bar (floor / back room / cellar / office) + the block (alley, the strip, a cheap room, a clothes
  shop, the docks).
- Systems: clothing (the exposure dial), debt/rent (the pressure), phone (optional).

### Step 2 — cascade (`04`) re-expressed as the DESIRE LADDER (`09`)
The spine is `09`'s worked ladder verbatim — 5 named wants, meter backstage:

| # | The WANT | Pursuing it raises | Clearing it → next want |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Don't get thrown out (rent/keep the job) | corruption + money | noticed → wants the owner's eye |
| 2 | Get the owner to want you | corruption + Sal's lock | he trusts you → wants leverage |
| 3 | Take the bar from him | corruption (peak) + leverage flags | you OWN it → wants to build |
| 4 | Recruit your first girl | empire flags + standing | the stable opens → wants to grow |
| 5 | Become the madam the city answers to | endgame | the ending |

- **Stats (each a job, `04` no-dead-stat):** `corruption` = the lewd door (cascade tiers). `money` = survival +
  the empire (the pressure, `13`). `energy` = pacing. **Per-NPC locks** = each target's own axis (`07`). *Note
  flagged below:* unlike the estate, this fantasy does **not** obviously need a `standing/charisma` stat —
  watch for a padded gatekeeper.

### Step 3 — casting (`06`) — roles + hooks
- **Boyd** — *pressure source*: "the smiling shark who'll seize the bar — or take other payment." Drives Act 1.
- **Dee** — *corrupting on-ramp* + peripheral: "the supplier who deals only when you're desperate, and likes
  you cornered." Fuels self-corruption.
- **Sal** — *core target*: "your late partner's loyal best friend, who's wanted you for years and hates himself
  for it" — **but also the man who owns the thing you're taking.** (slow-burn.) The emotional + structural heart.
- **Marcus** — *peripheral target*: "smooth regular, easy transactional heat." (dating/transactional.)
- **Rosa** — *recruit / second-act core target*: "the new waitress as broke and scared as you once were — your
  first girl, your mirror." (corruption arc — she's where Act 2 lives.)
- **The cold out-of-towner** — *gatekeeper* — **CUT on the self-check** (see Finding G): no secondary stat to
  earn him, no role the fantasy needs. The estate needed standing; this game doesn't.

### Step 4 — arcs (`07`)
- **Sal (core, slow-burn):** rich two-meter; end-state expands the hook *and* the structural turn (guilt →
  devotion → he signs it over). Lewd rungs **double-locked** (MC corruption tier + Sal's own lock, built by
  serving/flirting); non-lewd talk ungated (builds his lock in Act 1). First-night capstone (odometer+flags) →
  repeatable loop.
- **Rosa (Act-2 core):** rich two-meter, but her arc **opens in Act 2** — she's the proof the cascade inverts
  (now the PLAYER is the corrupting on-ramp for *her*). Double-locked on the player's *now-high* corruption +
  Rosa's own fear→hunger lock.
- **Marcus / Dee (peripheral):** light (one odometer + player-corruption floor), one capstone, no loop.
- **Boyd (antagonist):** flags + the deadline; confrontation capstone (pay / lose / arrangement).

### The 6 fixes, applied
- **Desire ladder (`09`):** the 5 wants above; every feeder hung on the current want (flash for tips → *for the
  dress that turns Sal's head*). ✓
- **Reactive world (`11`):** clothing exposure → lewd reactions, per-place ceiling × NPC disposition; the strip
  / alley / docks high-ceiling (can go forced), the bar floor mid, the shop low. ✓
- **Economy (`13`):** one wallet; income = corruption ladder (waitress wage → work the floor revealing → back
  room → escort → the stable); pressure via scaling sinks (climbing rent → the dress → buying/fixing the bar →
  recruiting). ✓
- **Legibility (`14` A):** the desire ladder *is* the tracker; the active want + next concrete action shown
  ("Make rent $X — work the floor for tips, evenings"); next rung telegraphed. ✓
- **Pacing (`14` B):** each want ends in a payoff; payoffs escalate (first big tip → first night with Sal →
  taking the bar → first recruit); big/small alternate; rates per-game. ✓

## Brutal re-critique — the NEW findings (what running the WHOLE arc surfaced)

Dry-run #1 only walked Act 1 (the estate front-end). Walking *all the way to madam* exposes that **our entire
design effort has been front-loaded.** The findings cluster in the back half:

### Finding A — the core target is INSTRUMENTAL (seduce-for-power), which fights quality #5 / R6
Sal is desired as a **conquest / a means** (take the bar), not (only) as a person you ache for. But `05` #5 says
"characters you *desire*" and `09` R6 says "each NPC arc is felt as *wanting HER*." A purely instrumental
seduction reads cold — the opposite of desire. **Yet this is the fantasy's whole charge** ("seduction as
weapon"). So either the fantasy violates #5, or **#5/R6 are too narrow.**
- **Resolution (a real doctrine refinement):** desire has more than one flavor. **R6 should accommodate
  CONQUEST-desire** — wanting to *break / own / bring to heel* a person is as charged as wanting to bed them,
  *as long as the pursuit is hot and the target has agency.* The fix is to author Sal so the player wants the
  *conquest* viscerally (his guilt cracking, the power flipping) — not to pretend it's a love story. The
  double-edged hook ("the friend who wants you" + "the man you're stealing from") already supplies both
  registers; the arc must *play the conquest as the desire*, not bury it under romance.
- **Action:** amend `09` R6 / `05` #5 to name conquest/ownership as a legitimate desire flavor (not just
  romantic/sexual longing). Small but load-bearing for any power-rise fantasy.

### Finding B — the fantasy spans M (Act 1) → F (Act 2); the orientation is an unsurfaced authoring call
Act 1 is seduce-the-man (M/F); Act 2 is corrupt-the-women (F/F). For a female PC that's a bi/pan power fantasy —
**coherent and hot IF intended**, but the player who came for one register may not want the other, and nothing
in the pipeline forces this to be a *deliberate* decision.
- **Resolution:** Step-0a (or casting) must **declare the desire span** — which genders/registers the core
  targets cover across the acts — so it's chosen, not stumbled into. Here it's intended (the fantasy literally
  says "him" then "women"), so it passes; but the *pipeline* should make any game state it.
- **Action:** add a one-line "desire span" output to Step 0a / casting (`05`/`06`). Minor.

### Finding C — the ENDGAME drains the lewd into MANAGEMENT (the biggest finding)
"Become the madam" + the economy's "the stable earns" + the sinks "recruiting / upgrading the place" describe an
**empire-management** loop. Pacing P2 says *escalate* — but in an empire game the natural escalation is toward
**spreadsheets** (more girls, more upgrades, more passive income), exactly when the player is most powerful. **The
hot content risks thinning at the top** — the classic 4X/management drift. This is the back-half version of LC's
flatness: not a grind-up, a *drain-down*.
- **Resolution (a real pacing/economy rule):** **Act 3's payoffs must stay CARNAL, not administrative.** The
  madam doesn't *manage* the stable — she *uses* it. Every empire beat cashes out as content:
  - each recruit is a **full corruption arc** (Rosa is the template — Act 2/3 cores, with their own
    double-locks and loops), not a +income unit;
  - "upgrading the bar" = unlocking **new kinds of scenes** (the back room becomes a play space), not stat bumps;
  - the madam endgame = **commanding scenes** (directing the girls, the player as the apex of the content), the
    hottest beats, not a dashboard.
- **Action:** add to `14` B a rule — **P7: the endgame escalates in CONTENT, never into management; every empire
  sink buys a scene/arc, never a number.** And to `13`: recruits are arcs (cost = a corruption arc to author),
  not income widgets. This is the most important output of this dry-run.

### Finding D — the reactive world makes her PREY while the cascade makes her PREDATOR (coherence)
`11`'s forced/non-con mode (high exposure × lawless place → the choice is removed, she's taken) makes the player a
**victim**. But the fantasy is a **power rise** — she ends as the one who *uses* others. A madam getting raped in
the alley in Act 3 is tonally incoherent with "the city answers to you."
- **Resolution:** **the reactive-world risk curve should INVERT with the power curve.** Early (the fall, Act 1),
  she's exposed/desperate/prey — forced content fits the descent and the charge. Late (Act 2-3, rising), the same
  exposure reads as her *weaponizing* her body (sought/choice modes dominate; she's courted/feared, not taken).
  So `11`'s "forced" ceiling shouldn't key on *place + exposure alone* — it should also **gate down as the
  player's power/corruption rises.** Prey → predator is the arc; the reactive world must track it.
- **Action:** add to `11` — the **forced mode is act-scoped**: available during the fall, recedes as she gains
  power (tie the forced threshold to the cascade act, not just place×exposure). Real addition; ties `11`↔`04`.

### Finding E — the late-game money MOTOR dies (the pressure that drove everything)
Act 1 runs on broke-pressure (`13` E6). But once "the stable earns," she's rich — **the engine that made earning
hot (and pulled her down the lewd path) is gone.** Scaling sinks (recruiting/upgrading) help, but a
self-sustaining empire removes the *squeeze*. Without a late pressure, Act 3 has no clock.
- **Resolution:** **swap the pressure, don't lose it.** Boyd's debt (survival pressure, Act 1) gives way to a
  bigger threat in Act 3 — a rival madam, a crooked cop, Boyd's boss, the city — so power still has teeth and
  money still has a wanted use. The *form* of pressure escalates (survive → defend the empire), the *presence*
  of pressure never dies.
- **Action:** note in `13` — **the pressure source must escalate across acts** (survival → empire-threat); a
  late antagonist replaces the debt clock. Ties to casting (a late-act pressure role). Medium.

### Finding F — the double-lock degenerates in Act 3 (expected, worth stating)
By Act 3 the MC-corruption "door" is maxed/wide open, so the double-lock collapses to *just the NPC's own lock*
for every late target. That's fine — but it means **late targets (Rosa et al.) can't lean on a rising MC door
for their pacing**; each needs a *full, self-contained* lock + rung ladder to stay legible and paced. (Front-act
targets borrowed pacing from the climbing MC door; late targets can't.)
- **Action:** authoring note in `07` — late-act NPC arcs carry their *own* full pacing; don't rely on the (now
  static) MC door. Minor.

### Finding G — confirmed: no padded gatekeeper (the stat-discipline worked)
The estate had `standing`; this fantasy doesn't need it, and the casting self-check (`06`) **cut** the gatekeeper
regular rather than inventing a `charisma` stat to justify him. This is the no-dead-stat rule (`04`) working as
intended — a *positive* result. The stat set is `corruption` + `money` + `energy` + per-NPC locks, each with a
job. ✓

## What this re-run proves
1. **The front-end + all 6 fixes hold** — and hold *harder* than dry-run #1, because the canonical fantasy clears
   the Step-0a bar the estate failed. The desire ladder, reactive world, economy, legibility, and pacing all
   compose. This is a genuinely good *front half*.
2. **Our design has been front-loaded.** Every gap we ever found (dry-run #1) and fixed (`09`–`14`) was an
   **Act-1 / front-end** gap. Walking to the endgame exposes that **the back half (Act 3 / the empire) is
   under-designed** — and its failure mode is the *mirror* of LC's: not grind-up-to-nothing, but
   **drain-down-into-management** (Finding C), plus three supporting issues (D power/prey inversion, E pressure
   motor, F late-lock).
3. **The fixes needed are refinements, not new gaps** — mostly *one rule each* on docs we already wrote.
   **All six APPLIED (2026-06-09):**
   - `14` B **+P7** (endgame escalates in content, never management) + anti-pattern "management drift" + Check — *the big one.* ✓
   - `11` forced-mode is **act-scoped** (prey early → predator late, gated on power tier, not place×exposure alone). ✓
   - `13` **+E8** (pressure escalates across acts: survival debt → empire-threat) **+E9** (recruits are arcs, not income widgets). ✓
   - `09` **R6** + `05` **#5** accommodate **conquest-desire** (wanting to break/own is a legit flavor; cold-instrumental is the failure). ✓
   - `05` **0a-(3)** + `06` (hook flavor + self-check) declare the **desire span**. ✓
   - `07` **§3b** + self-check: late-act arcs carry **own full pacing** (double-lock degenerates when the MC door maxes). ✓

## Verdict
**The pipeline now produces a good *front half* with confidence, and a good *back half* once the six refinements
above land.** No fix is structural — they're targeted additions to docs that already exist, all pointing at the
same blind spot: **we designed the fall and never designed the reign.** That's the dry-run doing its job again —
it told us exactly where the remaining work is.
