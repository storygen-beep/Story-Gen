## §2 Frank's voice spec

### Background (consolidated from doc 16 §2)

Ex-construction worker, 30+ years with his hands. Retired into landlording. Inherited or self-earned property — refuses to elaborate. Wife (Diana) present in slice but emotionally distant from Frank. Lives set in his ways, TV at high volume. Treats Maya initially as unwanted tenant complication; suspicious of her motives. Slow to soften. Watches.

### Speech patterns (load-bearing — every Frank line conforms)

| Pattern | Rule | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Sentence length | 4-8 words common; verb-chopped; rarely grammatically complete | "Coffee's ready." "Door's stuck." |
| Names things, not feelings | States facts where others express opinions | "Rent's due Sunday." not "I expect you to pay on time." |
| Asks questions that aren't questions | Implies an offer or instruction | "You eat?" (= you should eat). "You cold?" (= here, take this) |
| Rarely names Maya | "You" / "girl" (with edge) / addresses nothing | Never "Maya, I want you to know..." |
| No exclamations | Period at end, never "!" | "Get in here." not "Get in here!" |
| No apologizing in words | Adjusts behavior wordlessly | After a fight: hands her coffee the next morning, doesn't say sorry |
| No backstory unprompted | Doesn't volunteer history | Mentions Diana once: "Diana's at church." Done. |

### Voice samples per stage

| Stage | Sample line | Tone |
|---|---|---|
| Stage 0 (Suspicious landlord) | "You eat?" / "Bookkeeping's tomorrow. Office. Seven." | Terse, transactional |
| Stage 1 (Grudging warmth) | "Coffee's still warm." / "You did good with the books last week." | Acknowledging without warmth-words |
| Stage 2 (Restrict, post-catch) | "I told you. Door open. Always." / "Sit. Where I can see you." | Imperative, structural — not anger, control |
| Stage 3 (Tease, pre-cracked) | "Lessons run late tonight. Hope you weren't planning anything." | Charged double-meaning |

- **Frank calls Maya "good girl" / "baby girl"** starting from **Stage 4 (Cracked, sex-active — frank_cracked is_true)** — kicks in as soon as Frank is actively fucking her, not gated to post-first-night. (Disambiguation 2026-05-16 — original text "Stage 4 (Cracked, post-first-night)" read contradictorily; resolved as Stage 4 = sex-active.)
- At **Stage 5 (post-first-night + sleepover, corr 50+)** the daddy register is the DEFAULT in all sex scenes — not occasional, baseline. Routine call/response between them.
- Pre-Stage 3: NO daddy framing — Frank is still landlord, voice is terse-transactional, no sexual register
- Stage 5 examples: "Come to daddy." / "Daddy's going to take care of you." / "Tell daddy what you want."

### Anti-patterns — BANNED in Frank dialogue

❌ "Frank's eyes lit up with surprise" (cinematic narration)
❌ "He couldn't help but feel..." (generic interiority)
❌ "Maya, I want you to know..." (over-named, direct)
❌ Frank explaining backstory unprompted
❌ Academic vocabulary ("nevertheless," "consequently")
❌ Frank apologizing in words
❌ Frank using "honey" / "sweetheart" — he's not that man
❌ Soft daddy framing once Stage 4+ ("just relax" instead of "good girl")
❌ Long Frank monologues (>3 sentences in a row without Maya cut-in or action beat)

## §7 Anti-patterns (what NOT to write for Frank)

Hard bans for ALL Frank canvas authoring.

❌ **Relational/non-physical Lane 2 ambients.** No "morning chat over coffee" / "shared silence reading paper" / "Maya hands him water." Every Lane 2 beat opens with physical contact or explicit visual. (The C6 bug.)

❌ **Literary atmospheric prose.** No "kitchen smells of coffee" / "the kettle clicks" / "window light." Image carries the scene. (Doc 30 §7.1 rule 3.)

❌ **Third-person voice.** No "she leans" / "he watches her cross." Second-person throughout. (Doc 30 §7.1 rule 1.)

❌ **Contraception language anywhere.** No condom / pull-out / pill / safe-sex. Bareback throughout. (Doc 30 §7.3.1.)

❌ **Softening of daddy framing once Stage 4+.** Once Maya hits Stage 4, daddy register is in. Frank uses "good girl" / "baby girl" — not "honey" or "Maya."

❌ **"Maya hesitates" / "Frank waits respectfully" beats.** Frank takes. Maya wants it. No mutual-consent-negotiation prose.

❌ **"They have a moment" beats.** Every beat must satisfy the 6-field scene atom (doc 30 §7.2). If a beat doesn't have a fantasy beat + stat advance, it's not a scene.

❌ **Multi-paragraph stage direction per beat.** 2-sentence cap. (Doc 30 §7.1 rule 2.)

❌ **Inferential character work.** No "the cup he keeps for her" / "the chair he added when she moved in." Surface-level only. (Doc 30 §7.1 rule 5.)

❌ **Frank apologizing in words.** Frank doesn't say sorry. He adjusts behavior wordlessly. (Doc 16 §2 anti-pattern.)

❌ **Frank explaining backstory unprompted.** Maya's backstory + Frank's history stay implicit. No "let me tell you about my late wife" monologues.

❌ **Frank using "honey" / "sweetheart" / "Maya" routinely.** "You" / "girl" / unnamed. "Daddy" register kicks in once Stage 3+.

❌ **Long Frank monologues.** Max 3 Frank sentences in a row without Maya cut-in or action beat.
