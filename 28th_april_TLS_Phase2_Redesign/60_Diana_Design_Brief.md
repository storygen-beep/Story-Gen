## §5 — Awareness mechanics (committed, NOT blocked)

`npc_diana.awareness` — 0–100 silent accumulator. NO arc_stages per Doc 30 §4.2.

**Awareness bands** (Doc 30 §6):
- 0–24: cold/distant (default neighbor mode)
- 25–49: suspicious / watchful
- 50–74: knowing / cold
- 75–100: shut-out (functionally terminal pre-confrontation)

**Awareness fed by:**
- Frank Lane 2/3 cascades: `+1 diana_awareness` per visit to charged Frank surfaces (verified line 3350 + others). Diana accumulates awareness as Maya escalates with Frank.
- Frank capstones: `scene_franks_bedroom_evening` exit grants `+1 diana_awareness`; sleepover grants `+3`; declaration / catch grant smaller amounts.
- Diana kitchen ambient choices: morning/evening scenes have player choices that grant `+1 awareness` (cold/distant Maya response) or `-1 awareness` (warm/conciliatory Maya response, +1 trust).

**Awareness consumed by:**
- `scene_diana_confrontation` gate: fires once at `awareness >= 8` + `frank_bedroom_first_done is_true`. The 8 threshold is intentionally low — Diana sees the catch happen + a few escalations.
- Future post-confrontation bands gate cuckold content (Phase 2+ scope).

**Trust trait** (`npc_diana.trust`): separate accumulator from awareness. Trust climbs via warm Maya choices in kitchen ambients (line 3095: "Sit with her a minute" = +1 trust, -1 awareness; line 3107: "Coffee and out" = +1 awareness). Trust + Awareness are INVERSELY correlated by Maya's behavioral choices.

**Verified consistent.** No changes to awareness mechanics in this brief — the system is mechanically clean.
